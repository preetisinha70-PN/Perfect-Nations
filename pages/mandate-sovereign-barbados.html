Open the indicative term sheet
Borrower
The Government of Barbados
Adviser
Perfect Nations, sovereign adviser to the Government
Instrument
A debt conversion: guaranteed debt replaces costly debt, following the 2024 conversion, structured as a sustainability-linked loan (IDB).
Credit enhancement
A development bank guarantee, sought in phase two.
Size, tenor, price
Set from the debt chosen and the food gap of up to $192 million a year. No figure is assumed here.
Use of savings
The Blue Food Fund, established in law, for local farms, fisheries and sea farming only.
Linked terms
Terms can be linked to the food indicator, as the 2024 conversion was linked to its own targets.
Resilience
Climate resilient debt clauses, as in Barbados’s 2035 global bond (Parliament of Barbados).
Indicators
Barbadians moderately or severely food insecure, 31% in 2022 (FAO), and food grown and caught at home.
Verification
AI monitoring with an independent verifier; every disbursement and every quarterly result published.

Illustrative: no terms have been agreed with any party. Sizes and prices are set in the mandate, never assumed.
