01Govern · The cup

Good governance comes first. Before any finance.

A nation can secure basic standards of living for all only through institutions that serve its people. The cup in the logo stands for this: Perfect Nations sets out to recognize the nations that govern best, judged on the six dimensions of governance the World Bank tracks.

  1. 01Voice and Accountability
  2. 02Political Stability
  3. 03Government Effectiveness
  4. 04Regulatory Quality
  5. 05Rule of Law
  6. 06Control of Corruption

The six dimensions: World Bank, Worldwide Governance Indicators, 2025 revision.

The Perfect Nations Cup. A recognition for the best governance of basic standards of living. Criteria in preparation.
