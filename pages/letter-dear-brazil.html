Dear Brazil,

Across the blue sky on your flag runs a promise in four words, Ordem e Progresso: order and progress. You hold more than half your land in forest, and you have taught the world to pay for keeping it standing.

Think of what you have already built. Your forest covers about 4.9 million square kilometres, and every one of you shares 26,918 cubic metres of renewable fresh water. Rivers, wind and sun already make more than three-quarters of your electricity. At COP30 in Belém you launched the Tropical Forest Forever Facility, a fund that pays nations to keep their forests standing, and by September 2026 it held $7.3 billion in commitments. In November 2025 investors placed orders of about $6.7 billion for your $1.5 billion sustainable bond.

At home the record is just as real. Fewer than one in ten of you is food insecure, and you have left the United Nations Hunger Map. Ninety-seven in every hundred of you cook with clean fuel, and essential health care scores 84 out of 100 on the World Health Organization index. Bolsa Família protects the incomes of families with young children in every state.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. At the last count, in 2020, only about half of you had a safely managed toilet at home. Nearly half of your children reached the end of primary school unable to read a simple text, and one in five of you lives below $8.30 a day. Shelter sets your Coverage today, at 49 out of 100, with reading and income behind it. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine the nation that priced its forest for the world turning the same genius toward every home. Picture Perfect Brazil: a morning when every home, in the favela and on the river, has clean water and a safe toilet; when every child in Belém, Recife and São Paulo reads with ease by the age of ten; when the forest that cools the planet also pays the families who keep it; when every young person finds work that pays. And no Brazilian falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you already own: your forest, your water, your clean grid, your land, the fund you designed and the trust the markets have shown you. They are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Brazilian to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“You built a fund that pays the world to keep its forests standing. Now build the one that keeps every Brazilian safe at home.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Brazil Design Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Brazil will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
