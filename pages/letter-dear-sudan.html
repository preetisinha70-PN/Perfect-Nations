Dear Sudan,

You are living through the largest hunger crisis on earth, and nearly nine million of you have been driven from your homes. Relief comes first, and peace above all. When it comes, your Nile farmland, your gold and your people will need capital that arrives as fast as the need. We will be ready to design it with you.

Even in war, you have kept working. The Kenana sugar factory never stopped, families still receive salaries and help through a mobile banking app, and engineers have brought piped water back to Port Sudan. Partners stand with you: in April 2026 the African Development Bank approved US$87 million to help your farmers grow more food.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Shelter sets your Coverage today: 73.7% of city dwellers live in slums (UN-Habitat, 2022). Energy and education come next: each of you uses about 309 kWh of electricity a year, against the 1,000 a decent life needs, and 47% cook with clean fuels (Ember, 2024); and 47.5% of young people complete lower secondary school (World Bank, 2019). Each of them is within your reach.

When peace comes, imagine Perfect Sudan: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your farmland, your clean grid, your ground, your land and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“Peace first. Then capital as fast as the need, so that no Sudanese family goes hungry again.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Sudan ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Sudan will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
