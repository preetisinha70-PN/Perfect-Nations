Dear Fiji,

The light blue of the Pacific, with your shield of sugar cane, coconut, bananas and the dove of peace. Almost every one of your young people completes lower-secondary school, and you sold the Pacific’s first sovereign blue bond.

Think of what you have already built. In November 2023 you offered the first sovereign blue bond in the Pacific, in three- and fifteen-year tranches, to fund coastal protection, aquaculture and clean towns. In January 2025 the World Bank approved $199.6 million to make four major bridges on Viti Levu withstand storms.

At home the record is just as real. Ninety-seven in every hundred of your young people complete lower-secondary school. Seventy-one in every hundred of you can always count on enough good food. Essential health care scores 69 out of 100 on the World Health Organization index.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Sixty-one in every hundred of you lived below $8.30 a day at the last count, in 2019. Only forty-two in every hundred of you drink safely managed water at home. Only forty-eight in every hundred of you have a safely managed toilet at home. Income sets your Coverage today, at 38.7 out of 100, with water and shelter close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Fiji: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Fijian falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you already own: your ocean, your reefs, your forests, your tourism and a people who already finish school. They are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Fijian to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“You sold the Pacific its first blue bond. Now let the ocean’s wealth carry every Fijian family to work that pays.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Fiji ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Fiji will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
