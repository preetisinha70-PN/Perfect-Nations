Dear Liberia,

Two thirds of your land is forest, and you renew more fresh water per person than almost any nation in Africa. One in three of you has electricity. Let the forest and the water carry the capital that brings power, schools and clinics to every county.

Look at what you are already building. In April 2026 the IMF approved about US$266 million to help you build buffers against climate shocks, and the World Bank and its partners are bringing solar power, broadband and roads to the south-east. In November 2026 a new project began to support communities that keep your forests standing, with a goal of 50,000 jobs.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Energy sets your Coverage today: 1% cook with clean fuels and technology, and 34.9% have electricity (World Bank, 2024). Food and shelter come next: 79% are moderately or severely food insecure (FAO, 2024); and 60.5% of city dwellers live in slums (UN-Habitat, 2022). Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine Perfect Liberia: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your forest, your fresh water, your clean grid, your ground and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“The rainforest that shelters you can also pay for the light in every home.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Liberia ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Liberia will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
