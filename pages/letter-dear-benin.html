Dear Benin,

You brought your deficit within the regional ceiling a year before it was due, and your partners have answered with dams, nutrition and coastal protection. Four in ten of you are under fifteen. The task is to turn a reputation for discipline into capital priced for it.

Look at what you are already building. In January 2026 you sold your first international sukuk, US$500 million over seven years, and in September a guarantee from the African Development Fund helped you raise €500 million from international banks for twelve years, for schools, clinics, water and power. In Cotonou and its neighbours, US$400 million from the World Bank is building safer and faster roads along the corridors where most of your jobs are.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Energy sets your Coverage today: 8% cook with clean fuels and technology, and 59% have electricity (World Bank, 2024). Shelter and water come next: 16.4% have safely managed sanitation, and 64% of city dwellers live in slums (WHO/UNICEF, 2024); and 17.7% have safely managed drinking water (WHO/UNICEF, 2024). Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine Perfect Benin: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your farmland, your forest, your coast, your fresh water and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“You kept your word on the budget. Now let the markets price it, and let the price fund your children.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Benin ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Benin will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
