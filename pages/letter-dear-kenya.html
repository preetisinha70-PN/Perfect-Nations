Dear Kenya,

The shield on your flag stands for the defence of freedom, and the black, red, green and white around it stand for your people, your struggle, your land and your peace. Since 1963 you have shown, again and again, that a young nation can invent what the world later copies.

Think of what you have already built. In 2007 you put a bank in every pocket: M-PESA, money sent by text message, which a study in the journal Science found had lifted 194,000 households out of poverty. Beneath the Rift you drilled for the heat of the earth itself, and today 943.7 megawatts of geothermal power run day and night, whatever the weather; four-fifths of your electricity now comes from sun, wind, water and steam. At Lake Turkana you closed the largest private investment in your history and raised one of Africa’s largest wind farms. In 2019 you issued the first green bond in East Africa, and repaid it in full. In 2024 you repaid your first Eurobond in full, and in February 2026 the market lent you $2.25 billion at lower rates than the year before.

At home the record is just as real. A Kenyan company has financed solar power and phones for 4.8 million customers who pay in small daily sums. In Nairobi’s informal settlements, prepaid water taps sell twenty litres for two shillings. Your nation is young and growing, 57.5 million strong. You have shown the world how to bank people the banks had left out, and the world now borrows your design.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Four in every five of your children reach the end of primary school unable to read a simple text. Each of you uses about 252 kilowatt hours of electricity a year, a quarter of what a decent life needs, and fewer than one in three cooks with clean fuel. About seven in ten of you cannot always count on enough good food. Reading sets your Coverage today, at 21 out of 100, with power and food close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine turning that same genius for invention toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Kenya: a morning when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse waiting and the lights on; when every tap runs clean, in the city and in the north; when every farmer irrigates with the sun and sells at a fair price; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten. The heat beneath the Rift powers the pump, the cold store and the workshop, not only the city, and your sea feeds and employs the people of the coast. And no Kenyan falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you have always had: the nerve to build what does not yet exist. The heat under the Rift, your clean grid, your land, your sea, your young people and the trust the markets are giving back to you are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Kenyan to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“The heat under the Rift is the rarest asset you own. The task now is to carry it to the clinic, the tap and the field.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Kenya Design Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Kenya will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
