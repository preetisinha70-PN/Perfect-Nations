The seven basic living standards

Seven conditions of a dignified life.

Each is held to the hardest test published worldwide. Choose a standard.

Seven rules keep it strict

No averages. No estimates. No one left out.

  1. Strict firstThe strict measure wherever it exists; any fallback is labelled.
  2. Five yearsOlder data shows its year, marked “dated”.
  3. No data, no number“Not measured”, never an estimate.
  4. The legal barWhere a law bars citizens from a standard, no score.
  5. Every citizenWhere data splits by sex or place, the lower group counts.
  6. Strict figures onlyNo gentler figure beside it.
  7. The weakest linkA nation is held at its lowest standard: no strength makes up for a failure.
