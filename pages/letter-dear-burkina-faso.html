Dear Burkina Faso,

Your gold lifted growth in 2025 and turned your current account to surplus. Most of you live from the land, and four in ten are under fifteen. The task is to bank the gold into farms, schools and power that last beyond the price of gold.

Look at what you are already building. In 2026 your own people, at home and abroad, lent you XOF 151.5 billion through the Diaspora Bonds, well above the XOF 125 billion you sought. In March 2026 alone the West African Development Bank approved XOF 131.5 billion for you: inputs for the cotton farmers, a larger solar plant with batteries at Koudougou, and the fuel that keeps the country moving.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Shelter sets your Coverage today: 87.9% of city dwellers live in slums (UN-Habitat, 2022). Energy and education come next: each of you uses about 135 kWh of electricity a year, against the 1,000 a decent life needs, and 18% cook with clean fuels (Ember, 2024); and 26.1% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school (World Bank, 2019). Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine Perfect Burkina Faso: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your gold, your farmland, your ground, your forest and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“Gold is a season. Farms, schools and power are a century. Let the season pay for the century.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Burkina Faso ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Burkina Faso will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
