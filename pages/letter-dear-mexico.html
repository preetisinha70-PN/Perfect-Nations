Dear Mexico,

An eagle on a cactus with a serpent in its beak: the sign your ancestors waited for before they founded Tenochtitlan. Essential health care scores 79 out of 100 on the World Health Organization index, and World Bank Group operations have helped mobilise nearly $12 billion of private capital in your country.

Think of what you have already built. World Bank Group operations have helped mobilise nearly $12 billion of private capital in your country. In December 2025 the World Bank approved a $500 million guarantee to draw private investment into infrastructure, and in October 2026 $500 million to strengthen your resilience to disasters.

At home the record is just as real. Eighty-eight in every hundred of you cook with clean fuel. Eighty-four in every hundred of you can always count on enough good food. Essential health care scores 79 out of 100 on the World Health Organization index.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Only forty-three in every hundred of you drink safely managed water at home. At the last count, in 2019, forty-eight in every hundred of your children reached the end of primary school unable to read a simple text. Only sixty-three in every hundred of you have a safely managed toilet at home. Water sets your Coverage today, at 43 out of 100, with reading and shelter close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Mexico: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Mexican falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you already own: your sun, your coasts, your factories, your border with the largest market on earth and a nation of extraordinary talent. They are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Mexican to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“You know how to bring private capital to the table. Now bring safe water to every Mexican tap.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Mexico ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Mexico will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
