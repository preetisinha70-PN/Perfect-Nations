Dear Ukraine,

Blue sky above yellow wheat: the plainest flag in Europe and one of the bravest. Even at war, nine in ten of your young people complete school and almost all of you have a safe toilet and clean fuel.

Think of what you have already built. In February 2026 the IMF approved an $8.1 billion, four-year programme, and had disbursed about $2.2 billion by July. In June 2026 the World Bank approved $3.39 billion for private-sector growth and jobs, and in May $880 million to modernise social protection.

At home the record is just as real. Ninety-seven in every hundred of you cook with clean fuel. Ninety-two in every hundred of you have a safely managed toilet at home. Ninety in every hundred of your young people complete lower-secondary school.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Thirty-two in every hundred of you cannot always count on enough good food. Twenty-three in every hundred of you lived below your national poverty line at the last count, in 2020. Essential health care scores 80 out of 100 on the World Health Organization index. Food sets your Coverage today, at 68 out of 100, with income and health care close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Ukraine: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Ukrainian falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you already own: your black earth, your grain, your engineers, your ports and a resolve the whole world has witnessed. They are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Ukrainian to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“You have kept a nation standing under fire. Now let every Ukrainian family count on a full table.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Ukraine ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Ukraine will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
