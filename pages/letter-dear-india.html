Dear India,

The saffron of your flag stands for courage, the white for peace and truth, the green for faith and the fertility of your land, and at its heart turns the wheel of law. Since 1947 you have held together the largest democracy on Earth.

Think of what you have already built. In a single decade you brought electricity to almost every one of your 1.46 billion people. By September 2026 you had installed 171 gigawatts of solar, the largest part of 299 gigawatts of renewable power. In 2023 you issued your first sovereign green bonds, and in October 2026 investors lent you money in green for thirty years, at 7.65 percent. Each of you now uses about 1,400 kilowatt hours of electricity a year, more than a decent life needs.

At home the record is just as real. Every school day, one kitchen network feeds 2.35 million children in more than 23,000 schools. An eye hospital that began in Madurai performed 78,581 surgeries in a single month. A housing trust built by working women has brought water, toilets, power and secure homes to 1.8 million people in 36 cities. A solar company that began in 1995 has powered more than 110,000 homes and businesses.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. At the last national count, in 2017, more than half of your children reached the end of primary school unable to read a simple text. Nearly two in five of you lack a safely managed toilet, one in four does not yet drink safely managed water at home, and one in five still cooks without clean fuel. Reading sets your Coverage today, at 44 out of 100, with shelter and health behind it. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine the nation that wired 1.46 billion people turning the same resolve to the classroom and the clinic. Picture Perfect India: a morning when every child, in every village and every city, reads with ease by the age of ten; when every home has a safe toilet and clean water from its own tap; when every mother lives a short walk from care that does not cost a year of savings; when the sun on your rooftops powers the pump, the cold store and the workshop. And no Indian falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you have always had: the scale to do what no one else can, and the will to do it. Your sun, your grid, your land, your green bonds and your 1.46 billion people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Indian to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“You wired a nation of 1.46 billion. Reading is the next network to build.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect India Design Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect India will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
