02 · How a state blueprint works

Assets, Capital, Solutions.

The method of every Perfect Nations blueprint, applied to a state.

  1. 01 Assets

    What the state owns, counted and valued.

    Land, water, energy, universities, state funds and, above all, its people.

  2. 02 Capital

    The instruments that finance it.

    Municipal and green bonds, outcome contracts, guarantees and the state’s own funds.

  3. 03 Solutions

    Seven basic standards of living for every resident.

    Food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood.
