Dear Gabon,

You are almost entirely forest, and you hold more fresh water per person than almost any nation in Africa. Yet you borrow at more than nine percent. The forest that cools the planet should lower the price of your capital, not sit outside your balance sheet.

Look at what you are already building. In 2023 you refinanced US$500 million of your bonds to protect your ocean, the first such debt conversion for marine conservation in mainland Africa. In 2026 the markets lent you US$920 million and then US$580 million, and the World Bank is bringing piped water, electricity and safe sanitation to more homes with US$150 million.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Education sets your Coverage today: 20.2% of young people complete lower secondary school (World Bank, 1995). Health and shelter come next: the WHO service coverage index stands at 48 (WHO, 2023); and 38.8% of city dwellers live in slums (UN-Habitat, 2022). Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine Perfect Gabon: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your forest, your fresh water, your ground, your clean grid and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“Your forest cools the planet. Let it lower the price of your debt.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Gabon ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Gabon will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
