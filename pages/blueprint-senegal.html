Design Studio · Perfect Capita

The Senegal wheel: held at 27.2 by water

  • To setPerfect CapitaThe yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards, set with Senegal in its own prices.
  • To setThe price of Perfect SenegalPerfect Capita times the population: the national bill, each year.
  • 27.2CoverageOut of 100, set by water, the standard Senegal provides least.
  • ~14mThe GapSenegalese without safely managed drinking water at home: 73 in every hundred.

The Living LineThe poverty line measures income. The Living Line measures a life: food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood. Perfect Capita is the yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards. Coverage scores each standard strictly out of 100, and a nation is held at its weakest: Senegal at 27.2, by water, with shelter at 30.1 and education at 31.4 next.

Strict measures. Food: share not moderately or severely food insecure (FAO). Water: safely managed drinking water where measured; where it is not, basic water, marked as such. Shelter: the lower of safely managed sanitation and the share outside urban slums. Energy: the lower of electricity per person against 1,000 kWh and clean cooking. Education: the lower of children reading by the end of primary school and lower-secondary completion. Health: WHO service coverage index. Livelihood: share above the stricter of the national poverty line and the World Bank line for the nation’s income group. Each figure links to its source.
