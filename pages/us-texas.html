03 · Solutions

Solutions for Perfect Texas, in order.

Health first: one Texan in six has no insurance, about twice the national rate.

  1. Phase 1Binding

    Health

    The challenge
    16.5% of Texans had no health insurance in 2025, against 8.4% nationally. Census Bureau, ACS 2025
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Issue a Texas health-access bond for community clinics in the border and rural counties, repaid from savings in uncompensated hospital care and paid on enrolment and outcomes.

  2. Phase 2At risk

    Food

    The challenge
    17.6% of households were food insecure in 2022–2024, above the national 13.3%. USDA, 2025
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Put the savings from SNAP accuracy (band 05) into a food fund with Texas food banks and grocers, county by county, starting in Dimmit County.

  3. Phase 2At risk

    Education

    The challenge
    28% of fourth graders read at the NAEP Proficient level in 2024. NAEP, 2024
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Apply the Permanent School Fund’s guarantee model to reading: an outcome contract, paid only for measured gains, backed by the Fund’s distributions.

Phase 3, hold the line: shelter (28,031 without a home on one night, January 2025) and livelihood (12.7% below the poverty line), with water and energy measured next.

05 · Perfect Nations Intelligence

A $725m SNAP bill that accuracy can cut.

From October 2027 every state pays part of its SNAP food benefits unless at least 94 of every 100 dollars are paid exactly right. AI that checks every case before it is paid can shrink that bill.

The bill

$725m

a year in SNAP benefits from October 2027, at the 2025 error rate CBPP, 2026

  • 9.34%of SNAP dollars paid in error, 2025 USDA, 2026
  • 10%share of benefits the State pays from October 2027 CBPP, 2026

Already running elsewhere

  • Nevada chose SAS in June 2026 to check every active SNAP case with AI and flag the ones most likely to be paid wrongly, before the money goes out. SAS, 2026
  • Michigan reviewed 17,500 SNAP payments in three months with an AI case-reading tool before they were issued; the ACLU of Michigan has since asked for its records. Michigan Public, 2026

What Perfect Nations advises

Bring the error rate under 8% and the share halves to 5%, about $360m a year saved. Fund AI case review from the savings, starting with the fiscal 2027 rate that sets the bill for fiscal 2030, and audit it every quarter for underpayments as well as overpayments.

An AI programme appears here only if it moves one of the seven basic standards, already runs in a U.S. state or city agency with a published result, and corrects errors in both directions, so that no eligible family is cut off. Perfect Nations is vendor-neutral: it advises on tools and never resells them.
