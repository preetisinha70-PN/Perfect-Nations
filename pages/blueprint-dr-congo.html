Design Studio · Perfect Capita

The DR Congo wheel: held at 3 by education

  • To setPerfect CapitaThe yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards, set with DR Congo in its own prices.
  • To setThe price of Perfect DR CongoPerfect Capita times the population: the national bill, each year.
  • 3CoverageOut of 100, set by education, the standard DR Congo provides least.
  • 97%The GapOf Congolese children could not read a simple text by the end of primary school in 2019. Education is the guarantee furthest from reach.

The Living LineThe poverty line measures income. The Living Line measures a life: food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood. Perfect Capita is the yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards. Coverage scores each standard strictly out of 100, and a nation is held at its weakest: DR Congo at 3, by education, with energy at 5 and water at 12 next.

Perfect Nations estimate, to be confirmed with DR Congo. The learning and livelihood figures are more than five years old and are marked as dated. The strict measures

  • Food18%Not food insecure; 82% are moderately or severely food insecureFAO, 2024
  • Water12%Safely managed drinking water; 36% have at least basic waterWHO/UNICEF, 2024
  • Shelter13%Safely managed sanitation; 78% of city dwellers live in slumsWHO/UNICEF, 2024
  • Energy55% cook cleanly; 22.5% have electricity, about 124 kWh a person a yearWorld Bank and ARE, 2024
  • Education33% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; datedWorld Bank, 2019
  • Health41WHO service coverage indexWHO, 2023
  • Livelihood15%Above US$3.00 a day; 85% live below it; datedWorld Bank, 2020

Strict measures. Food: share not moderately or severely food insecure (FAO). Water: safely managed drinking water where measured; where it is not, basic water, marked as such. Shelter: the lower of safely managed sanitation and the share outside urban slums. Energy: the lower of electricity per person against 1,000 kWh and clean cooking. Education: the lower of children reading by the end of primary school and lower-secondary completion. Health: WHO service coverage index. Livelihood: share above the stricter of the national poverty line and the World Bank line for the nation’s income group. Each figure links to its source.
