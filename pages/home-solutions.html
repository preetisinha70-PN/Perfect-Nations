    NigeriaSolution No. 5012 Nov 2026

    The Solar Market Bond

    83.5%of the electricity a Nigerian needs is missing

    Nigeria’s next green bond brings solar power to its markets, clinics and homes, state by state.

    • Energy
    • Livelihood
    The challenge
    Nigerians use about 165 kWh of electricity a person a year, against the Living Line of 1,000: 83.5% of the electricity a person needs is missing.
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Nigeria should dedicate its next sovereign green bond to solar mini-grids and storage for markets, clinics and homes, with private developers matching each connection and the states that open their own electricity markets served first. With super intelligence, rooftops, markets and diesel generators can be mapped from space, so every mini-grid is placed where it replaces the most diesel and serves the most traders.

    Flares to Power

    55.3%of the electricity Iraq needs is not supplied

    The challenge
    Iraq’s grid produced 24,600 MW in September 2026, against at least 55,000 MW needed for power around the clock: 55.3% of the electricity Iraq needs is not supplied.
