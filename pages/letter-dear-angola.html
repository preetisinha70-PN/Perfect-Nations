Dear Angola,

The red of your flag stands for the blood shed for your independence, the black for the African continent, and the cogwheel, the machete and the star for the worker, the farmer and the progress you promised one another. Since 11 November 1975 you have rebuilt a nation that war had tried to unmake.

Think of what you have already done. Between 2021 and 2025 you cut your public debt from 69 percent of what you produce to 50.5 percent, and you reduced the debt secured on your oil from about $16 billion to about $7 billion. In March 2026 investors offered about $5.2 billion for a $2.5 billion bond, and in May they came back for $1.5 billion more. Three-quarters of your electricity comes from water and other renewable sources. From the port of Lobito, a railway of some 1,300 kilometres is being rebuilt to carry the trade of a whole region to the sea.

At home the record is just as real. In Moxico, a solar park with storage at Cazombo is the first of 46 minigrids meant to bring power to about a million people. In Luanda, a coding campus drew nearly 25,000 applicants in its first three months. Your young entrepreneurs are building job boards, payment apps and health apps for a nation of 39 million that lives, seven in ten, in its cities.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Three in every four of you cannot always count on enough good food. Fewer than half of your young people complete lower secondary school, and essential health care scores 44 out of 100. More than half of you lived below $4.20 a day at the last count. Food sets your Coverage today, at 25 out of 100. Each of these guarantees is within your reach.

Now imagine the wealth that built Luanda’s skyline turned toward every table in the country. Picture Perfect Angola: a morning when the farms of the planalto feed the cities and sell at a fair price; when every child eats before school and stays to finish it; when the railway carries food inland as well as minerals out; when the rivers that light Luanda also pump clean water to every village. And no Angolan falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you already own: your oil, the Lobito corridor, your rivers, your forests, your young people, and the trust the markets have given back to you. They are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Angolan to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“Oil paid for the state. Your rivers, your railway and your land can feed every citizen.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Angola Design Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Angola will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
