Dear Chad,

Oil carries your budget and your exports, yet one in eight of you has electricity. Your farms and herds carry most families. Almost half of you are children. Let the oil buy the power, the water and the schools that will outlast it.

Your partners are investing in what lasts. In June 2026 the World Bank approved a $160 million grant for water security, and its $295 million energy access project has already sold 145,000 solar kits at subsidised prices. In July 2025 the International Monetary Fund approved a new programme of $625 million over four years.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Each of you uses about 18 kilowatt hours of electricity a year, less than one fiftieth of what a decent life needs, and only eleven in every hundred of you cook with clean fuel. At the last count, in 2019, ninety-four in every hundred of your children reached the end of primary school unable to read a simple text. Only six in every hundred of you have safely managed drinking water. Power sets your Coverage today, at 1.8 out of 100, with reading and water close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Chad: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Chadian falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

“Oil can light a nation once. Let it pay for the grid that lights it for good.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Chad ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Chad will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
