Perfect Capita · The new GDP

GDP counts what a nation produces. Perfect Capita counts what it costs to reach everyone.

PERFECT CAPITA, DEFINED

The yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards.

The poverty line measures income. The Living Line measures a life:food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood.

Each figure counts the people below the line on one standard. The groups overlap, so the figures are never added together. No single global price is published: each nation’s Perfect Capita is set with that nation, in its own prices.
