Perfect Nations™ · Letters to the NationsNo. 1 · Barbados

Dear Barbados,

The trident on your flag is broken on purpose, a clean break from the past, and since independence you have shown, again and again, that a small nation can design its own way.

Think of what you have already built. In 2019, when you restructured your debt, you wrote natural disaster clauses into the new bonds, so that a hurricane would no longer have to become a debt crisis. In 2022 you launched the Bridgetown Initiative and asked the world to rewrite its financial rules for the nations on the front line of climate change. That same year you became one of the first nations anywhere to draw on the IMF’s Resilience and Sustainability Facility, and you completed every review; and you turned debt into ocean protection, funding a plan for your whole sea. In 2024 you completed the world’s first debt-for-climate-resilience conversion and turned the savings into water security.

At home the record is just as real. Every household has electricity and cooks with clean fuel. Ninety-nine in every hundred of your people have drinking water at home. There is a teacher for every fourteen children in your primary schools, and more than fifty thousand solar water heaters already warm your homes. You have redesigned the contract between a nation and its lenders, and the world now borrows your clauses.

One guarantee still holds you back, and it is the one closest to every kitchen. About three in every ten Barbadians cannot always count on enough good food, on an island ringed by fish and warmed by sun all year. That is the standard that sets your Coverage today, at 69 out of 100, and it is the one you are best placed to lift.

Now imagine turning that same genius for design inward, toward every home on the island. Picture Perfect Barbados: a morning when every family sits down to a full table, fed from its own sea and its own fields; when every tap runs clean and not a drop is lost on its way; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and every elder is cared for close to home; when every fisher sails in a boat that is insured, and every roof holds through the season of storms. The sun that warms your beaches powers the island, and your sea, more than four hundred times the size of your land, feeds and employs the people who live beside it. And no Barbadian falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you have always had: the courage to draft what does not yet exist. Your sea, your sun, your water, your people and the trust you have earned are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Barbadian to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“The island that rewrote the rules for the world can now write the most important clause of all: the promise a nation makes to every one of its citizens.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Barbados Design Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Barbados will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
