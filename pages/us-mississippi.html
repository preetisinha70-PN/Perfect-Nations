03 · Solutions

Solutions for Perfect Mississippi, in order.

Livelihood first: the reading reform shows what Mississippi can do; incomes come next.

  1. Phase 1Binding

    Livelihood

    The challenge
    18.0% of Mississippians lived below the poverty line in 2025; 36.4% in Holmes County (2020–2024). Census Bureau, ACS 2025
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Turn the forests and school trust land into income: a Delta enterprise fund that invests in timber, processing and jobs in the poorest counties, with returns shared with the school trust.

  2. Phase 2At risk

    Food

    The challenge
    17.3% of households were food insecure in 2022–2024, above the national 13.3%. USDA, 2025
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Put the savings from SNAP accuracy (band 05) into a food fund with Mississippi food banks and grocers, starting in the Delta.

  3. Phase 2At risk

    Health

    The challenge
    10.2% of Mississippians had no health insurance in 2025. Census Bureau, ACS 2025
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Pay rural clinics on outcomes through a health-access bond, repaid from savings in uncompensated hospital care.

Phase 3, hold the line: reading (32% at the NAEP Proficient level, above the nation) and shelter (1,245 without a home on one night, January 2025), with water and energy measured next.

05 · Perfect Nations Intelligence

A $75m SNAP bill that accuracy can cut.

From October 2027 every state pays part of its SNAP food benefits unless at least 94 of every 100 dollars are paid exactly right. AI that checks every case before it is paid can shrink that bill.

The bill

$75m

a year in SNAP benefits from October 2027, at the 2025 error rate CBPP, 2026

  • 9.51%of SNAP dollars paid in error, 2025 USDA, 2026
  • 10%share of benefits the State pays from October 2027 CBPP, 2026

Already running elsewhere

  • Nevada chose SAS in June 2026 to check every active SNAP case with AI and flag the ones most likely to be paid wrongly, before the money goes out. SAS, 2026
  • Michigan reviewed 17,500 SNAP payments in three months with an AI case-reading tool before they were issued; the ACLU of Michigan has since asked for its records. Michigan Public, 2026

What Perfect Nations advises

Bring the error rate under 8% and the share halves to 5%, about $37m a year saved. Build AI checks into the State’s new eligibility system, and audit them every quarter for underpayments as well as overpayments.

An AI programme appears here only if it moves one of the seven basic standards, already runs in a U.S. state or city agency with a published result, and corrects errors in both directions, so that no eligible family is cut off. Perfect Nations is vendor-neutral: it advises on tools and never resells them.
