The briefing in one page

Barbados is close. Food sets the line.

The answer comes first, as it would to a Cabinet. Everything below supports it.

  • Six of the seven standards are above 80.

    Water, shelter, energy and education reach almost everyone; health scores 82 and livelihood 82.5. Food holds Barbados at 69.

  • The Gap can be financed.

    Up to $338 million a year would lift every Barbadian above the Living Line in all seven standards: 4.2% of GDP. Food is up to $192 million of it.

  • Barbados has done this before.

    Two debt conversions, in 2022 and 2024, freed savings for the ocean and for climate resilience. A third can be bound to food.

03 Capital architecture

Four instruments that could close the Gap.

Innovative financing for development first: each instrument builds on a deal Barbados has already done. Then what AI makes possible.

  • Food · The lead

    The Blue Food Swap

    Make the next debt conversion a blue food swap: refinance costly debt with guaranteed debt and bind the savings to local farms, fisheries and sea farming.

    Proved in Barbados: the 2024 debt-for-climate-resilience conversion, $300M, with guarantees of $150m each from the IDB and the EIB.

    With AIAI tracks every farm and fishery the savings reach and forecasts each harvest and catch.

    Source: IDB, 2024

  • Health · Development banks

    The Health Dividend

    Build on the debt-for-health refinancing with the IDB, so the savings finance a family doctor and a health record for every household.

    Proved in Barbados: an IDB guarantee of $175M for a debt refinancing, approved October 2026; the savings fund health services.

    With AIAI follows the savings into clinics and reports, district by district, who gained access to care.

    Source: IDB, BA-U0003

  • Livelihood · Private investors

    The Blue Enterprise Fund

    Invite private investors into a blue economy fund for fishers, boat builders and small tourism firms, so the sea becomes a livelihood for more Barbadians.

    Proved in Barbados: an IDB credit guarantee facility of $50M that covers up to 80% of a small-business loan.

    With AIAI matches small blue-economy firms to lenders and prices the risk the fund carries.

    Source: IDB

  • Education · Corporate partners

    Every Child, a Reader

    Invite hotels and international business firms to co-finance a national reading assessment and apprenticeships for every school leaver.

    Proved in Barbados: a World Bank Program-for-Results of $50M, approved March 2026, released as verifiable milestones are met.

    With AIAI runs short reading checks in every school, so the nation knows that every child can read.

    Source: World Bank, 2026

04 Launch

The path to market for the Blue Food Swap.

Any new structure must respect the debt path: public debt stood at 93.7% of GDP in June 2026 (Central Bank of Barbados). A conversion lowers the cost of debt already owed; it adds no new borrowing.

  1. Step one

    Ask the guarantors

    Ask the guarantors of the 2024 conversion to price a blue food swap.

  2. Step two

    Choose the debt

    Identify the costly debt to refinance, with the Ministry of Finance and the Central Bank of Barbados.

  3. Step three

    Bind the savings

    Write the savings into a Blue Food Fund that can finance only farms, fisheries and sea farming.

  4. Step four

    Prove it

    Agree the indicators, starting with the share of Barbadians who are food insecure, and publish them every quarter, verified by AI and an independent verifier.
