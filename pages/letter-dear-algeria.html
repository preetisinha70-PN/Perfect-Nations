Dear Algeria,

You are Africa’s largest nation by land, and every one of your households has electricity. Your gas still carries the budget, and your debt is held almost entirely at home. Use these years of energy income to build the assets that will carry the next fifty.

Look at what you are already building. In January 2026 your Treasury opened its first sovereign sukuk, drawing on national savings for roads, equipment and public services, and by April more than four-fifths had been subscribed. The African Development Bank is financing the 495-kilometre railway from Laghouat to El Meniaa, and at Adrar, in the south, a US$3.5 billion dairy farm and powdered-milk plant is rising to feed the nation from its own land.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Education sets your Coverage today: 32.1% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school (World Bank, 2007). Livelihood and shelter come next: 41.8% live below US$8.30 a day (World Bank, 2011); and 62.5% have safely managed sanitation, and 13.3% of city dwellers live in slums (WHO/UNICEF, 2024). Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine Perfect Algeria: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your reserves, your land, your ground, your grid and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“Gas built the grid. Let the grid, the land and the people now finance the next fifty years.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Algeria ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Algeria will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
