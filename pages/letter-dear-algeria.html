Dear Algeria,

You are Africa’s largest nation by land, and every one of your households has electricity. Your gas still carries the budget, and your debt is held almost entirely at home. Use these years of energy income to build the assets that will carry the next fifty.

Your partners are building with you. The African Development Bank has approved $878.09 million, announced in July 2026, for the second phase of the railway from Laghouat to El Meniaa, after €747.32 million for the first. In January 2026 your Treasury opened its first sovereign sukuk, seeking 296.65 billion dinars from national savings for public works, and in April Baladna signed $635 million of contracts for a dairy project with your National Investment Fund.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. At the last count, in 2007, sixty-eight in every hundred of your children reached the end of primary school unable to read a simple text. Forty-two in every hundred of you lived below $8.30 a day at the last count, in 2011. Fewer than two in three of you have safely managed sanitation. Reading sets your Coverage today, at 32.1 out of 100, with income and sanitation close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Algeria: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Algerian falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

“Gas built the grid. Let the grid, the land and the people now finance the next fifty years.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Algeria ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Algeria will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
