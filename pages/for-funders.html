Tanzania

Energy

~63m

Tanzanians who cook without clean fuel. World Bank, 2024

The Solution

Nyerere Power, Every Kitchen

Tanzania should turn the power of the Julius Nyerere station into clean kitchens, with a guaranteed facility like the one that brought nine banks to its railway, financing last-mile connections and electric cookers recovered through electricity bills.

The proof

Partial Credit Guarantee, Tanzania 2026: Up to $800M behind a $1.2B railway loan. African Development Bank, November 2026

The funder that fits

Banks, behind a development bank guarantee

A guaranteed facility brought nine banks to Tanzania’s railway. The same structure can finance last-mile power and electric cookers, recovered through electricity bills.
