The Perfect Nations ReDesign Studio

A community of Co‑designers.

Every nation with a blueprint has its own ReDesign Studio, where citizens and friends design the nation they want to live in. Together they form the Perfect Nations ReDesign Studio: selfless people, at home and abroad, building nations where every citizen has food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

  1. 01

    Write

    Writings

    Your vision of the perfect nation, and the economic solutions to achieve it, in up to 1,500 words. Published with your name.

  2. 02

    Speak

    Voices, the podcast

    One question for every guest: what does the nation already own, and how could it pay for a decent life for every citizen?

  3. 03

    Build

    Enterprises

    An enterprise or a technology already delivering one of the seven standards, ready to be counted and copied.

  4. 04

    Nominate

    Anyone, anywhere

    A person whose selfless work deserves a seat in their nation’s ReDesign Studio.
