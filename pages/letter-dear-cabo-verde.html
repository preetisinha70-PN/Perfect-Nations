Dear Cabo Verde,

You are ten islands with no oil and no gold, and your debt has fallen as a share of your economy every year since the pandemic. Your visitors and your diaspora already invest in you. Let the next decade price the sun, the wind and the sea as the assets they are.

Look at what you are already building. The wind of Santiago turns the turbines of Cabeólica, your first independent power producer, which already makes about a fifth of your electricity, and new turbines and batteries came on line in December 2025. The European Investment Bank and the European Union are rebuilding your ports with €147 million, and the World Bank Group has committed US$1.08 billion across 56 projects to an island nation of half a million people.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Livelihood sets your Coverage today: 65% live below US$8.30 a day (World Bank, 2015). Shelter and food come next: 46.4% of city dwellers live in slums (UN-Habitat, 2022); and 32.1% are moderately or severely food insecure (FAO, 2024). Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine Perfect Cabo Verde: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your visitors, your clean grid, your farmland, your forest and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“Islands without oil can still be rich in sun, wind and sea. Count them, and let them pay.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Cabo Verde ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Cabo Verde will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
