Every nation is richer than its balance sheet.

Perfect Nations values each nation’s five greatest assets, so that what it owns can finance what its people need.

  • 01

    64% of the wealth of nations, 2018

    People

    Human capital, the lifetime earnings of a nation’s people: the largest asset in every income group.

  • 02

    1 in 4 dollars of the world’s wealth, 2014

    Infrastructure

    Roads, rail, ports, power and buildings: produced capital.

  • 03

    23% of the wealth of low-income nations, 2018

    Land, farms and forests

    Farmland, pasture, forests and fisheries: renewable natural capital.

  • 04

    34% people’s share of the wealth of fossil-fuel nations

    Minerals and energy

    Oil, gas, coal and metals: wealth below ground, to be turned into people.

  • 05

    $547bn the value of mangroves as flood defence, 2018

    Ocean and coast

    Fisheries, reefs and mangroves: the blue wealth of a nation.

The World Bank valued the wealth of 146 nations at US$1,152 trillion in 2018, and people alone are 64% of it.

Sources: World Bank, The Changing Wealth of Nations 2021 (2018 data) and 2018 (2014 data).
