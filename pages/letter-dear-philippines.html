Dear Philippines,

A golden sun with eight rays for the eight provinces that first rose for freedom, and three stars for your islands. Your people send home more money than almost any nation’s, and the world’s banks lend to you on your reforms.

Think of what you have already built. In June 2026 the World Bank approved a $1 billion loan and a $20 million grant for your energy transition and climate resilience. In April it approved $600 million to lift reading and mathematics for 21 million students, and in July $268.6 million for water and sanitation on Bohol, Siargao and Jolo.

At home the record is just as real. Essential health care scores 69 out of 100 on the World Health Organization index. Sixty-seven in every hundred of you can always count on enough good food. Sixty-four in every hundred of you cook with clean fuel.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. At the last count, in 2019, ninety-one in every hundred of your children reached the end of primary school unable to read a simple text. Fifty-nine in every hundred of you lived below $8.30 a day at the last count, in 2023. Only forty-eight in every hundred of you drink safely managed water at home. Reading sets your Coverage today, at 9.1 out of 100, with income and water close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Philippines: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Filipino falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you already own: your seas, your islands, your geothermal power, the workers who carry your economy around the world and the English-speaking talent of a young nation. They are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Filipino to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“Your people carry the economy across every ocean. Now carry every Filipino child to a confident reader by ten.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Philippines Design Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Philippines will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
