Dear Vietnam,

A golden star on a field of red. In a single generation you lifted most of your people out of poverty, and in September 2026 the world’s index investors began to buy your companies as an emerging market.

Think of what you have already built. FTSE Russell has upgraded your stock market to emerging-market status, and the first 27 companies entered its global index on 21 September 2026; the World Bank expects up to $5 billion of portfolio inflows. In September 2026 the World Bank also approved more than $660 million for Hai Phong, the Mekong Delta highways and your fishing ports.

At home the record is just as real. Ninety-one in every hundred of you cook with clean fuel. Ninety-one in every hundred of you can always count on enough good food. Eighty-two in every hundred of your children read a simple text by the end of primary school.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Only forty-five in every hundred of you have a safely managed toilet at home. Only fifty-nine in every hundred of you drink safely managed water at home. Essential health care scores 71 out of 100 on the World Health Organization index. Shelter sets your Coverage today, at 45.4 out of 100, with water and health care close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Vietnam: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Vietnamese falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

None of this needs a miracle. It needs what you already own: your rivers and delta, your coast, your factories, a stock market the world now buys, and a nation that has already proved how fast it can climb. They are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every Vietnamese to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“The world now buys your companies as an emerging market. Now let every Vietnamese home have a safe toilet of its own.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Vietnam ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Vietnam will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
