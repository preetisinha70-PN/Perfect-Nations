Dear Togo,

You have turned a narrow strip of coast into a gateway for the Sahel, and your partners are financing the roads and rails behind your port. Four in ten of you are under fifteen. Let the hub that moves the region’s goods also move every Togolese family above the Living Line.

Your partners are building the roads and rails behind your port. In September 2026 the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank approved $75 million for transport and logistics. In June 2026 the World Bank approved $69 million for social cohesion in your northern regions and $40 million to build a gas market that can lower the cost of power. The International Monetary Fund has backed your programme since March 2024 with $403.4 million.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Only thirteen in every hundred of you have safely managed sanitation. Only fifteen in every hundred of you cook with clean fuel, though six in ten have electricity. At the last count, in 2019, eighty-two in every hundred of your children reached the end of primary school unable to read a simple text. Sanitation sets your Coverage today, at 13 out of 100, with clean energy and reading close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Togo: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Togolese falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

“You built the gateway. Let it carry every family through.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Togo ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Togo will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
