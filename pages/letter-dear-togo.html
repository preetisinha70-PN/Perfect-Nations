Dear Togo,

You have turned a narrow strip of coast into a gateway for the Sahel, and your partners are financing the roads and rails behind your port. Four in ten of you are under fifteen. Let the hub that moves the region’s goods also move every Togolese family above the Living Line.

Look at what you are already building. In June 2026 the World Bank approved US$200 million to modernise your transport and logistics around the Port of Lomé, and the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank joined it with US$75 million. In 2025 the World Bank and IFC launched a US$300 million programme to make you a regional hub for agribusiness and animal feed.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Shelter sets your Coverage today: 13% have safely managed sanitation, and 38.5% of city dwellers live in slums (WHO/UNICEF, 2024). Energy and education come next: 15% cook with clean fuels and technology, and 61.1% have electricity (World Bank, 2024); and 18.3% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school (World Bank, 2019). Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine Perfect Togo: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your port, your farmland, your forest, your fresh water and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“You built the gateway. Let it carry every family through.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Togo ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Togo will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
