03 · Solutions

Solutions for Perfect New York, in order.

Shelter first, because every other standard is harder to reach without a home.

  1. Phase 1Binding

    Shelter

    The challenge
    145,560 New Yorkers had no home on one night in January 2025. HUD, 2025
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Issue a State housing bond whose investors are repaid in part on outcomes: each household moved from shelter into a permanent home, building on New York’s own Pay for Success record. With super intelligence, New York can match every family in shelter to a vacant or buildable home and report each outcome as it happens.

  2. Phase 2At risk

    Food and livelihood

    The challenge
    14.0% of households were food insecure in 2022–2024, and 13.8% of New Yorkers lived below the poverty line in 2025. USDA, 2025
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Back a statewide social bond for food and income programs, priced off the State’s credit and paid against measured falls in hunger, county by county, starting with the Bronx. With super intelligence, the State can see where families are falling behind and reach them before they do.

  3. Phase 2At risk

    Education

    The challenge
    31% of fourth graders read at the NAEP Proficient level in 2024. NAEP, 2024
    What Perfect Nations advises
    Fund early reading through an outcome contract that pays only for measured gains, as the State first did for jobs in 2013. With super intelligence, every child can have a reading tutor that adapts to them, in their own language.

Phase 3, hold the line: health coverage (4.7% uninsured), with water and energy measured next.

04 · Capital already committed

New York already finances at scale.

Four instruments on record that a Perfect New York can build on.

  • $21.0bn

    MTA green bonds2016–2025

    Climate Bonds Certified green bonds issued by the Metropolitan Transportation Authority since 2016. MTA, 2025

  • $4.2bn

    The Environmental Bond Act2022

    Bonds for clean water, clean air and green jobs, approved by New York voters on 8 November 2022. State Environmental Conservation

  • nearly$2.7bn

    NY Green BankDecember 2025

    Committed to clean energy since 2013 by the State’s own green investment fund, supporting up to $10.4bn of projects. NY Green Bank, 2026

  • $13.5m

    Pay for Success2013

    The nation’s first state-led Pay for Success project: private investors paid upfront for job training for formerly incarcerated New Yorkers; the State repays on results. State Budget; Harvard GPL
