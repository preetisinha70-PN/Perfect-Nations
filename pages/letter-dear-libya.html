Dear Libya,

You are pumping more oil than at any time since 2013, and in April your rival authorities agreed one budget for the first time in thirteen years. Oil brings almost all your income. Let one budget become one plan: oil money turned into assets every Libyan owns.

In April 2026 your House of Representatives and High Council of State agreed one budget of 190 billion dinars, the first since 2013. Your partners in oil are staying for decades: in January 2026 the Waha concessions were extended to 2050. The same month Misurata Free Zone announced a $2.7 billion partnership to modernise and expand its port, and the World Bank Group keeps a Development Trust Fund ready to help you plan.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Only twenty-two in every hundred of you have safely managed sanitation. Thirty-two in every hundred of you cannot always count on enough good food. Your health services reach 71 on the World Health Organization index. How many of your children read, and how many of you live below a decent income, has not been measured in recent years: those standards wait to be counted. Sanitation sets your Coverage today, at 21.7 out of 100, with food and health close behind. Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine that same resolve turned toward every home in the country. Picture Perfect Libya: a morning when every family cooks with clean fuel and sits down to a full table; when every tap runs clean and every home has a safe toilet; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives within reach of a clinic with the lights on, and every young person finds work that pays. And no Libyan falls below a decent life, because the nation has decided that none will.

“One budget is a beginning. Let it become one plan, and one future owned by every citizen.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Libya ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Libya will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
