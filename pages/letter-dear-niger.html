Dear Niger,

You send your oil to the sea through a pipeline across a neighbour, and you are connecting hundreds of thousands of citizens to power each year. Almost half of you are children, and on the last measure nine in ten could not read by the end of primary school. Let the oil teach and light every one of them.

Look at what you are already building. Your crude now flows by pipeline to the coast of Benin, and in May 2026 you cut the cost of that journey from US$27 to US$15 a barrel. The World Bank finances 23 operations worth US$4.6 billion in your country, among them the Kandadji dam, and in April 2026 the African Development Bank approved US$180.6 million for young farmers in all eight regions.

The work ahead is plain, and it is worth saying plainly. Energy sets your Coverage today: 6% cook with clean fuels and technology, and 21.3% have electricity (World Bank, 2024). Shelter and education come next: 9.1% have safely managed sanitation, and 70.4% of city dwellers live in slums (WHO/UNICEF, 2024); and 9.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school (World Bank, 2019). Each of them is within your reach.

Now imagine Perfect Niger: a morning when every family eats well and drinks clean water from a tap; when every home is sound and lit, and every meal is cooked on clean fuel; when every child walks to a school where a teacher is waiting, and reads with ease by the age of ten; when every mother lives a short walk from a clinic with a nurse on duty; and when every adult earns a decent living. None of this needs a miracle. Your oil line, your power to come, your farmland, your land and your people are assets no balance sheet counts, and together they can carry every one of your citizens to food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and a livelihood.

“Half of you are children. Let every barrel buy a classroom and a light.”

So this is an invitation. Bring your vision, your story and your solutions to the Perfect Niger ReDesign Studio, and join the Co-designers who are already imagining what comes next. Perfect Niger will not be designed for you. It will be designed by you.

Yours in design,

Preeti Sinha

President and CEO, Perfect Nations
