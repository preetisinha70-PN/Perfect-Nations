Design Studio · Perfect Capita

Perfect Capita cannot be reached while any citizen is barred by law.

  • To setPerfect CapitaThe yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards, set with Afghanistan in its own prices.
  • To setThe price of Perfect AfghanistanPerfect Capita times the population: the national bill, each year.
  • BarredCoverageNo score is given while girls are barred from school after grade six and women from university. Of the standards that are measured, energy is lowest, at 14.
  • 9 in 10The GapTen-year-olds in Afghanistan who cannot read a simple text. Education is the standard furthest from reach.

The Living LineThe poverty line measures income. The Living Line measures a life: food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood. Perfect Capita is the yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards. Coverage scores each standard strictly out of 100, and a nation is held at its weakest. In Afghanistan, girls are barred from school after grade six and women from university, so no score is given. Of the standards that are measured, energy is lowest, at 14, then water at 31.

Perfect Nations estimate, from the latest published figures. Shelter has no national measure and education is barred by decree, so neither is scored. The strict measures

Strict measures. Food: share not in acute food crisis (IPC Phase 3 or above), the worst season of the year; 17.4 million people were projected in crisis. Water: safely managed drinking water. Shelter: no national measure of housing quality exists, so none is given. Energy: electricity used per person (5,850 GWh in 2022, divided by the World Bank population), against 1,000 kWh; about four-fifths of it is imported. Education: barred by decree for girls after grade six and for women at university; learning poverty from a 2022 analysis. Health: WHO service coverage index; the bar on women’s medical training is reported by the United Nations. Livelihood: share above the national poverty line. Each figure links to its source.
