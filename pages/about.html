About Perfect Nations

A race every nation can win

The logo turns the contest between nations into the one race worth winning. In this race, every winner makes the world richer, and no nation has to lose.

The Vision
Every nation able to ensure seven basic standards of living for each of its people: food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood.
The Mission
Perfect Nations unites super intelligence (SI) and innovative financing for development to co-design, with every nation, seven basic standards of living for all.
  • The cupDevelopment is a race, not charity, and every nation can enter.
  • The laurelsHonour earned in public. No nation rises alone.
  • The seven starsOne for each basic living standard, together the prize.
  • The goldThe wealth every nation owns but has never counted.

The team behind every blueprint
