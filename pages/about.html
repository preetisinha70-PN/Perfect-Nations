About Perfect Nations

A race every nation can win

Perfect Nations is a new-age sovereign advisory firm, founded and led by Preeti Sinha.

The Vision
Every nation able to ensure seven basic standards of living for each of its people: food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood.
The Mission
Perfect Nations co-designs, with every nation, the innovative financing for development (FfD) that secures seven basic standards of living for all.
