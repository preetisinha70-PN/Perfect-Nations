01

Sovereign bonds

Borrowing on the capital markets in the nation’s own name.

9
  • International Sovereign Bond$3BBorrow dollars or euros from investors worldwide.Pakistan · 2026 · investor demand of nearly $6B

    The treasury sells bonds abroad in a hard currency and pays a fixed coupon until it repays in full.

    Source: London Stock Exchange, September 2026Pakistan blueprint

  • Debut Eurobond$1.25BA nation’s first bond on the international market.DR Congo · 2026 · orders above $5B across two tranches

    A first issue sets the nation’s price with global investors and opens the market for every later bond.

    Source: Rawbank, April 2026DR Congo blueprint

  • Local-Currency Infrastructure BondKSh 240.9BBorrow at home, in the currency the budget earns.Kenya · 2024 · accepted, after KSh 288.7B of bids

    Sold through the central bank to local banks, pension funds and savers, often tax-free to draw demand.

    Source: Central Bank of Kenya, February 2024Kenya blueprint

  • Retail Treasury BondP84.87BCitizens lend to their own government, in small amounts.Philippines · 2026 · awarded at 6.875% a year

    Sold through banks and apps in small denominations, it turns household savings into public investment.

    Source: Bureau of the Treasury, September 2026Philippines blueprint

  • Retail Mobile BondKES 3,000Any citizen can buy a government bond by phone.Kenya · 2017 · the minimum to invest, by phone

    A government bond sold in small amounts through mobile money, so ordinary savers can own a share of public finance.

    Source: FSD Kenya, 2017Kenya blueprint

  • Diaspora Bond$300MA bond sold to citizens living abroad.Nigeria · 2017 · Nigeria’s first diaspora bond

    The nation borrows from its own people overseas, who often accept a lower return to invest at home.

    Source: Debt Management Office, NigeriaNigeria blueprint

  • Panda BondCNY 7BBorrow in yuan, in China’s onshore market.Indonesia · 2026 · the Republic’s first panda bonds

    A foreign government sells yuan bonds inside China and reaches a new pool of investors.

    Source: Ministry of Finance of Indonesia, July 2026Indonesia blueprint

  • Samurai Bond$500MBorrow in yen from Japanese investors.Egypt · 2026 · sovereign borrowing in Japan

    Bonds in yen, sold in Tokyo, add Japanese investors to the nation’s lenders.

    Source: International Monetary Fund, country report 2026Egypt blueprint

  • Sovereign Sukuk$2.35BIslamic finance: returns from real assets, not interest.Indonesia · 2024 · three tranches, orders of $4.5B

    Investors receive returns from state assets held in trust, under Islamic law, in place of interest.

    Source: Ministry of Finance of Indonesia, June 2024Indonesia blueprint

8
  • Social Bond$400MBond money ring-fenced for homes, schools and clinics.Ecuador · 2020 · for affordable homes

  • Sustainability-Linked Bond$2BMiss the national climate target and the coupon rises.Chile · 2022 · the first of its kind

    The money is general budget money, but the interest rate steps up if the nation misses targets it set in advance.

  • Sovereign Green BondINR 16,000 crBond money ring-fenced for clean energy and climate.India · 2023 · in the first issuance

    The proceeds finance eligible green projects under a published framework, with allocation reports to investors.

  • Sovereign Blue Bond$15MBond money ring-fenced for oceans and fisheries.Seychelles · 2018 · the world’s first

  • Sovereign Green Sukuk$1.25BGreen bond finance that complies with Islamic law.Indonesia · 2018 · the world’s first

    Source: UNDP Indonesia, April 2018Indonesia blueprint

  • Sustainable Bond$1.5BOne bond for social and environmental spending together.Brazil · 2025 · orders three times the size

Capital markets

7
  • IPO Cornerstone Investment$145MA development bank anchors a listing, so others buy in.Philippines · 2025 · Maynilad, water for about 10.5 million people

  • Mortgage Refinance BondKSh 1.4BLong-term money so banks can offer affordable home loans.Kenya · 2022 · first tranche, oversubscribed more than fourfold

Pay for results

7
  • Outcome Bond$225MInvestors earn more as verified results arrive.Brazil · 2024 · Amazon reforestation

    The principal is protected; the return rises with the result, here the carbon a replanted forest removes.

    Source: World Bank, August 2024Brazil blueprint

  • Conservation Outcome Bond$150MThe return rises as an endangered species recovers.South Africa · 2022 · tied to black rhino numbers

    Investors forgo coupons; a success payment follows the measured growth of the rhino population.

    Source: World Bank, March 2022South Africa blueprint

  • Carbon-Linked Outcome Bond$30.5MThe coupon is paid from carbon credits a project earns.Ghana · 2025 · of private capital for clean cookstoves

  • Development Impact Bond160%Investors fund the work; outcome payers repay them for results.India · 2018 · of the learning target

    Investors pay for delivery up front. A donor or foundation repays them, with a return, only once an independent evaluator verifies the results.

    Source: Educate Girls, 2018India blueprint

  • Social Impact BondThe same deal at home, with the government paying for results.Colombia · 2017 · paid for jobs found, not for training given

    Investors fund a programme; the government repays them only for the outcomes it wanted, here formal jobs found and kept.

    Source: Inter-American Development BankColombia blueprint

  • Program-for-Results$588MA loan disbursed only as agreed results are verified.Tanzania · 2021 · for primary school learning

    Each tranche is tied to a disbursement-linked indicator, such as pupils reaching a learning level, and is released when that result is verified.

    Source: World Bank, project P169380Tanzania blueprint

  • Results-Based Payments$19.47MA set price for each verified unit of result.DR Congo · 2025 · paid for 3.89 million tonnes of emissions reduced

    A buyer pays a fixed price per verified unit: a tonne of emissions avoided, a child vaccinated, a home connected.

    Source: World Bank, June 2025DR Congo blueprint
