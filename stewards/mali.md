## The seven standards

- **Food, 79.4%:** Not food insecure; 20.6% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=ML
- **Water, 86.4%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=ML
- **Shelter, 7.5%:** City dwellers outside slums; 92.5% live in slums. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=ML
- **Energy, 2%:** 2% cook with clean fuels and technology; 49.5% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=ML
- **Education, 9.6:** 9.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2012, dated. World Bank, 2012, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=ML
- **Health, 41:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MLI
- **Livelihoods, 54.5%:** Above the national poverty line; 45.5% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=ML

Furthest from reach: Energy (98%). Of Malians still cook without clean fuels.
