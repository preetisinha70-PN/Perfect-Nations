## The seven standards

- **Shelter, 7.5%:** City dwellers outside slums; 92.5% live in slums. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=ML

- **Education, 9.6:** 9.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2012, dated. World Bank, 2012, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=ML
- **Health, 41:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MLI
- **Livelihoods, 54.5%:** Above the national poverty line; 45.5% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=ML

Furthest from reach: Energy (98%). Of Malians still cook without clean fuels.

- **41.9%:** Total public debt of the central government, share of GDP, 2025 (estimate); 44.0% in 2024; projected at 40.8% in 2026. IMF Country Report No. 26/165, July 2026, French edition, https://www.imf.org/fr/publications/cr/issues/2026/07/06/mali-second-review-under-the-staff-monitored-program-press-release-and-staff-report-577497 (opened 17 November 2026). The first review had projected 40.9% for 2026.
- **XOF 3,993bn:** State spending in the amended finance law for 2026 (XOF 3,993.319 billion, up from XOF 3,578.2 billion in the initial law); revenue XOF 3,372.895 billion; deficit XOF 620.425 billion. Sika Finance, https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/mali-le-budget-2026-porte-a-3-993-31-milliards-fcfa-en-hausse-de-11-6-apres-revision-de-la-loi-de-finances_62756 (business press; the law is to be read at the Ministry of Economy and Finance). The 2027 draft law, adopted in the Council of Ministers on 18 September 2026, projects spending of XOF 4,462.646 billion (La Nouvelle Tribune, September 2026).
- **$800M:** Infrastructure a mining-backed fund could unlock, from the 2023 mining code. Mining Weekly (Reuters), 3 August 2026, https://www.miningweekly.com/article/mali-says-mining-backed-fund-could-unlock-up-to-800m-for-infrastructure-projects-2026-08-03

IMF Country Report No. 25/106 (Press Release No. 25/111: “an immediate disbursement of US$129 million (SDR 93.3 million)”; the staff report gives “about US$122.5 million”), https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2025/english/1mliea2025001-print-pdf.pdf (opened 17 November 2026)
- **5.5%:** Real growth expected in 2026, after 4.9% projected for 2025; fiscal deficit projected by IMF staff at 2.4% of GDP in 2026. IMF Country Report No. 26/165 (above).

## The enterprises

- **myAgro (Food):** mobile layaway for farm inputs; “In 2025, myAgro served more than 250,000 farmers across Senegal, Mali, and Côte d’Ivoire”, 65 percent of them women. African Development Bank, 23 April 2026, https://allafrica.com/stories/202604230742.html
- **La Voûte Nubienne, AVN (Shelter):** “Since 2007, the deployment of the programme in Mali has enabled the completion of more than 3,000 NV projects for the benefit of more than 25,000 people.” AVN, Mali page (update of August 2023), https://lavoutenubienne.org/mali-235
- **Africa GreenTec (Energy):** its Malian subsidiary, Africa GreenTec Électrification Rurale (AGT-ER) SARL, “produces, distributes and sells renewable energy in 20 communities”; results-based financing of up to USD 1,953,000 from CEI Africa Foundation (Nairobi, 23 January 2024). https://sun-connect.org/cei-africa-foundation-awards-africa-greentec-an-rbf-grant-to-densify-18-gmgs-and-build-7-new-gmgs-in-rural-mali/
- **Kabakoo Academies (Education):** “founded in Mali in 2018”, “over 34,000 registered learners”. Social Enterprise World Forum, March 2026, https://sewfonline.com/kabakoo/. Its co-founder and Chief Executive Officer, Michèle Traoré, is a 2025 Elevate Prize winner, https://elevateprize.org/winner/michele-traore-w2025/
- **OKO (Livelihood):** crop-specific parametric climate insurance; “OKO is part of the Orange Money menu in Mali”; 33,341 farmers insured to date, USD 482K paid in claims, 8,596 farmers compensated. https://www.oko.finance/ and https://www.oko.finance/product
