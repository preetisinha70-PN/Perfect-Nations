## The seven standards

- **Food, 72.4%:** Not food insecure; 27.6% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=TT
- **Water, 69.4%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=TT
- **Shelter, 38%:** Safely managed sanitation; 8.6% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=TT
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=TT
- **Education, 79.4:** 79.4% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2016, dated. World Bank, 2016, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=TT
- **Health, 75:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~TTO
- **Livelihoods, 69.5%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 30.5% live below it, dated. World Bank, 1992, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=TT

Furthest from reach: Shelter (62%). Of people in Trinidad and Tobago lack safely managed sanitation.
