## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Cuba. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CU
- **Water, 95.2%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=CU
- **Shelter, 45.9%:** Safely managed sanitation; 11% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CU
- **Energy, 96%:** 96% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CU
- **Education, 89.2:** 89.2% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=CU
- **Health, 86:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CUB
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for Cuba is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=CU

Furthest from reach: Shelter (54.1%). Of Cubans lack safely managed sanitation.
