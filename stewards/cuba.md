## The seven standards

Re-measured 12 November 2026 at the Steward-General's request, on the delivery test: where Cuba's own agencies publish how much of a service actually reaches people, the standard takes the lower of the strict measure and that delivered share. The first edition (10 November 2026) read only World Bank indicators of connection and access (electricity access 100%, clean cooking 96%, basic water 95.2%, WHO service coverage index 86), which counted Cubans as served while the grid meets about a third of demand. Computed in `tools/measures/cuba_2026_11_12.py`.

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO food insecurity survey (FIES) is published for Cuba. WFP: the state ration (canasta normada) covered only 46% of the recommended energy intake in 2023 (2,100 kcal reference). WFP, Cuba country strategic plan 2026 to 2030, WFP/EB.A/2026/8-A/2, 27 May 2026, https://executiveboard.wfp.org/document_download/WFP-0000173486, accessed 12 November 2026.
- **Water, 71.6%:** 2,681,770 people had their piped supply cut at dawn on 3 November 2026 (Antonio Rodriguez, President of INRH, Criterio Compartido, Radio Rebelde, 4 November 2026; Cubadebate, https://www.cubadebate.cu/noticias/2026/11/04/diaz-canel-sobre-el-abasto-de-agua-en-criterio-compartido-responder-al-pueblo-con-hechos-y-no-con-retorica/). 1 - 2,681,770 / 9,434,593 = 71.6%. Basic water 95.2% (WHO/UNICEF JMP, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=CU). 66% of the cuts were caused by power cuts at pumping stations (INRH, same programme).
- **Shelter, 45.9%:** Safely managed sanitation; 11% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CU
- **Energy, 33.4%:** The Union Electrica (UNE) daily note of 10 November 2026: 970 MW available against 2,900 MW of peak demand, a deficit of 1,950 MW and 1,960 MW of load cut at the peak; on 9 November supply was cut for want of generation for all 24 hours. Granma, https://www.granma.cu/cuba/2026-11-10/afectacion-10-11-2026-07-11-12. 970 / 2,900 = 33.4%. Clean cooking 96% (World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CU) and electricity access 100% measure connection, not supply.
- **Education, 89.2:** 89.2% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=CU
- **Health, 30%:** "Only 30% of the basic list of medicines is available" (President of Cuba, closing speech of the XXIV International Meeting of Communist and Workers' Parties, Havana, 8 August 2026; text published 10 August 2026, https://communistparty.ie/en/2026/08/it-is-not-enough-to-condemn-the-empire-we-must-build-popular-power-at-the-grassroots-level-win-the-streets/). The same 30% was given by the Minister of Public Health to the National Assembly health commission in July 2025 (30% in health units, 32% in pharmacies). WHO service coverage index 86 (2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CUB).
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey is published for Cuba. WFP: a household needed ten to eleven times the minimum monthly wage to buy a food basket covering its members' caloric needs (2023). WFP, same document.

Furthest from reach: Health (70%). Of the basic list of medicines is not available to Cubans.

## Open gaps

- Cuba is not a member of the IMF or the World Bank Group: no IMF debt or growth figures and no World Bank financing.
- No capital already committed and no Newswire item on record yet.
- Population: ONEI (9.43 million, end of 2025) and the World Bank (10.94 million, 2025) disagree; the page uses ONEI. Independent demographer Juan Carlos Albizu-Campos estimates fewer still (secondary, not used).
- Health: the 30% figure links to the published text of the President's speech; link the official Spanish text (Presidencia or Cubadebate) when found.
- Energy: the reliability of supply is read from one day's UNE note (10 November 2026); the weekly review reads the latest note and updates the figure.
- No FAO food insecurity measure and no poverty survey: Food and Livelihood stay Not measured, and Perfect Capita is not declared.
