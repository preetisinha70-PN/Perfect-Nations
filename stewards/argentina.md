## The seven standards

- **Food, 66.2%:** Not food insecure; 33.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=AR
- **Water, 99%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient), dated. WHO/UNICEF, 2016, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=AR
- **Shelter, 48.5%:** Safely managed sanitation; 14.5% of city dwellers live in slums, dated. WHO/UNICEF, 2016, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=AR
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 98.4% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=AR
- **Education, 40.9:** 40.9% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=AR
- **Health, 80:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~ARG
- **Livelihoods, 71.8%:** Above the national poverty line; 28.2% live below it. World Bank, 2025, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=AR

Furthest from reach: Education (59.1%). Of children in Argentina cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school, on 2019 data.
