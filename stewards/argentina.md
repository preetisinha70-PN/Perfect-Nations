## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Agua Segura (Water):** water-treatment technology and hygiene training in schools and community centres; with Opella, the “Manos al Agua” initiative installs ultrafiltration in 26 institutions in the Buenos Aires metropolitan area and the interior, with a focus on Salta, to reach more than 5,500 people in 2025. Mercado, 9 December 2025, https://mercado.com.ar/gestion-sustentable/opella-y-agua-segura-impulsan-acceso-a-agua-potable-y-talleres-wash-en-escuelas-argentinas
- **Fundación Multipolar (Shelter):** recovery from homelessness through work over 12 to 18 months; manages the largest shelters of the City of Buenos Aires, aiding around 8,000 people a day; 113 people placed in paid work since June 2019. Ashoka (Fellow since 2023), https://www.ashoka.org/en/fellow/malena-fam%C3%A1
- **Mamotest (Health):** impact company born in Corrientes for early detection of breast cancer where traditional health systems do not reach, with its own artificial intelligence algorithm for reading mammograms. iProUP, 23 May 2025, https://www.iproup.com/innovacion/56370-de-corrientes-a-abu-dhabi-el-camino-de-crecimiento-de-esta-startup-nortena ; World Economic Forum, https://weforum.org/organizations/mamotest
- **Arbusta (Livelihood):** technology services company that hires and trains young people aged 18 to 28 with no work experience, begun in 2013 for young people from vulnerable neighbourhoods of Buenos Aires; artificial intelligence training and validation for companies. iProUP, 19 April 2026, https://www.iproup.com/startups/66929-de-jovenes-sin-experiencia-a-expertos-ia-sedujo-a-mercado-libre-y-universidad-de-eeuu
- **Asteroid (Háblalo) (Livelihood):** free communication app used by more than 500,000 people with disabilities in 75 countries; Háblalo for Employment supports their entry into customer service jobs; finalist, Premio a la Innovación con Impacto 2026 (Fundación Banco Nación and Endeavor Argentina). Endeavor Argentina, 2 November 2026, https://www.endeavor.org.ar/blog-article-novedades-programa-innovacion-impacto-emprendimientos-endeavor-fbn/
