## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Habitat for Humanity Bulgaria (Shelter):** revolving home improvement funds with local partners; interest-free loans up to €600 repaid within a year, for urgent repairs including sanitary facilities; 3,345 households supported through 2,137 microloans in 2014 to 2020; repayment above 95%. FEANTSA, November 2023, https://www.feantsaresearch.org/public/user/Resources/reports/2023/Renovation_case_studies/Habitat_Bulgaria_Booklet.pdf ; micro-financing for urgent repairs since 2008, Bulgarian National Radio, https://old-news.bnr.bg/en/post/101965195/habitat-bulgaria-the-big-problem-with-dangerous-housing-is-not-among-the-state-s-priorities
- **Bulgarian Food Bank (Food):** “the largest national charity dedicated to reducing food insecurity”; “more than 88 000 elderly people, children, and families facing hardship”; “nearly 330 frontline charities, services, and community groups”. European Food Banks Federation, 31 March 2026, https://www.eurofoodbank.org/bulgarian-food-bank-from-small-acts-to-great-impact-through-an-easter-campaign/
- **Jamba (Livelihood):** career hub for people with disabilities in Sofia, launched in 2017; café and catering with a training kitchen; “hundreds of people with disabilities” in stable employment. America for Bulgaria Foundation, https://us4bg.org/?p=34428
- **The Social Teahouse (Livelihood):** supports people with intellectual disabilities or from vulnerable backgrounds through job training and mentorship. Social Enterprise World Forum, Bulgarian case studies, 2025, https://sewfonline.com/?p=34476
- **Joy for our Children (Livelihood):** a bakery where children and young people build working skills in a protected environment, with the goal of paid jobs. Reach for Change, Bulgaria, https://reachforchange.org/entrepreneurs/?country=BG

Bulgaria had 176 registered social enterprises in June 2025 (Economic.bg, 12 June 2025, https://www.economic.bg/en/a/view/social-entrepreneurship-key-to-sustainable-development-in-the-south-west-region).
