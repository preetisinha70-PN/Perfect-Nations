## The seven standards

- **Food, 94.6%:** Not food insecure; 5.4% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=KR
- **Water, 99.3%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=KR
- **Shelter, 95.5%:** City dwellers outside slums; 4.5% live in slums. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=KR
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=KR
- **Education, 96.8:** 96.8% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=KR
- **Health, 88:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~KOR
- **Livelihoods, 99.5%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 0.5% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=KR

Furthest from reach: Food (5.4%). Of people in the Republic of Korea are moderately or severely food insecure.
