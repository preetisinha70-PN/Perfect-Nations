## The register (Capital already committed)

Eighteen lines, grouped humanitarian, multilateral, regional, bilateral, private; newest first within each. Opened 26 November 2026 unless marked.

Humanitarian

- **Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan 2026 ($2.9bn):** OCHA, https://humanitarianaction.info/plan/1514/document/sudan-humanitarian-needs-and-response-plan-2026/article/23-cost-response. Funding and people from UNHCR and IOM, 6 November 2026, https://www.unhcr.org/node/31859: “only around 47 per cent of the required resources received”; 20.4 million people targeted of 33.7 million in need.
- **WFP emergency food and nutrition ($520M):** https://www.wfp.org/emergencies/sudan: “US$520 million to provide life-saving food and nutrition assistance to 5.4 million people until April 2027.” Also: “WFP urgently requires US$426 million from October to March 2027”; “WFP is having to cut 900,000 vulnerable people from emergency food assistance until the end of 2026”.
- **UNICEF Humanitarian Action for Children 2026 ($962.9M):** UNICEF Sudan Humanitarian Situation Report, August 2026, https://www.unicef.org/sudan/reports/unicef-sudan-humanitarian-situation-report-august-2026: “As of 31 August 2026, UNICEF had mobilized US$352.2 million against US$962.9 million HAC appeal (63 per cent funding gap).” UNICEF 2026 HAC Sudan, https://www.unicefusa.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/UNICEF-2026-HAC-Sudan.pdf: “UNICEF seeks $962.9 million to reach 13.8 million people, including 7.9 million children”.
- **UK humanitarian aid for Sudan, 2026–27 (£146M):** UK Government, 15 April 2026, https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-boosts-funding-to-local-responders-to-help-more-than-18-million-people-in-sudans-humanitarian-crisis: “The Foreign Secretary today (15 April) will announce the UK’s £146 million aid pledge for Sudan”; the package will “reach over 1.8 million people in need”.
- **EU humanitarian aid for Sudan, 2026 (€162M):** European Commission, Sudan, https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/where/africa/sudan_en: “In 2026, the EU has allocated €162 million for lifesaving assistance to the most vulnerable in Sudan.” The overall 2026 EU contribution to the response, with neighbouring countries, is €276.3 million. Berlin conference, 15 April 2026, https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/news-stories/news/eu-and-member-states-pledge-over-eur812-million-response-crisis-sudan-2026-04-15_en: “the European Commission’s contribution is €360.8 million”; “€215.5 million will support people in need in Sudan”.
- **Sudan Regional Refugee Response Plan 2026 ($1.6bn):** UN News, 17 February 2026, https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/02/1166979: “the UN refugee agency, UNHCR, and 123 partners appealed on Tuesday for $1.6 billion”; the plan “aims to deliver lifesaving assistance this year to 5.9 million people across seven neighbouring countries”. For people who fled Sudan; separate from the plan inside Sudan.

## The enterprises (Already working in Sudan)

- **Food, Solar Foods:** TechCabal, 8 October 2025, https://techcabal.com/2025/10/08/solar-foods-sudan-food-insecurity/: a Sudanese social enterprise that dries and preserves produce with solar dryers, working with more than 5,000 farmers and 40 cooperatives, many led by women displaced by the war; earned income from drying fees, ingredient and consumer sales and dryer sales; founder won the 2025 Bayer Foundation Women Empowerment Award. Operating now: TechCabal, 3 July 2026, https://techcabal.com/2026/07/03/alaa-hamadto-on-building-through-uncertainty-and-believing-in-sudan-again/ (operations rebuilt on rented land in Kassala after the Khartoum North factory was damaged and looted); Forbes Africa, 13 October 2026 (“relocated from Khartoum to Kassala, in eastern Sudan”).
- **Food, Al Teital Advance Industries:** Al Jazeera, 4 November 2026, https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/sudan-factories-struggle-to-restart-after-devastating-wartime-losses: a Khartoum sesame-oil maker, 300 workers before the war, restarting (not re-opened on 26 November 2026).
- **Water, Practical Action:** Practical Action, 30 December 2025, https://practicalaction.org/?p=120569: 16 solar-powered water yards repaired and 10 built; “more than 20,000 families in 23 communities in Kassala and Gedarif states are able to drink safe water”. An international charity; the work is donor-funded.
- **Energy, Jebel Kidta:** Forbes Africa, 13 October 2026, https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/13/sudans-smes-on-technical-support-and-financial-assistance-amidst-civil-war: “a company selling and installing solar energy systems”; “has sold more than 1,253 solar systems”; “helped provide electricity to farms, homes, schools and health clinics across Gedaref State”; moved from Nyala to Gedaref; supported by the Sudan SME Catalyser Fund.
- **Livelihood, Cashi:** Wamda, 19 November 2026, https://www.wamda.com/2026/11/sudans-cashi-secures-660000-investment-frances-proparco: “Its digital wallet, MyCashi, has reached 2.2 million registered users.”; “The company also offers payment services using USSD, SMS and offline cards”; “payment solutions designed to facilitate the distribution of humanitarian assistance”.
- **Livelihood, Bankak:** AFP via Ahram Online, 1 December 2025, https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/557834.aspx: the Bank of Khartoum’s mobile banking app, used for salaries, remittances and aid payments (not re-opened on 26 November 2026). A bank product.

Searched on 26 November 2026 for further enterprises: Sudanese solar companies and pay-as-you-go solar; Sudanese startups operating through the war (health, agritech, solar, edtech); water enterprises in Gedaref, Kassala and Port Sudan; digital health and telemedicine; pharmacies and medicine delivery. No health, shelter or education social enterprise operating in Sudan with a 2024-or-later source was found. Seen but not listed: Speetar (telehealth, based in Libya; Sudan named as a planned market, MIT Solve); Sudan Family Planning Association (an IPPF member association: 43.4 million services to 12.6 million clients in 2024; earned income not shown); Nutricrop (fertiliser, seed and agrochemical supplier that resumed with the Sudan SME Catalyser Fund, Forbes Africa, 2026).
