## The seven standards

- **Food, 60%:** Not in IPC Phase 3 or above; nearly 19.5 million, two in five, face acute food insecurity. IPC via WFP, 2026, https://www.wfp.org/news/joint-news-release-wfpfaounicef-risk-famine-persists-nearly-195-million-people-face-acute-food
- **Water, 64.9%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=SD
- **Shelter, 26.3%:** City dwellers outside slums; 73.7% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=SD
- **Energy, 30.9:** About 309 kWh of electricity a person a year, against the Living Line of 1,000; 47% cook with clean fuels (2024). Ember, 2024, https://ember-energy.org/data/yearly-electricity-data/
- **Education, 47.5:** 47.5% of young people complete lower secondary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=SD
- **Health, 48:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~SDN
- **Livelihoods, 53.5%:** Above the national poverty line; 46.5% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2009, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=SD

Furthest from reach: Shelter (73.7%). Of city dwellers in Sudan live in slums.

## The record and the register

### The record (5 lines)
- **$2.9bn:** Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan 2026, including $526 million for refugees. OCHA, 2026, https://humanitarianaction.info/plan/1514/document/sudan-humanitarian-needs-and-response-plan-2026/article/23-cost-response
- **57%:** Funding gap of the 2026 plan as of 8 October 2026; nutrition 30% and water and sanitation 23% funded. Sudan Tribune, 2026, https://sudantribune.com/?p=319889 (business and news press, not OCHA’s Financial Tracking Service).
- **$402M:** Needed by WFP from October 2026 to March 2027 for emergency food for 4 million people. WFP, 2026, https://www.wfp.org/emergencies/sudan
- **$182M (added 25 November 2026):** World Bank press release, 12 January 2025, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/01/12/world-bank-approves-182-million-to-enhance-health-and-safety-nets-in-afe-sudan: grants for health and safety nets, including a $62.5 million IDA grant to UNICEF and a $19.5 million grant to WHO (read in search results).
- **$76.3M (added 25 November 2026):** World Bank press release, 2 June 2025, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/06/02/advancing-energy-and-digital-connectivity-in-afe-sudan-new-project-to-support-private-sector: ASCENT-Sudan, an IDA grant, electricity and better internet access for 150,000 people through 500 renewable energy systems.

### The register (5 lines)
- **Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan 2026 ($2.9bn, Appeal):** food, nutrition, water, health, protection and education for more than 20 million people. OCHA, https://humanitarianaction.info/plan/1514/document/sudan-humanitarian-needs-and-response-plan-2026/article/23-cost-response
- **WFP emergency food and nutrition ($402M, Appeal):** life-saving assistance for 4 million people; 800,000 cut from assistance for lack of funds. WFP, https://www.wfp.org/emergencies/sudan
- **World Bank, ASCENT-Sudan ($76.3M, Grant: Approved):** release of 2 June 2025 (above). The project page (https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P507788) lists Bank approval on 18 September 2025 in one view and 16 June 2025 in another, closing 28 June 2029; the recipient and implementing agency is the Eastern and Southern African Trade and Development Bank. A third-party tracker gives $73 million; the line keeps the World Bank figure.
- **World Bank, Sudan Enhancing Community Resilience Project, second additional financing ($80M, Grant: Approved):** project page P181490, https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P181490: approved 22 May 2025, an IDA grant of $50 million and a STARS trust fund grant of $30 million (read in search results; the page itself does not open in these reviews).
- **World Bank, health and safety net grants ($182M, Grant: Approved):** release of 12 January 2025 (above). The SANAD Emergency Crisis Response Safety Net Project (P505963, $100 million, UNICEF and WFP, approved 1 May 2025 on its project page, closing 31 December 2026) appears to be the safety net part of this package ($62.5 million + $19.5 million + $100 million = $182 million); it is not listed again, to avoid counting it twice.
