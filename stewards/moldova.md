## The seven standards

- **Food, 74.9%:** Not food insecure; 25.1% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=MD
- **Water, 75.8%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=MD
- **Shelter, 93.5%:** City dwellers outside slums; 6.5% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured, dated. UN-Habitat, 2018, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=MD
- **Energy, 98%:** 98% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=MD
- **Education, 89:** 89% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2006, dated. World Bank, 2006, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=MD
- **Health, 71:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MDA
- **Livelihoods, 66.4%:** Above the national poverty line; 33.6% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=MD

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (33.6%). Of Moldovans live below the national poverty line.
