## The seven standards

- **Food, 82.7%:** Not food insecure; 17.3% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=NZ
- **Water, 100%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=NZ
- **Shelter, 88.8%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=NZ
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=NZ
- **Education, 89.1:** 89.1% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=NZ
- **Health, 89:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~NZL
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for New Zealand is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=NZ

Furthest from reach: Food (17.3%). Of New Zealanders are moderately or severely food insecure.
