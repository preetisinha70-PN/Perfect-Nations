## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **Bjarg Housing Foundation, Bjarg íbúðafélag (Shelter):** “an independent nonprofit organization”; stable, long-term rental housing for low-income individuals and families; “has built and delivered more than a thousand units to tenants”, with around 500 more in preparation, design or construction; 40 rental units at Safamýri, planned for May 2026. City of Reykjavík, 9 October 2024, https://reykjavik.is/en/node/36960 ; established by BSRB and the Icelandic Confederation of Labour (ASÍ) in December 2016 (BSRB, https://bsrb.is/en/about-bsrb/bjarg-ibudafelag).
- **Samhjálp (Food):** more than five decades of work with people living in poverty and social isolation; the soup kitchen Kaffistofan, open every day of the year, around 100,000 meals a year and up to 250 a day; the rehabilitation home Hlaðgerðarkot for 30 people; intermediate and supported homes in Reykjavík and Kópavogur. Reykjavík Marathon charity page, https://rmi.is/en/charity/charities/416-samhjalp-nonprofit . Six capital-area municipalities pay Samhjálp ISK 2.5 million a month for five months to keep Kaffistofan open longer in winter (City of Reykjavík, 24 October 2024, https://reykjavik.is/en/node/37113).
- **Hrafnista (Health):** a new 80-place nursing home at Nesvellir, Reykjanesbær, opened 15 January 2026, its construction overseen by Sjómannadagsráð, whose chairman cites seven decades of experience in running nursing homes; 64 new nursing places at Hrafnista Boðaþing, Kópavogur, opened in September 2025. Government of Iceland, 15 January 2026, https://www.stjornarradid.is/efst-a-baugi/frettir/stok-frett/2026/01/15/Nytt-80-ryma-hjukrunarheimili-Hrafnistu-opnad-i-Reykjanesbae/
- **Sólheimar (Livelihood):** an eco-village established in 1930 on the vision of a society where people with and without special needs live and work together; organic greenhouses, woodworking, ceramics and candle-making workshops, a café and shop selling organic vegetables and products crafted by residents, and a guesthouse. Guide to Iceland, last updated 9 October 2026, https://guidetoiceland.is/travel-iceland/drive/solheimar-ecovillage ; the Vala shop sells Sólheimar products (South Iceland, https://www.south.is/en/service/sesseljuhus-environmental-center).
- **Brynja leigufélag (Shelter):** a rental housing company for disabled people, which spreads its homes across ordinary neighbourhoods, mainly by buying single apartments in apartment blocks, and has built serviced houses in Reykjavík, Hafnarfjörður, Reykjanesbær and Egilsstaðir; working with Búseti to build at least 27 apartments, with five apartments at Hallgerðargata 20 due at the end of 2024. ÖBÍ réttindasamtök, memo to the Environment and Transport Committee of the Althingi, 21 March 2024, https://www.althingi.is/altext/erindi/154/154-1771.pdf
- **Hringsjá náms- og starfsendurhæfing (Education):** an education and vocational rehabilitation centre at Hátún 10d, Reykjavík, recognised by the Ministry of Education and Children as a provider of adult education for 23 September 2024 to 23 September 2027. Ministry of Education and Children, letter of 23 September 2024, https://stjornarradid.is/library/03-Verkefni/Menntamal/FramhaldsfrAedsla/vidurkenning-framhaldsfraedsluadila-Hringsj-2024.pdf
