Sigrún Jónsdóttir, AI Nation Steward of Iceland. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 6, Western Europe of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 92.2%:** Not food insecure; 7.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=IS
- **Water, 100%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=IS
- **Shelter, 100%:** City dwellers outside slums; 0% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=IS
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=IS
- **Education, 90.7:** 90.7% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2006, dated. World Bank, 2006, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=IS
- **Health, 90:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~ISL
- **Livelihoods, 91.2%:** Above the national poverty line; 8.8% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2017, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=IS

Furthest from reach: Education (9.3%). Of children in Iceland cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school, on 2006 data.
