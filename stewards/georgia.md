## The register (14 lines)

Sources opened on 18 November 2026, unless marked.

- **Government of Georgia, Eurobond, coupon 5.125% ($500M, issued):** listed on the London Stock Exchange; announced 29 January 2026; investor demand “reached a record high of USD 2.8 billion”, about 5.5 times the amount offered, from more than 100 international investors; “The newly issued Eurobonds were used to refinance the 2021 Eurobonds”; the Ministry states that the coupon is the lowest among Eurobonds issued in the past four years by countries with a comparable credit rating and that the country’s risk level fell by more than 50 basis points. Ministry of Finance of Georgia, https://www.mof.ge/en/n/events/sakartvelos_mtavrobam_tsarmatebit_ganakhortsiela_500_milioni_ashsh_dolaris_odenobis_evroobligatsiebis_gamoshveba_refinansireba ; the same demand is stated by the National Bank of Georgia, 29 January 2026, https://nbg.gov.ge/en/media/news/natia-turnava-strong-international-investor-interest-in-government-bonds-signals-low-sove . The five-year tenor is reported by 1TV (https://1tv.ge/lang/en/news/london-stock-exchange-congratulates-georgia-on-successful-transaction-of-usd-500-5-year-bond/), not by the Ministry page; the bond redeemed early was the 2021 Eurobond of US$500 million, coupon 2.75%, due April 2026 (CBW, https://www.cbw.ge/economy/georgia-begins-early-redemption-of-500-million-eurobond-prepares-new-issuance).
- **World Bank (IBRD), Commercial Bank Resolution Preparedness Project ($35M, signed):** loan agreement between Georgia and the IBRD signed on 30 July 2026 by Lasha Khutsishvili, Minister of Finance, and Rolande Pryce, World Bank Regional Director for the South Caucasus; US$35 million in contingent financing (a credit line) for Georgia’s Resolution Fund, to be activated only upon an event defined by law; a safeguard until the Fund reaches 3 percent of insured deposits. National Bank of Georgia, 30 July 2026, https://nbg.gov.ge/en/media/news/natia-turnava-the-world-bank-s-support-once-again-reinforces-confidence-in-georgia-s-fina ; listed by Germany Trade and Invest as “Georgia Bank Resolution Readiness Project”, P511994 (https://www.gtai.de/de/trade/georgien/entwicklungsprojekte/staerkung-der-bankabwicklung-in-georgien-2016350).
- **World Bank, Trans-Caspian Transport Corridor: Georgia Accessibility and Transport Enhancement (TC-GATE) ($372M, approved):** approved 2 June 2026; total project cost over US$750 million, the remainder co-financed by AIIB and ADB; electric locomotives and the efficiency, finances and governance of JSC Georgian Railway; two four-lane segments in Kakheti (Badiauri–Chalaubani–Bakurtsikhe) and a Gurjaani–Telavi road; digitisation of road asset management, a National Highway Control Center and intelligent transport systems; support for women entrepreneurs in Kakheti. Expected: 95% locomotive availability, a 20% revenue increase, net emissions cuts of more than 2.3 million tons, about 43 minutes less from Telavi to Poti. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/02/georgia-boosts-role-in-trans-caspian-transport-corridor-with-world-bank-group-support . The press release does not state the lending terms.
- **World Bank (IBRD), ESPIRE Program, Phase 1 ($35M, approved):** Enhancing Energy Security through Power Interconnection and Renewable Energy Program, P179950; approved 21 May 2024; corridor and seabed surveys for the Black Sea Submarine Cable and coordination between governments; Phase 2 (grid on land) and Phase 3 (the cable, with other financiers) only if the cable proceeds. World Bank press release, 21 May 2024, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/05/21/world-bank-approves-35-million-investment-for-black-sea-submarine-cable-project-preparatory-activities (the project page https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P179950 could not be opened on 18 November 2026).
- **AIIB, TC-GATE co-financing ($181.07M, approved):** sovereign; financing approved 15 September 2026; co-financed with the World Bank as lead co-financier; components co-financed at equal shares, pari passu, under the WB–AIIB Co-financing Framework Agreement, including road connectivity (US$336 million). AIIB project page, https://www.aiib.org/en/projects/details/2026/approved/georgia-trans-caspian-corridor-georgia-accessibility-and-transport-enhancement-project.html ; AIIB loan L01123A and the pari passu terms in the procurement notice, https://www.aiib.org/en/projects/details/2026/_download/Georgia/GPN-TCTC-GATE-AIIB-FINAL.pdf .
- **ADB, Tbilisi Bypass Project, Lots 4 and 5 ($250M, approved):** news release of 30 July 2026; an 18.9-kilometre, four-lane section bypassing Tbilisi, Tbilisi Airport–Lochini and Lochini–Rustavi, with access and service roads; upgrades part of the East–West Highway. https://www.adb.org/news/adb-supports-tbilisi-bypass-250-million-loan-boost-georgia-regional-connectivity
- **ADB, Clean, Local, Equitable Access to Reliable Water Program (CLEAR) ($233.2M, approved):** results-based loan approved 24 October 2025; water supply in at least 100 rural settlements; climate- and disaster-resilient infrastructure; operation and maintenance rules with digital tools. https://www.adb.org/news/adb-supports-water-system-development-rural-georgia
- **ADB, Batumi–Sarpi Road ($360M, approved):** approved 26 September 2025; 11 km to trans-European motorway standards from the Batumi bypass to the border with Türkiye at Sarpi, the final section of the East–West Highway; slope stabilisation, rockfall protection, 500 m of shoreline protection; road asset management, road safety and a bridge-testing laboratory at the Georgian Technical University. ADB news release (Georgian), https://www.adb.org/ka/news/adb-supports-batumi-sarpi-road-construction-georgia . BM.ge reports the loan as US$359.7 million (https://bm.ge/en/news/adb-approved-usd-1-bln-in-loans-for-georgia-in-2025).
- **ADB, Energy Storage and Green Hydrogen Development Project ($104M, approved):** approved 16 April 2025; a 200 MW/200 MWh battery energy storage system at the Ksani substation near Tbilisi; Georgian State Electrosystem implements; US$400,000 technical assistance for green hydrogen. https://www.adb.org/news/adb-help-establish-first-energy-storage-facility-georgia
- **ADB, Modern Skills for Better Jobs Sector Development Program, Subprogram 2 (GEL 68.2M, approved):** policy-based loan of GEL 68,198,000 (about US$25 million), approved 22 November 2024; quality and access of vocational education and training; ADB’s first local currency sovereign loan in Georgia. https://www.adb.org/news/adb-program-helping-enhance-vocational-education-and-training-georgia
- **EBRD, Aversi Pharma (€10M, announced):** loan of up to €10 million, 6 June 2026, to renovate and automate a newly acquired central warehouse in Tbilisi; Aversi Pharma runs one of Georgia’s largest pharmacy chains. https://www.ebrd.com/home/news-and-events/news/2026/ebrd-lends--10-million-to-aversi-pharma-in-georgia.html
- **EBRD, Credo Bank ($40M, announced):** loan of up to US$40 million (€34.6 million), 4 June 2026; medium-term finance for MSMEs across Georgia, with a focus on the regions; at least 45 per cent on-lent to women-led and women-owned businesses. https://www.ebrd.com/home/news-and-events/news/2026/ebrd-provides-up-to-us--40-million-to-credo-bank-to-support-msme.html
- **ADB, TBC Bank (GEL 270M, announced):** GEL 270 million (about US$100 million), news release of 26 January 2026; up to 70% for MSMEs, at least 20% of that for women-owned and women-led enterprises; at least 30% for green investments under the National Bank of Georgia’s green taxonomy. https://www.adb.org/news/adb-and-tbc-bank-partner-expand-financing-msmes-georgia
- **Council of Europe Development Bank, Microbank Crystal (€8M, approved):** programme loan approved by the Administrative Council on 13 June 2025; for micro-entrepreneurs and microenterprises, including women entrepreneurs and smallholder farmers, with limited or no access to the traditional banking system; working capital and fixed investment, including agricultural investments. https://coebank.org/en/project-financing/projects-approved-administrative-council/supporting-inclusion-through-microfinance-in-georgia/ ; Crystal states, 15 August 2025, that it matches the loan with 50% for a portfolio of €16 million (https://ir.crystal.ge/?p=7105).

## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **Georgian Wheelchair Workshop (Health):** “GWW is the only local wheelchair manufacturer in Georgia, producing frames from raw materials”; standard urban, all-terrain, lightweight and oversize/bariatric manual wheelchairs. WHO Regional Office for Europe, “Assistive technology market assessment report: Georgia”, 2025, https://atscalepartnership.org/sites/default/files/2025-11/WHO-EURO-2025-12387-52161-80102-%20georgia%20eng.pdf ; “a social enterprise that produces assistive technologies for persons with disabilities”, employing people with disabilities alongside non-disabled staff, 42 Gr. Lortkipanidze St., Tbilisi (Whirlwind Wheelchair, global network, undated, https://whirlwindwheelchair.org/about-us/global-network/georgian-wheelchair-workshop/).
- **Microbank Crystal (Livelihood):** licensed as a microbank by the National Bank of Georgia on 12 February 2025; 27 years of experience; more than 100,000 clients, 48 branches; EFSE loan of US$11.5 million for MSMEs and women-led businesses, 29 September 2025. Crystal, https://ir.crystal.ge/microbank-crystal-secures-usd-11-5-million-senior-loan-from-efse-to-support-msmes/ ; see also the CEB line in the register.
- **Babale (Livelihood):** “pioneering social enterprise in Georgia dedicated to supporting the independent living of individuals with Down syndrome”; operates in Tbilisi; 26 youth take part, over 57 on a waiting list; funded through its enterprise activities. Grand Circle Foundation, partner in 2025, https://grandcirclefoundation.org/schools/babale-social-enterprise/ ; founded in 2015 by the Georgian Down Syndrome Association (Actio, https://actio.ge/en/info-page/sotsialuri-satsarmo-babale-2).
- **Kodala (Livelihood):** social enterprise working since 2016 that “mainly employs people with disabilities”; about 200 types of wooden toys, decorative items and children’s furniture; demand up about 35% on a year earlier. Commersant, 7 March 2025, https://commersant.ge/en/news/business/demand-for-our-products-has-soared-by-35-kodala ; with EU support it opened the vocational social entrepreneurship school “Kodala” (EU for Georgia, 6 January 2022, https://eu4georgia.eu/eu-supports-the-first-vet-school-of-social-entrepreneurship-in-georgia/).
- **Peacock, Parshevangi (Livelihood):** founded in Kutaisi in 2014; “Supporting the socio-economic integration of people with disabilities”; “11 regular employees and hires up to 15 people at peak times”; quilled cards, souvenirs and inclusive education materials. EDEC (Education Development and Employment Center), undated page, https://edec.ge/en/social-enterprises/ ; EU Neighbours East story on Parshevangi (four of its 11 employees are people with disabilities), undated, https://euneighbourseast.eu/news/stories/georgia-eu-supported-social-entrepreneurship-offers-vulnerable-people-a-second-chance-in-life/
