## The seven standards

- **Food, 73.4%:** Not food insecure; 26.6% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GE
- **Water, 70.5%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=GE
- **Shelter, 63%:** Safely managed sanitation; 7.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=GE
- **Energy, 95%:** 95% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GE
- **Education, 85.9:** 85.9% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=GE
- **Health, 71:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GEO
- **Livelihoods, 54.4%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 45.6% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=GE

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (45.6%). Of Georgians live below US$8.30 a day.
