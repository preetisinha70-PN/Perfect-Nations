## The register (4 lines)

Sources opened on 19 November 2026.

- **Central Bank of Kuwait for the Ministry of Finance, treasury bonds and public debt tawarruq, 7 years (KD 150M, issued):** “The Central Bank of Kuwait, acting on behalf of the Ministry of Finance,” announced on 28 October 2026 an issue of Treasury Bonds and Public Debt Tawwaruq of KWD 150 million, 7 years, by competitive auction at a uniform yield of 4.125%. Earlier 2026 announcements on the same page: 23 September, KWD 100 million, 7 years, 3.625%; 19 August, KWD 100 million, 5 years, 3.375%; 29 July, KWD 250 million, 5 years, 3.250%; 22 July, KWD 150 million, 3 years, 2.500%; 24 June, KWD 150, 250, 100 and 50 million of 2, 3, 5 and 7 years at 2.750%, 2.700%, 3.250% and 3.125%; 20 May, KWD 150 million, 3 years, 2.750%. The domestic issues follow the debt law of March 2025: the KD 150 million three-year issue of February 2026 was the 12th tranche “since Kuwait relaunched its debt law in March 2025” (AGBI, https://www.agbi.com/finance/2026/02/kuwait-issues-495m-bonds-for-local-banks/).
- **State of Kuwait, acting through the Ministry of Finance, sovereign bonds, three tranches ($6bn, issued):** “$3 billion in three-year bonds, $1.5 billion in five-year bonds and $1.5 billion in 10-year bonds” “priced at final spreads of 70 basis points (bps), 75 bps and 85 bps above US Treasuries, respectively”; “The total order book exceeded $18 billion”. The RNS announcement of 28 July 2026 (base offering circular and pricing supplements) is listed at https://www.investegate.co.uk/company/62ML ; its text, with the coupons, was not read.

## The enterprises

Opened 19 November 2026.

- **GreenLife (Food):** “a Kuwaiti company co-founded by Adel Al Shamali and Tareq Almazeedi”; Kuwait’s first commercial aeroponic farm; GAP certification in August 2024; phase 1 of 7,500 m², phase 2 of 16,500 m²; 800 to 900 kg a day of Lollo lettuce and 600 to 700 kg a day of iceberg lettuce; water use 96% below field farming. iGrow News, 24 July 2025, https://igrownews.com/greenlife-and-cleangreens-bring-aeroponics-to-kuwaits-desert/ . Green Life Company, founded in 2021, aims at “year-round production of fresh, pesticide-free leafy greens” (HortiDaily, 9 January 2026, https://www.hortidaily.com/article/9799879/kuwait-advancing-automated-leafy-greens-production-into-extreme-climates).
- **Dawraty (Education):** operates “a bilingual, AI-powered learning platform that enables institutions to deliver and track accredited education”; founded by Dr Ryan Dougherty and Hamad AlThunayan; partners include Kuwait University and the University of Bahrain; active in Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Jordan; a $2 million seed round at a $10 million valuation, backed by Qatar Development Bank. Wamda, 8 September 2026, https://www.wamda.com/index.php/ar/2026/09/kuwaits-dawraty-secures-2-million-seed-backed-qatar-development-bank
- **Fawzia Al Sultan Healthcare Network, FSHN (Health):** a non-profit providing physiotherapy and behavioural healthcare, paediatric therapy, care for older adults, mental health programmes and Employee Assistance Programs; children from low-income families “usually receive treatments for free or at a highly reduced cost”; about 70% of the children treated are covered by sponsorships; Run Kuwait held on 14 March 2025. FSHN, https://www.fshnkuwait.org/?p=21 . “FSHN is the only non-profit mental health care provider in Kuwait”; “fees are estimated according to the requirements of each plan” (https://www.fshnkuwait.org/?p=307).
- **Dawi (Health):** “the online psychological counseling platform DAWI”; under a memorandum with the Supreme Council for Family Affairs, “Beneficiaries can book free psychological support and counseling sessions”, through its website or mobile app. Kuwait Times, 16 March 2026, https://kuwaittimes.com/article/41171/kuwait/other-news/family-council-and-dawi-sign-mou-to-provide-psychological-support/ . “Dawi is an innovative digital medical platform based in Kuwait”; Fresh Minds is “the parent company of the digital medical services app, Dawi”; online booking, virtual consultations and a second medical opinion; a subsidiary of Aayan Leasing and Investment Company acquired a strategic stake in Fresh Minds (MENA Startup Digest, 21 October 2025, https://menastartupdigest.com/?p=19433).
- **312 Café (Livelihood):** “Kuwait’s first café staffed entirely by people with disabilities, 312 Café is dedicated to empowerment through opportunity”; Salmiya; “The café has partnered with institutions such as the British Embassy in Kuwait”; co-founder Georges Khoury. Scene Traveller, 7 February 2026, https://scenetraveller.com/Guides/Eight-Inclusive-Eateries-Around-the-MENA-Region . The name honours the International Day of Persons with Disabilities on 3 December (Apostolic Nunciature in Kuwait, 25 September 2021, https://nunciaturekuwait.com/l/visit-to-312-cafe).
