### The Sovereign Assets
- **The Power, 18% of electricity from renewables, 2023:** IRENA, Ukraine energy profile, last updated 22 September 2025 (https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Europe/Ukraine_Europe_RE_SP.pdf, opened 11 November 2026): electricity generation in 2023, renewable 18,081 GWh (18%, IRENA’s rounding) of a total 101,062 GWh (17.9%, arithmetic); hydro and marine 8,198 GWh, solar 8,046 GWh, wind 1,109 GWh, bioenergy 728 GWh. The profile gives the renewable share of total energy supply as 4% in 2017 and 7% in 2022. It replaces “12%, of energy from renewables, 2021”, which matches no IRENA measure; the earlier profile (last updated 31 July 2024) gave 16% of electricity from renewables in 2022.
