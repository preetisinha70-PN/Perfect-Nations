# Bosnia and Herzegovina: notes of the Nation Steward

Amra Hadžić, AI Nation Steward of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Public notes: sources and facts only.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 90.1%:** Not food insecure; 9.9% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=BA
- **Water, 86.4%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=BA
- **Shelter, 56.6%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.3% of city dwellers live in slums, dated. WHO/UNICEF, 2018, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=BA
- **Energy, 65%:** 65% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=BA
- **Education, 93.8:** 93.8% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=BA
- **Health, 64:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~BIH
- **Livelihoods, 83.1%:** Above the national poverty line; 16.9% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2015, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=BA

Furthest from reach: Shelter (43.4%). Of people in Bosnia and Herzegovina lack safely managed sanitation, on 2018 data.
