## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Partner Microcredit Foundation (Shelter):** loans to low-income households and microenterprises for small-scale energy efficiency, programme 2024–2026 (Council of Europe Development Bank, approval of 26 January 2024, https://coebank.org/en/project-financing/projects-approved-administrative-council/partner-microcredit-foundation-supporting-energy-efficiency-investments-for-low-income-households-in-bih/); insulation, double-glazed windows and new heating systems, with an energy-efficiency strategy since 2012 (CEB story, undated, https://coebank.org/en/news-and-publications/projects-focus/warmer-homes-greener-future-energy-efficiency-meets-microfinance/).
- **Insieme cooperative (Food):** Bratunac, near Srebrenica; berries for fresh fruit packs, jams and juices, also sold in Italy; “una 50ina di famiglie socie, 350 che conferiscono i loro prodotti”; “l’80% degli operatori sono donne”. Avvenire, 21 February 2024, https://www.avvenire.it/rubriche/protagoniste/i-frutti-di-pace-di-rada-nel-cuore-dellorrore_73786
- **Bosnian Handicrafts, BHcrafts (Livelihood):** Tuzla; “BHcrafts currently employs 500 women from different religious and ethnic groups”; “89% of BHcrafts’ operations are currently financed by its product sales”. Schwab Foundation for Social Entrepreneurship, awardee page (undated), https://schwabfound.org/awardees/lejla-radoncic ; begun by Norwegian People’s Aid in 1995, the first certified fair trade producer in Bosnia and Herzegovina (2009) (WFTO Europe, undated, https://wfto-europe.org/news/member-of-the-month-bhcrafts).
- **Muharem Berbić Friends Club (Livelihood):** Kakanj; a project of the Muharem Berbić Foundation in a 1936 industrial building; “We use the total profit for the salaries of our employed people with disabilities”. New European Bauhaus Prizes (undated), https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/node/363311
- **Mikrofin (Livelihood):** Banja Luka; “the largest microfinance institution in Bosnia and Herzegovina” (EBRD, undated, https://www.ebrd.com/home/news-and-events/news/2026/ebrd-supports-women-entrepreneurs-in-bosnia-and-herzegovina.html); CEB €10 million loan to expand microcredit, at least 60% for micro-enterprises in rural areas and women entrepreneurs (CEB, 1 October 2025, https://coebank.org/en/news-and-publications/news/ceb-approves-11-new-loans-totalling-800-million-euros/).
