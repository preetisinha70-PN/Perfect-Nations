## The seven standards

- **Food, 88.8%:** Not food insecure; 11.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=LV
- **Water, 97%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=LV
- **Shelter, 92.5%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.6% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=LV
- **Energy, 92%:** 92% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=LV
- **Education, 91.6:** 91.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=LV
- **Health, 77:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~LVA
- **Livelihoods, 77.5%:** Above the national poverty line; 22.5% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=LV

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (22.5%). Of Latvians live below the national poverty line.
