## The seven standards

- **Food, 92.5%:** Not food insecure; 7.5% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=SK
- **Water, 99.6%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=SK
- **Shelter, 83.4%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=SK
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=SK
- **Education, 77.9:** 77.9% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=SK
- **Health, 78:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~SVK
- **Livelihoods, 86.3%:** Above the national poverty line; 13.7% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=SK

Furthest from reach: Education (22.1%). Of children in Slovakia cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school.
