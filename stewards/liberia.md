## The enterprises

- **Liberia-American Poultry Farm (Food):** Bomi County, established in 2021; capacity for over 550,000 birds and daily egg production of roughly 350,000 (as stated by its managing director); more than 450 Liberians employed. Food Business MEA, 17 March 2026, https://www.foodbusinessmea.com/liberias-us10m-poultry-project-expands-creates-jobs/
- **Easy Solar (Energy):** pay-as-you-go solar through an agent network in Sierra Leone and Liberia; solar access for over one million people across West Africa since 2016. Acumen, https://acumen.org/companies/easy-solar-2/. Also: Beyond the Grid Fund for Africa, 4 October 2022, https://beyondthegrid.africa/news/beyond-the-grid-fund-for-africa-signs-a-new-project-in-liberia
- **Green Gold Liberia (Energy, clean cooking):** a social enterprise that produces and distributes bio-briquettes, biogas and biofuels (AECF, 7 February 2022, https://aecfafrica.org/countries/liberia); led a local initiative on briquettes from organic waste and clean cooking stoves (UNDP Liberia, 28 April 2026, https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/turning-environmental-challenges-clean-solutions).
- **Rising Academies (Education):** an operator in the Liberia Education Advancement Program; more than 50,000 students in Ghana, Liberia and Sierra Leone. King Philanthropies, https://kingphilanthropies.org/portfolio-organization/rising-academies/
- **Last Mile Health (Health):** a non-profit, the longest-serving community health partner to the Ministry of Health; the national community health programme reached full scale in June 2024. https://lastmilehealth.org/where-we-work/liberia; role as national technical partner, 27 January 2026, https://lastmilehealth.org/2026/01/27/transitioning-county-management-in-grand-bassa-and-grand-gedeh-a-step-toward-government-ownership-of-liberias-national-community-health-program/
- **J-Palm Liberia (Livelihood):** buys palm kernels from smallholder farmers and cold presses them into oil for health and beauty products; traceability app with mobile money payments in 20 communities. Africa Business Heroes, June 2024, https://www.africabusinessheroes.org/en/the-show/newsletter/hsotm-june-2024/hero-spotlight-of-the-month. Also: GSMA Mobile for Development, https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/blog/j-palm-liberia-transforming-sustainable-palm-oil-through-mobile-blockchain-technology/
