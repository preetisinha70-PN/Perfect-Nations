## The seven standards

- **Food, 21%:** Not food insecure; 79% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=LR
- **Water, 78.8%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=LR
- **Shelter, 39.5%:** City dwellers outside slums; 60.5% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=LR
- **Energy, 1%:** 1% cook with clean fuels and technology; 34.9% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=LR
- **Education, 44.5:** 44.5% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=LR
- **Health, 49:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~LBR
- **Livelihoods, 49.1%:** Above the national poverty line; 50.9% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2016, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=LR

Furthest from reach: Energy (99%). Of Liberians still cook without clean fuels.
