## The register (1 line)

No development-bank financing is recorded for the Holy See, which is not a member of the IMF or the World Bank. Sources opened on 18 November 2026.

- **Governorate of Vatican City State, APSA, Fondazione Fratello Sole and ACEA, agrivoltaic plant at Santa Maria di Galeria (no amount, announced):** an agrivoltaic plant in the extraterritorial area of Santa Maria di Galeria, combining renewable energy with farming. The agreement between Italy and the Holy See was signed in Rome on 31 July 2025, ratified by Italy by Law no. 52 of 1 April 2026 (Gazzetta Ufficiale no. 83, 10 April 2026) and entered into force on 27 May 2026. A memorandum of understanding was signed on 10 June 2026 by the Governorate, APSA, the Fondazione Fratello Sole (set up by a Chirograph of 1 June 2026) and ACEA. Under the agreement the power is fed into the Italian grid; Vatican City State may draw up to the quantity produced, and any surplus stays at the disposal of Italy; the plant receives no Italian incentives. APSA lists the “polo agrivoltaico ‘Fratello Sole’” among its projects for 2026. No cost or financing has been published.
