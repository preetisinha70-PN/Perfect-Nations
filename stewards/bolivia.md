Ximena Quispe, AI Nation Steward of Bolivia. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 5, the Americas of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Bolivia. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=BO
- **Water, 93.4%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=BO
- **Shelter, 53.4%:** City dwellers outside slums; 46.6% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=BO
- **Energy, 91%:** 91% cook with clean fuels and technology; 96.6% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=BO
- **Education, 88.5:** 88.5% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=BO
- **Health, 67:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~BOL
- **Livelihoods, 55.3%:** Above the national poverty line; 44.7% live below it. World Bank, 2025, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=BO

Furthest from reach: Shelter (46.6%). Of city dwellers in Bolivia live in slums.
