## The hero

- **$429M, five World Bank agreements signed, 2026:** “five new financing agreements worth US$429 million” signed by the Government of Togo and the World Bank Group, which raised the World Bank’s “active portfolio in Togo to US$2.1 billion”. LogistAfrica, “Togo: World Bank Commits US$200 Million to Modernize Logistics and Transport Systems”, 21 July 2026, https://logistafrica.com/en/logistic/togo-world-bank-commits-us200-million-to-modernize-logistics-and-transport-systems/ (opened 17 November 2026). The article names five operations: PASLT US$200 million, the West Africa Regional Electricity Market Program US$100 million, PRIME-GAS US$40 million, COSO additional financing US$69 million and WURI US$20 million. Four of the five are confirmed on World Bank pages (see the register); the US$100 million for the electricity programme is not stated on the World Bank release. The World Bank’s Togo page states the active portfolio: “20 operations for a total commitment of $2.1 billion”, https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/country/togo (opened 17 November 2026).
- **$385M, transport and logistics programme, all financiers:** AIIB, Project Summary Information, Transport Connectivity and Logistics Improvement Project (P001063), dated 9 September 2026: total project cost USD385 million (World Bank USD200 million, EIB USD90 million, AIIB USD75 million, private sector USD20 million), https://www.aiib.org/en/projects/details/2026/_download/Republic_of_Togo/PSI-P001063-Togo-PASLT-Sep-9-2026.pdf (opened 17 November 2026).

IMF Press Release No. 26/230, 29 June 2026, https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/06/29/pr26230-togo-imf-completes-the-combined-3rd-and-4th-reviews-under-ecf-arrangement (opened 17 November 2026).

## The enterprises

Sources opened on 17 November 2026.

- **Bboxx (Energy):** solar home systems in Togo since 2017; over 100,000 Togolese people with access to clean energy; first customers in Africa to receive a government subsidy for solar energy payments (2019, CIZO). Bboxx, https://www.bboxx.com/countries/togo/
- **DokitaEyes (Health):** digital patient record by QR code, launched 2015; 45,000 users, more than 230 partner health facilities. Togo First, 6 December 2025, https://www.togofirst.com/en/health/0612-17745-dokitaeyes-a-decade-of-digitalizing-the-patient-journey-in-togo
- **Aquaponie du Togo (Food):** aquaponics in Lomé, first system on 40 square metres since November 2023, water savings estimated at 90%. Togo First, 10 January 2026, https://www.togofirst.com/en/agriculture/1001-17944-togo-aquaponics-startup-turns-to-soilless-farming-to-face-climate-risks
- **Gozem (Livelihood):** V+ programme, 1,000 drivers made vehicle owners, daily repayments. Togo First, 1 October 2024, https://m.togofirst.com/economy/i/14889/gozem-program-helps-1000-drivers-become-vehicle-owners-togo
- **WAGES (Livelihood):** more than 15,000 borrowers and over 90,000 savers; 131,178 beneficiaries, 53% women. SIDI partner page, https://sidi.fr/en/partners/partenaires/wages
- **Assilassimé (Livelihood):** microfinance without collateral or guarantees; 75,627 beneficiaries; women 90% (88% in the page’s summary figures); health mutual fund since 2023. SIDI partner page, https://www.sidi.fr/en/partners/partenaires/assilassime/
