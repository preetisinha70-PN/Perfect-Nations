## The seven standards

- **Food, 43.7%:** Not food insecure; 56.3% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=TG
- **Water, 20.5%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=TG
- **Shelter, 13%:** Safely managed sanitation; 38.5% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=TG
- **Energy, 15%:** 15% cook with clean fuels and technology; 61.1% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=TG
- **Education, 18.3:** 18.3% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=TG
- **Health, 42:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~TGO
- **Livelihoods, 44.4%:** Above US$4.20 a day; 55.6% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.LMIC?locations=TG

Furthest from reach: Shelter (87%). Of Togolese lack safely managed sanitation.

## The record and the register

- **$200M:** Logistics and Transport Systems Improvement Program (PASLT), IDA, approved 16 June 2026. World Bank, 2026, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/16/strengthening-togo-s-logistics-competitiveness-by-modernizing-its-transport-infrastructure
