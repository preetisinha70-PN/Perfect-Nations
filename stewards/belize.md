## The register (12 lines)

Sources opened on 17 November 2026, unless stated.

- **World Bank (IDA), Belize Early Childhood Development and Female Empowerment Project ($23.5M):** approval by the Board of Executive Directors announced 20 April 2026; IDA credit of US$23.5 million and a US$1.28 million grant from the Early Learning Partnership; 60 new classrooms in existing primary schools, 30 preschools rehabilitated or upgraded, around 80 early development centres for children up to age 4. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/04/20/world-bank-supports-belize-in-expanding-early-childhood-education-and-women-s-access-to-jobs
- **Green Climate Fund, CREWS in Belize and Trinidad and Tobago, SAP063 (no amount shown):** approved 30 October 2025; GCF grant USD 24,117,448; total USD 27.1 million with co-financing of USD 2,995,104; accredited entity the Caribbean Development Bank; no share for Belize stated. https://www.greenclimate.fund/project/sap063 ; CDB release “CDB and GCF Finance USD27 Million Initiative to Strengthen Early Warning Systems in Belize and Trinidad and Tobago”, 7 August 2026, https://www.caribank.org/node/12178
- **Green Climate Fund, Integrated Utility Services, Phase 1, FP275 (no amount shown):** approved 3 July 2025 at the 42nd GCF Board meeting; GCF financing USD 26,736,295 (loan USD 20,800,000, grant USD 5,936,295); total USD 68.7 million with US$42,010,000 from the CDB and partners; Barbados, Belize and Jamaica; 40,700 people expected to benefit; no share for Belize stated. https://www.greenclimate.fund/project/fp275 ; CDB release of 31 July 2025, https://www.caribank.org/node/11528 ; CDB release of 12 March 2026 (“including rooftop solar”), https://www.caribank.org/node/11842
- **IDB, Rural Electrification Program ($23.75M):** approved by the IDB Board of Executive Directors; US$20 million global multiple-works loan from the Bank’s ordinary capital (25 years, five and a half years’ grace, SOFR-based), US$2.75 million of non-reimbursable investment financing and US$1 million of non-reimbursable financing from the Bank’s low-carbon energy fund; about 3,000 rural households; solar photovoltaic minigrids with battery storage and smart metering. https://www.iadb.org/es/noticias/belice-aumentara-el-acceso-electricidad-de-la-poblacion-rural-con-apoyo-del-bid ; approval date 16 September 2026 on the IDB project page BL-J0011, https://www.iadb.org/en/project/BL-J0011
- **IDB, catastrophe swap for hurricane risk ($20M):** up to US$20 million of coverage, a maximum payout of US$10 million per policy year, protection from 1 August 2026 to 31 May 2028; Swiss Re provides the underlying parametric coverage; “the first of its kind at the IDB”. https://www.iadb.org/en/news/idb-strengthens-belizes-hurricane-resilience-first-catastrophe-swap
- **Caribbean Development Bank, BNTF 11 ($5.2M+):** release of 15 July 2026; more than US$5.2 million for six sub-projects, four in education and two in water, over a four-year cycle; launched in Belize City with the Government of Belize and the Belize Social Investment Fund. https://www.caribank.org/newsroom/news-and-events/caribbean-development-bank-launches-bntf-11-funding-cycle-belize-strengthen-community-development
- **IDB, public-sector efficiency loan ($8M):** approved by the IDB Board of Executive Directors; total cost US$9 million with US$1 million of local counterpart funding; executed by the Ministry of Public Service and Disaster Risk Management; five-year disbursement period. https://www.iadb.org/en/news/belize-boost-public-sector-efficiency-idb-support (the release states no date).
- **Caribbean Development Bank, Eighth Power Project ($27M):** “US$27 million for Belize’s Eighth Power Project” to modernise the electricity grid for about 114,000 households and businesses, among US$464 million of new support approved in 2025. CDB release of 10 March 2026, https://www.caribank.org/newsroom/news-and-events/cdb-reports-strong-surge-development-financing-approvals-across-region
- **IDB, labour force participation and employability ($8M):** US$7 million loan with a five-and-a-half-year grace period and US$1 million from the IDB Grant Facility; a separate US$7 million loan from Taiwan’s International Cooperation and Development Fund; over 20,000 jobseekers, at least 2,360 trained in priority sectors; loan contract signed. https://www.iadb.org/en/news/idb-supports-belize-expanding-labor-force-participation-and-employability (the release states no date; The San Pedro Sun reported the signing at the Belize Investment Summit on 6 September 2025).
- **IDB and the Global Partnership for Education, Skills for the Future Program ($20M):** US$15 million IDB loan (25 years, five and a half years’ grace, SOFR-based) and US$5 million GPE grant; six high schools to become STEAM labs. https://www.iadb.org/en/news/idb-and-gpe-partner-education-funding-belize (the release states no date).
- **IDB, Sustainable and Inclusive Belize, BL-L1041 ($15M):** approved 14 September 2022; status Implementation; agriculture and tourism. https://www.iadb.org/en/project/BL-L1041
- **Blue Bond for Ocean Conservation ($364M):** A blue loan through The Nature Conservancy refinanced the $553 million Superbond; Belize committed to protect 30% of its ocean; financial close November 2021. https://www.dfc.gov/media/press-releases/dfc-provides-610-million-political-risk-insurance-innovative-debt-conversion (not re-opened on 17 November 2026). The Blue Bond and Finance Permanence Unit, Office of the Prime Minister, presented its milestones to 2029 on 8 December 2025, with a biodiversity protection zone plan of 20.05% and targets of 25% and then 30% (https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Belize%20Blue%20Bonds_Presentation_December%208%202025-compressed.pdf).

## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Hillside Health Care International (Health):** “operates as a faith-based facility providing healthcare to an underserved population near Eldridgeville, Belize”; “Approximately 50 patients are visited in their homes each week”; “Students are required to pay HHCI $2,000 for a four-week rotation” (rotations for 2025-2026 and 2026-2027). Northwestern University Global Health, https://www.globalhealth.northwestern.edu/education/affiliations/Hillside_Belize.html. “provides free medical care and disease prevention”; primary care “at its outpatient clinic and on mobile clinics to 16 villages”; “HHC has worked in Belize since 2000”. Idealist, https://www.idealist.org/en/nonprofit/15c3337afb0d45358f584f07953f13fb-hillside-health-care-eldridgeville
- **Maya Mountain Cacao (Livelihood):** “a certified B Corp”, a major buyer of cacao beans in southern Belize, which “operates on a social enterprise model, buys fresh cacao beans from more than 500 smallholder farmers” and “facilitates the organic certifications for participating farms”. Mongabay, 26 June 2025, https://news.mongabay.com/2025/06/cacao-agroforestry-in-belize-hits-the-sweet-spot-for-people-and-nature/
- **Trio Farmers Cacao Growers (Livelihood):** “made up of 31 mostly Indigenous Maya farmers”; a 379-hectare agroforestry concession in Maya Mountain North Forest Reserve; “In 2024, Trio Farmers Cacao Growers reaped a record harvest of 63.5 metric tons of wet beans”. Mongabay, 26 June 2025, same address.
