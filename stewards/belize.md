## The seven standards

- **Food, 54.5%:** Not food insecure; 45.5% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=BZ
- **Water, 94.9%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=BZ
- **Shelter, 84.3%:** City dwellers outside slums; 15.7% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=BZ
- **Energy, 89%:** 89% cook with clean fuels and technology; 99.6% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=BZ
- **Education, 23.6:** 23.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2001, dated. World Bank, 2001, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=BZ
- **Health, 70:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~BLZ
- **Livelihoods, 48%:** Above the national poverty line; 52% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2018, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=BZ

Furthest from reach: Education (76.4%). Of children in Belize cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school, on 2001 data.
