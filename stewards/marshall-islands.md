## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Marshall Islands. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=MH
- **Water, 87.4%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=MH
- **Shelter, 97.6%:** City dwellers outside slums; 2.4% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=MH
- **Energy, 80%:** 80% cook with clean fuels and technology; 97.2% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=MH
- **Education, 100:** 100% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=MH
- **Health, 66:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MHL
- **Livelihoods, 63.5%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 36.5% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=MH

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (36.5%). Of Marshallese live below US$8.30 a day, on 2019 data.

## The record and Capital already committed

- **$2.3bn:** United States economic assistance to the Marshall Islands under the Compact of Free Association Amendments Act of 2024, through 2043. Arms Control Association, 2024, https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2024-04/news-briefs/us-approves-funding-pacific-island-nations
- **$60M:** Renewable Energy Generation and Access Increase Project, September 2024. World Bank, 2024, https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P181250
- **23.5%:** Remittances received from citizens abroad, share of GDP, 2024. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.TRF.PWKR.DT.GD.ZS?locations=MH
- **8.8%:** General government gross debt, share of GDP, 2025. IMF, WEO Oct 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/GGXWDG_NGDP@WEO/MHL
- **Compact of Free Association, economic assistance ($2.3bn):** Twenty years of US economic assistance to the Marshall Islands under the renewed Compact; $7.1 billion in all for Micronesia, the Marshall Islands and Palau. https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2024-04/news-briefs/us-approves-funding-pacific-island-nations
- **Renewable Energy Generation and Access Increase Project ($60M):** Solar power for Majuro and the outer islands. https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P181250
- **Fiscal Management and Disaster and Climate Resilience DPO with a Cat DDO ($21M):** Budget support with funds that release when disaster strikes. https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P504373
- **Pacific Resilience Project II, additional financing ($15M):** Coastal protection and preparedness. https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P181646
