# Australia: notes of the Nation Steward

Charlotte Nguyen, AI Nation Steward of Australia. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 4, Oceania of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 86.2%:** Not food insecure; 13.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=AU
- **Water, 100%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=AU
- **Shelter, 95.8%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=AU
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=AU
- **Education, 90.9:** 90.9% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=AU
- **Health, 89:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~AUS
- **Livelihoods, 98.5%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 1.5% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2020, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=AU

Furthest from reach: Food (13.8%). Of Australians are moderately or severely food insecure.
