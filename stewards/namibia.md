### The register and the record
- **Public debt:** Minister of Finance, FY2026/27 Mid-Year Budget Review Statement, 22 October 2026, read from the copy hosted by PwC Namibia (https://www.pwc.com/na/en/assets/pdf/2026-minister-of-finance-mid-year-budget-statement.pdf, opened 9 November 2026): the debt-to-GDP ratio “declined by 1.9 percentage points, from 67.3 percent to 65.4 percent” in FY2025/26 (para. 15); “Total public debt is projected at N$201.7 billion, equivalent to 68.7 percent of GDP” for FY2026/27 (para. 58); government guarantees estimated at N$11.8 billion, 4.0 percent of GDP. The Budget Statement of 26 February 2026 gave total government debt of N$174.5 billion, 65.2% of GDP, in January 2026; the IMF’s 2026 Article IV gives gross public debt of 66.1% of GDP for 2025 (calendar year).
- **Eurobond:** IMF Press Release 26/92, 27 March 2026: “the redemption of Namibia’s US$750 million Eurobond in October 2025”.
- **World Bank, Transmission Expansion and Energy Storage Project (P177328):** press release of 6 May 2024 ($138.5 million project, NamPower; a second Auas–Kokerboom line and battery storage); World Bank aide-memoire (https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111324052032014/text/P177328-7873c6c9-0fe0-49bf-a8d9-da6c67be95ed.txt, opened 9 November 2026): IBRD loan of US$100 million approved 6 May 2024, Green Climate Fund loan of US$20 million and grant of US$3.5 million; a US$15 million IBRD Global Public Goods grant was signed in November 2024. 100 + 15 + 20 + 3.5 = 138.5 (arithmetic). Loan signing was planned for early 2025 and has not been confirmed in a primary page.
- **World Bank, Namibia Renewable Energy Scale Up Support Project (P179377):** Project Information Document of 6 February 2023 (financing table US$4.44 million from ESMAP; the text says US$4.5 million) and the aide-memoire (components of US$2.44 million and US$2.0 million; advance of US$300,000 to NamPower on 14 November 2023).
- **African Development Bank:** releases read on allAfrica: Transport Infrastructure Improvement Project phase two, $196.43 million, approved 31 October 2023 (207 km of new rail track; the release also mentions 518 km of track); Hyphen front-end engineering design loan, $10 million from the Sustainable Energy Fund for Africa, release of 9 December 2025.
- **Climate Fund Managers (SDG Namibia One Fund):** 22 December 2023, 24% stake in the Hyphen project with initial development funding of €23 million (the Government completed its purchase on 18 December 2024, per Hyphen’s shareholders page); 4 September 2024, €25 million from the European Union, fund target $1 billion; 19 December 2025, up to $5.15 million for the Zhero Molecules Walvis Bay project.
- **Namibia for Life:** WWF release (no date on the page), $63 million at launch, potential to grow to more than $75 million, up to 100 conservancies, more than 20% of the country, more than 283,000 conservancy members; Community Conservation Fund of Namibia, 26 May 2026, N$1 billion of long-term conservation financing (https://www.ccf-namibia.org/2026/05/namibia-secures-n1-billion-conservation-financing-milestone/).
