## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Equatorial Guinea. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GQ
- **Water, 64.7%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient), dated. WHO/UNICEF, 2017, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=GQ
- **Shelter, 35.3%:** City dwellers outside slums; 64.7% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=GQ
- **Energy, 50%:** 50% cook with clean fuels and technology; 65.1% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GQ
- **Education, 18.9:** 18.9% of young people complete lower secondary school; 2015, dated. World Bank, 2015, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=GQ
- **Health, 49:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GNQ
- **Livelihoods, 41.9%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 58.1% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=GQ

Furthest from reach: Education (81.1%). Of young people in Equatorial Guinea do not complete lower secondary school, on 2015 data.
