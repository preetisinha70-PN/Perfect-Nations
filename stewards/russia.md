## The seven standards

- **Food, 97.2%:** Not food insecure; 2.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=RU
- **Water, 76.3%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=RU
- **Shelter, 58.2%:** Safely managed sanitation; 2.6% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=RU
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=RU
- **Education, 96.2:** 96.2% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=RU
- **Health, 81:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~RUS
- **Livelihoods, 92.8%:** Above the national poverty line; 7.2% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=RU

Furthest from reach: Shelter (41.8%). Of people in Russia lack safely managed sanitation.
