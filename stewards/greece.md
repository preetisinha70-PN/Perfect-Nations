## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Boroume (Food):** matches surplus food with people in need; “an average of 24,400 meals a day”; “over 650 nonprofits across Greece”. Stavros Niarchos Foundation, https://www.snf.org/en/news-stories/stories/boroume-saving-food-to-save-people-from-hunger/
- **Food Bank Greece (Food):** founded in 1995, member of the European Food Banks Federation; works with 324 institutions; over 1.8 million meals with support from The Hellenic Initiative. Greek City Times, 27 November 2025, https://greekcitytimes.com/2025/11/27/thi-food-bank-greece-support/
- **Electra Energy Cooperative (Energy):** certified social cooperative in Athens, founded in 2016 (SEED2SCALE, https://www.seedeuproject.eu/partners/electra-coop/); partner in SPETE, an enabler of the European Commission’s Support Service for Citizen-led Renovation, established 2025, renovating homes for vulnerable households. https://citizen-led-renovation.ec.europa.eu/enablers/spete-greece_en
- **Hyperion Energy Community (Energy):** Athens; 123 members; 500 kWp solar park for self-consumption; project to assist 20 energy-vulnerable women in Athens. EU Energy Poverty Advisory Hub, presentation of 18 March 2026, https://energy-poverty.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2026-04/3.%20Sandy%20EPAH%202026%20-%20Energy%20poverty%20Greece%20-%20G4P%20_%20COMET.pptx.pdf
- **Shedia (Livelihood):** street paper launched in Athens in March 2013; 120 to 150 vendors in Athens and Thessaloniki; restaurant and upcycling workshops. Homeless World Cup, 11 May 2023, https://www.homelessworldcup.org/stories/celebrating-10-years-of-shedia
