## The seven standards

Not re-read at source on 17 November 2026.

- **Shelter, 68.9%:** Safely managed sanitation; 26.3% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CN
- **Energy, 90%:** 90% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CN
- **Education, 81.8:** 81.8% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2016, dated. World Bank, 2016, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=CN
- **Health, 85:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CHN

Furthest from reach: Shelter (31.1%). Of people in China lack safely managed sanitation.

## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Xi Han Er car washes (Livelihood):** car washes employing people with intellectual and developmental disabilities; the first opened in Shenzhen in 2015; “By the end of last year, 63 Xi Han Er car washes had opened across China, providing stable jobs for nearly 700 workers”; the China Disabled Persons’ Federation supports the nationwide expansion (documents of May 2022 and December 2024). China Daily, updated 13 April 2026, https://regional.chinadaily.com.cn/CDPF/2026-04/13/c_1176414.htm
- **Inclusion Factory (Livelihood):** Taicang, Jiangsu; founded in 2014; began with 12 workers assembling automotive parts and now employs more than 40 people; its consultancy supports a further 30-plus in finding jobs at mainstream companies; initiated by the Taicang Roundtable. China Daily, updated 4 December 2025, https://regional.chinadaily.com.cn/jiangsu/taicang/2025-12/04/c_1146200.htm
- **Starstart (Livelihood):** a bakery-and-coffee chain in Wenzhou, Zhejiang, with a supportive workplace for autistic young people; eight outlets, two more in the pipeline; employs 18 autistic youths. China Daily, updated 13 April 2026, https://regional.chinadaily.com.cn/CDPF/2026-04/13/c_1176414.htm
