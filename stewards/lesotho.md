## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **M4.78bn water royalties expected, 2025/26:** Government of Lesotho, 2026/27 Budget Speech, 18 February 2026, paragraph 30 (“The fiscal year will close with a headline surplus of approximately 3.2 percent of GDP … driven largely by exceptionally high-water royalties of M4.78 billion”), https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-27%20Budget%20Speech%2018-02-2026%20.pdf (opened 9 November 2026). Annex I gives M4,778.0 million for 2025/26 (a projection) and M4,953.4 million budgeted for 2026/27; Annex III gives 4,953.4 for 2025/26, which conflicts with Annex I.
- **The lead, about 780 million cubic metres a year:** Trans-Caledon Tunnel Authority, Lesotho Highlands Water Project case study, https://www.tcta.co.za/case-study/lesotho-highlands-water-project/ (opened 9 November 2026): “South Africa receives about 780 million m3 of water from Lesotho”; Phase II adds 465 million m³ a year.
- **99.8% renewable share of electricity output:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, renewable electricity output (% of total electricity output), 2021, read 12 October 2026 (as on The Clean Power card).
- **49.7% below the national poverty line, 2017:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, poverty headcount ratio at national poverty lines (SI.POV.NAHC), read 12 October 2026.

### The Sovereign Assets
- **The Highland Water, 780M m³ a year:** TCTA, as above. Royalties from the Budget Speech, as above. News24 (28 March 2026) reported about R3.8 billion (about US$220 million) a year; it is a news report and no longer stands as a source on the page.
- **The Clean Power, 99.8%, 2021; 60.6% of citizens connected:** World Bank, World Development Indicators (renewable electricity output; access to electricity, % of population), read 12 October 2026. The World Bank release of 27 May 2026 gives household access to electricity of 59% in 2024 (a different measure).
- **The Mountains, 92.9% of land in agriculture, 2024:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, agricultural land (% of land area), read 12 October 2026.
- **The Diaspora, remittances 20.8% of GDP, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, personal remittances received (% of GDP), read 12 October 2026.
- **The People, 2.36m, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (2,363,325, the `population` of the nation file), read 12 October 2026.
