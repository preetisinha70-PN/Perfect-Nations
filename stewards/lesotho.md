- **Education, 3.1:** 3.1% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2016, dated. World Bank, 2016, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=LS
- **Health, 55:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~LSO
- **Livelihoods, 43.2%:** Above US$4.20 a day; 56.8% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2017, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.LMIC?locations=LS
