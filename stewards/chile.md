## The seven standards

- **Food, 80.8%:** Not food insecure; 19.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CL
- **Water, 97.8%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=CL
- **Shelter, 92.7%:** City dwellers outside slums; 7.3% live in slums. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=CL
- **Energy, 96%:** 96% cook with clean fuels and technology; 99% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CL
- **Education, 72.8:** 72.8% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=CL
- **Health, 84:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CHL
- **Livelihoods, 93.5%:** Above the national poverty line; 6.5% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=CL
