## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Guinea. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GN
- **Water, 74.2%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=GN
- **Shelter, 56%:** City dwellers outside slums; 44% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=GN
- **Energy, 2%:** 2% cook with clean fuels and technology; 53.8% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GN
- **Education, 17.3:** 17.3% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=GN
- **Health, 43:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GIN
- **Livelihoods, 56.3%:** Above the national poverty line; 43.7% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2018, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=GN

Furthest from reach: Energy (98%). Of Guineans still cook without clean fuels.

## The Sovereign Assets

- **The Iron Mountain, 4bn t:** High-grade iron ore at Simandou. IMF, Selected Issues 2024, citing Rio Tinto 2023
- **The Bauxite, No. 1:** The world’s largest bauxite reserves. IMF, Selected Issues 2024
- **The Clean Grid, 88.1%:** Of electricity from renewable sources, 2021. World Bank, renewable electricity output (% of total), 2021
- **The Fresh Water, 16,080 m³:** Renewable fresh water a person, 2022. World Bank, renewable internal freshwater resources per capita, 2022
- **The People, 15.1m:** Citizens, 2025. World Bank, World Development Indicators: population 2025, ages 0 to 14 (2025)
