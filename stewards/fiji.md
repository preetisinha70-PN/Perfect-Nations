Litia Naivalu, AI Nation Steward of Fiji. Public notes: sources and facts only.

## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **933,000 citizens, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (933,154), read 30 October 2026. The same figure is on The People card.
- **97% complete lower-secondary school:** UNESCO, lower-secondary completion rate, 2024, 97.3% (via World Bank, indicator SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS), as on the Education standard.
- **$199.6 million for four critical bridges, 2025:** World Bank, “World Bank Supports Critical Infrastructure Benefitting More Than Half a Million People in Fiji”, 24 January 2025, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/01/24/world-bank-supports-critical-infrastructure-benefitting-more-than-half-a-million-people-in-fiji (opened 8 November 2026): US$199.56 million (FJD 464 million) in all, of which the World Bank US$50 million, ADB cofinancing US$134.5 million and the Government of Fiji US$15.06 million.
- **The lead, the first sovereign blue bond:** UNDP Pacific, “Launch of Fiji’s first-ever Sovereign Blue Bond”, 2 November 2023, https://www.undp.org/pacific/press-releases/launch-fijis-first-ever-sovereign-blue-bond (opened 8 November 2026): a FJD 20 million ceiling, three-year bonds at 1% and fifteen-year bonds at 4.2%, issued on 8 and 9 November 2023, for nature-based coastal protection, aquaculture, sustainable towns, and solid waste and wastewater. The Fiji Times (11 November 2023) reports bids above $60 million, three times the issue. Neither UNDP, RNZ (3 November 2023) nor the Fiji Times describes it as the first in the Pacific.
- **The blue bond tender (the record):** Reserve Bank of Fiji, “Fiji Sovereign Blue Bond Issuance Tender Results”, https://www.rbf.gov.fj/download/488/fiji-sovereign-blue-bonds/42983/domestic-markets-fiji-sovereign-blue-bonds-tender-08-11-2023.pdf (opened 14 November 2026): tender 7 November 2023, settlement 8 November 2023; FJD 20 million offered; bids of FJD 5.10 million (three years) and FJD 55.20 million (fifteen years), FJD 60.3 million in all (arithmetic); FJD 5 million and FJD 15 million accepted at coupons of 1.00% and 4.20%. Re-checked 14 November 2026: neither UNDP (2 November 2023; 11 November 2021) nor the Reserve Bank of Fiji describes it as the first in the Pacific.

### The Sovereign Assets
- **The People, 933,000, 2025; 60 in every hundred in towns and cities:** World Bank, World Development Indicators (population and urban share), read 30 October 2026.
- **The Land, 17% of land in farms and pasture:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, agricultural land (% of land area), read 30 October 2026.
- **The Forest, 61.7% of land under forest:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, forest area (% of land area), read 30 October 2026.
- **The Water, 31,052 m³ of renewable fresh water per person:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, renewable internal freshwater resources per capita, read 30 October 2026.
- **The Power, 52% of electricity from renewables, 2023:** IRENA, Renewable Energy Statistical Profile, Fiji, last updated 22 September 2025 (Statistical_Profiles/Oceania/Fiji_Oceania_RE_SP.pdf; read 14 November 2026 on IRENA’s content server): renewable 670 of 1,285 GWh in 2023 (IRENA shows 52%; 52.1% by arithmetic); hydro and marine 540 GWh, bioenergy 116 GWh, solar 14 GWh, wind 0 GWh. It replaces 60.1% “of energy from renewables, 2021” (World Bank, renewable energy share), a different indicator. Energy Fiji Limited’s Annual Report 2025 gives 49.39% of its own 2025 generation from its renewable stations (616.577 million units).

### The register and the record
- **Fiji Growth and Resilience Second Development Policy Financing (P511754), $150 million:** World Bank FinancesOne, https://financesone.worldbank.org/countries/fiji (opened 8 and 14 November 2026): three commitments of $104.5 million, $18 million and $27.5 million, approved 25 June 2026; the sum is arithmetic. The AIIB release of 24 July 2026 gives US$230 million of budget support from the two institutions together; $150 million plus $80 million is arithmetic.
- **Accelerating Trade Facilitation in the Pacific (P508537), $11 million, approved 9 September 2025:** World Bank Finances One, Fiji (opened 14 November 2026); the page gives the name, date and amount only.
- **Public debt (the record):** Ministry of Finance, Quarter 3 Debt Report FY2025–2026 (June 2026), https://www.finance.gov.fj/quarter-3-debt-report-fy-2025-2026/ (opened 14 November 2026): total government debt FJD 11,028.3 million at the end of April 2026, 78.5% of GDP (domestic 67.9%, FJD 7,493.4 million; external 32.1%, FJD 3,534.9 million); government-guaranteed debt FJD 1,131.5 million, 8.0% of GDP, at 30 April 2026. The IMF (2026 Article IV, 29 May 2026) gives central government debt of 78.7% of GDP for 2025 and 82.5% projected for 2026, a different measure.
- **Critical Bridges:** ADB release of 28 November 2024, https://www.adb.org/news/adb-approves-199-5-million-project-improve-domestic-connectivity-fiji (opened 14 November 2026): ADB approved the $199.5 million project, funded by a $120 million ADB concessional loan and a $14.5 million grant, with a proposed $50 million World Bank loan and $15 million from the Government.
- **ADB health loan and coastal grant, signed in Suva:** Ministry of Finance, “Fiji Secures $149m Boost For Health And Coastal Protection”, 12 April 2026, https://www.finance.gov.fj/fiji-secures-funds-for-health-coastal-pretection/ (read 14 November 2026 at https://www.finance.gov.fj/?p=6117): FJD 112 million (USD 50 million) loan, 40 years, 10-year grace, 1%; FJD 37.33 million (USD 16.7 million) grant; Government contribution about FJD 2.7 million. The Quarter 3 Debt Report says both were secured on 10 April 2026.
- **Healthy Oceans and Water Security Improvement Project:** ADB Grant Agreement 1037-FIJ(SF), dated 23 September 2025, https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/project-documents/57294/57294-001-grj-en.pdf (opened 14 November 2026): grant of $10,000,000; loan agreement of even date of $135,000,000; grant closing date 30 June 2033. The ADB approval release of 12 September 2025, as carried in the register before, gave a $125 million loan and a $10 million grant. The Fiji Times’ “$385m” (30 September 2025) is in Fiji dollars.
- **The Fiji Sun’s “AIIB $178 million”** (28 July 2026) is the Fiji-dollar equivalent of the AIIB’s US$80 million, approved 16 July 2026 (AIIB project page).
- **The ADB concessional financing of May 2026** (Newswire, 7 May 2026) is the US$200 million policy-based loan of the second phase of the Sustainable and Resilient Growth Program, approved by the ADB Board on 16 September 2026 (ADB Board minutes).
