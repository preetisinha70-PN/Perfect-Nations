## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **933,000 citizens, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (933,154), read 30 October 2026. The same figure is on The People card.
- **97% complete lower-secondary school:** UNESCO, lower-secondary completion rate, 2024, 97.3% (via World Bank, indicator SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS), as on the Education standard.
- **$199.6 million for four critical bridges, 2025:** World Bank, “World Bank Supports Critical Infrastructure Benefitting More Than Half a Million People in Fiji”, 24 January 2025, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/01/24/world-bank-supports-critical-infrastructure-benefitting-more-than-half-a-million-people-in-fiji (opened 8 November 2026): US$199.56 million (FJD 464 million) in all, of which the World Bank US$50 million, ADB cofinancing US$134.5 million and the Government of Fiji US$15.06 million.
- **The lead, the first sovereign blue bond:** UNDP Pacific, “Launch of Fiji’s first-ever Sovereign Blue Bond”, 2 November 2023, https://www.undp.org/pacific/press-releases/launch-fijis-first-ever-sovereign-blue-bond (opened 8 November 2026): a FJD 20 million ceiling, three-year bonds at 1% and fifteen-year bonds at 4.2%, issued on 8 and 9 November 2023, for nature-based coastal protection, aquaculture, sustainable towns, and solid waste and wastewater. The Fiji Times (11 November 2023) reports bids above $60 million, three times the issue. Neither UNDP, RNZ (3 November 2023) nor the Fiji Times describes it as the first in the Pacific.
