### The enterprises
- **SHABA Water Company (Water):** UNICEF Somalia, story published 23 August 2026: SHABA “manages Borama’s water supply network” under a tripartite arrangement with the Ministry of Water Resources and the local council; nearly 22,000 households connected; 45 water kiosks.
- **BECO (Energy):** The Star, 30 September 2025 (tariffs from $1.00 a kilowatt-hour in 2014 to $0.41 in 2023); the Somali National News Agency, 7 June 2020, names the towns it serves and its 8 MWp solar plant. Not re-opened on 26 November 2026.
- **Baano (Health):** Exploration of Digital Health Technologies, 25 November 2024, “Revolutionizing healthcare in Somalia”: “Baano has emerged as a comprehensive telehealth solution”, with voice and video consultations across specialties. A 2026 case study (International Journal of Telemedicine and Applications, “Implementation of Integrated Telemedicine Services in Resource-Limited Somalia: A Case Study of Baano Healthcare Technology”) is listed but did not open.
- **KIMS Microfinance (Livelihood):** Acumen, 13 June 2025: “the largest microfinance institution in Somalia”, more than 46,000 MSME loans, nine branches, scaling off-grid solar financing. IFC disclosure, 2026, as above.
