## The seven standards

- **Food, 75.8%:** Not food insecure; 24.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=TN
- **Water, 65%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=TN
- **Shelter, 79.8%:** Safely managed sanitation; 7.6% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=TN
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=TN
- **Education, 34.5:** 34.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2011, dated. World Bank, 2011, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=TN
- **Health, 76:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~TUN
- **Livelihoods, 83.4%:** Above the national poverty line; 16.6% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=TN

Furthest from reach: Education (65.5%). Of children in Tunisia cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school, on 2011 data.
