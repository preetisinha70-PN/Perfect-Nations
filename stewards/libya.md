## The seven standards

- **Food, 68.4%:** Not food insecure; 31.6% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=LY
- **Water, 96.4%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=LY
- **Shelter, 21.7%:** Safely managed sanitation; 16.6% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=LY
- **Energy, 95%:** 95% cook with clean fuels and technology; 77.4% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=LY
- **Education, Not measured:** Neither learning outcomes nor lower-secondary completion is published for Libya. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=LY
- **Health, 71:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~LBY
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for Libya is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=LY

Furthest from reach: Shelter (78.3%). Of Libyans lack safely managed sanitation.
