### The Sovereign Assets
- **The People, 237.5m, 2025; 64 in every hundred in towns and cities:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population and urban share, read 30 October 2026; not re-read.
- **The Land, 77.1% of land in farms and pasture; The Forest, 18.9% of land under forest; The Water, 990 m³ of renewable fresh water per person; The Power, 22%:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, read 30 October 2026; not re-read.
- **The Power, label:** the card reads “of energy from renewables, 2021”. IRENA’s energy profile for Nigeria (updated 31 July 2024, https://www.irena.org/IRENADocuments/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/Nigeria_Africa_RE_SP.pdf) gives renewables at 75% of total energy supply in 2021 (mostly biomass) and 27% of electricity generation in 2022 (9,447 GWh of 34,780 GWh). The 22% is therefore not a share of energy; it is most likely the World Bank’s renewable electricity output share for 2021, to be confirmed.
