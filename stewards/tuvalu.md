## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Tuvalu. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=TV
- **Water, 8.7%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=TV
- **Shelter, 39.7%:** Safely managed sanitation; 50.9% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=TV
- **Energy, 84%:** 84% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=TV
- **Education, 100:** 100% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=TV
- **Health, 65:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~TUV
- **Livelihoods, 39.4%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 60.6% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2010, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=TV

Furthest from reach: Water (91.3%). Of Tuvaluans lack safely managed drinking water.
