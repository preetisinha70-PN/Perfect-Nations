### The hero
- **Nearly 90%, of fiscal revenue from oil exports, 2024; the lead, oil exports that pay nearly nine-tenths of fiscal revenue:** IMF Press Release 24/210, 10 June 2024 (https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2024/06/10/pr-24210-south-sudan-2023-art-iv-consult-1st-and-2nd-reviews-extends-program, opened 10 November 2026): “Oil exports account for nearly 90 percent of fiscal revenues and 95 percent of exports”.
