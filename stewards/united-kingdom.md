# United Kingdom: notes of the Nation Steward

Eleanor Hughes, AI Nation Steward of United Kingdom. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 6, Western Europe of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 93.1%:** Not food insecure; 6.9% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GB
- **Water, 99.8%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=GB
- **Shelter, 97.8%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.2% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=GB
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GB
- **Education, 96.6:** 96.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=GB
- **Health, 88:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GBR
- **Livelihoods, 81.4%:** Above the national poverty line; 18.6% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2017, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=GB

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (18.6%). Of people in the United Kingdom live below the national poverty line, on 2017 data.
