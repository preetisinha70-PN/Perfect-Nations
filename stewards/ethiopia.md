## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **135.5 million citizens, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (135,472,051), read 30 October 2026. The same figure is on The People card.
- **$3.4 billion IMF programme, approved 2024:** IMF, Extended Credit Facility arrangement approved 29 July 2024, SDR 2.556 billion, about US$3.4 billion at approval (IMF Country Report 26/174, Fifth Review under the ECF arrangement, July 2026, https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1ethea2026002.pdf, opened 8 November 2026). Total disbursements after the fifth review: about US$2.647 billion.
- **$1.45 billion World Bank reform financing, 2026:** World Bank Finances One, Ethiopia, https://financesone.worldbank.org/countries/Ethiopia (opened 14 November 2026): Inclusive Growth Development Policy Operation (P514383), approved 25 June 2026, US$600 million and US$850 million; World Bank Program Document PD000249, https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060226161033684/pdf/BOSIB-82b946b6-ccb3-4f15-8dbd-bd599ec938e6.pdf: “Development Policy Operation (DPO) of US$1,450 million”, an IDA grant of SDR 619.6 million (US$850 million, including US$200 million from the Crisis Response Window) and an IDA credit of SDR 437.4 million (US$600 million); first tranche US$950 million, second US$500 million. It replaces “$1B World Bank reform loan, 2025”: that operation was a $650 million grant and a $350 million credit (World Bank, 3 July 2025).
- **The lead, electricity entirely renewable in 2023:** IRENA, Ethiopia energy profile, updated 22 September 2025, https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/Ethiopia_Africa_RE_SP.pdf (opened 14 November 2026): 100% of electricity generation in 2023 (17,750 GWh: hydro and marine 17,126, wind 576, solar 34, bioenergy 14); renewables 89% (2017) and 90% (2022) of total energy supply.

- **The Land, 34.3% of land in farms and pasture:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, agricultural land (% of land area), read 30 October 2026.
- **The Forest, 23.8% of land under forest:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, forest area (% of land area), read 30 October 2026.
