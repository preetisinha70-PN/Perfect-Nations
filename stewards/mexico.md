## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **131.9m citizens, 2025; the lead, a nation of 131.9 million:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (131,946,900, the `population` of the nation file), read 30 October 2026.
- **$12B, private capital mobilised with the World Bank Group; the lead, nearly $12 billion:** World Bank feature “Catalyzing Jobs: How World Bank Group Operations Helped Mobilize Nearly US$12 Billion in Private Capital in Mexico”, dated 14 October 2026, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/10/13/the-job-catalysts-how-mexico-mobilized-us-12-billion-in-private-capital (opened 9 November 2026): “Between fiscal year 2022 and fiscal year 2026 (a July-June period)” the World Bank Group “helped cumulatively attract nearly US$12 billion in private capital in Mexico”, rising from US$1.8 billion in fiscal year 2022 to US$3.8 billion in 2026.
- **$500M, disaster resilience, October 2026:** World Bank press release of 21 October 2026, as linked in the register.
- **The lead, a $500 million guarantee drawing private capital into infrastructure:** World Bank press release of 18 December 2025 (Spanish), as linked in the register: a credit enhancement instrument of up to US$500 million, with about US$1 billion of private capital expected for transport infrastructure.
