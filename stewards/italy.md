## The register (9 lines)

Sources opened on 18 November 2026.

- **Cassa Depositi e Prestiti, social bond due February 2034 (€750M, issued):** placed 9 February 2026; eight years; coupon 3.375%; demand above €4.6 billion from more than 110 investors; international investors more than 85% of the allocation; the eighth CDP social bond and its twelfth ESG issue since 2017, worth €8 billion in all; proceeds for the eligible social categories of CDP’s Green, Social and Sustainability Bond Framework, in particular Italian small and medium-sized enterprises, with particular weight on those in economically disadvantaged areas. CDP press release no. 10, https://www.cdp.it/internet/public/cms/documents/Comunicato_stampa_n.10_del_09_02_2026_Social_Bond_CDP.pdf
- **European Union, recovery and resilience plan (€194.4bn, active; refreshed):** €71.8 billion in grants and €122.6 billion in loans; 150 investment streams and 66 reforms; 39% for climate objectives and 25.6% for the digital transition; €15.7 billion for the healthcare system and €26.9 billion for education and active labour market policies; Commission Implementing Decision of 22 May 2026 authorising the ninth instalment, with the press release “Commission greenlights Italy’s ninth payment request for €12.8 billion under NextGenerationEU”; an updated plan of August 2026 amends the Council Implementing Decision of 13 July 2021. European Commission, https://reforms-investments.ec.europa.eu/recovery-and-resilience-facility-1/country-pages/italys-recovery-and-resilience-plan_en (the line cited the Council timeline, which did not open).
- **European Investment Bank, Puglia Region sustainable development (€150M, signed):** release of 3 August 2026; the first €50 million tranche of a €150 million package signed; supports selected investments of the 2021–2027 Regional Programme financed by the European Regional Development Fund; investment programme of about €2.34 billion; expected results include water, social, healthcare, education and transport infrastructure. https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2026-269-a-more-sustainable-and-resilient-puglia-eib-provides-eur150-million-to-the-region
- **European Investment Bank, City of Naples framework loan (€40M, signed):** release of 9 June 2026; planned investments of about €95.6 million; new affordable rental homes in Pianura and Chiaiano; schools, social facilities and sports infrastructure; green spaces, renewable energy and sustainable mobility. https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2026-200-eib-provides-eur40-million-to-the-city-of-naples-to-support-urban-regeneration-and-sustainable-development-of-the-city
- **European Investment Bank, Campi Flegrei reconstruction and seismic safety (up to €1.4bn, signed):** release of 5 November 2025; approved for the Italian Republic as a framework loan to be signed with the Ministry of Economy and Finance in two programmes; up to €550 million for private residential buildings and up to €850 million for public buildings; works expected to be completed by 2032; the related project, Naples Area Seismic Risk FL, is shown “Signed | 17/12/2025”. https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-433-from-the-eib-up-to-eur1-4-billion-for-the-reconstruction-and-safety-of-campi-flegrei-in-naples

- **European Investment Bank, AIMAG integrated water services (€50M, signed; private):** release of 13 July 2026; project AIMAG Settore Idrico Green Loan, “Signed | 09/07/2026”; backed by InvestEU; more than 200 upgrades to water, sewerage and wastewater treatment infrastructure; around 200,000 people in the Province of Modena. https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2026-247-eib-provides-eur50-million-to-aimag-to-improve-water-services-across-the-areas-served-by-the-group

## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **Last Minute Market (Food):** “impresa sociale, spin off accreditato dell’Università di Bologna, che dal 1998 combatte lo spreco”; with Chef Express and Hera, surplus portions from catering points are “recuperato e donato a enti benefici del territorio”; more than 14,000 portions by the end of 2025; now 12 catering points and as many charities from Friuli Venezia Giulia to Sicily. Gruppo Cremonini, press release, Bologna, 11 March 2026, https://www.cremonini.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/COS_Hera_Recupero-eccedenze-alimentari.pdf ; described as a social enterprise and spin-off of the University of Bologna that works with companies, schools and third-sector organisations on the European Circular Economy Stakeholder Platform, https://circulareconomy.europa.eu/platform/en/node/5370
- **DAR=CASA (Shelter):** a Milan housing cooperative that realised Carbonia 3 (48 apartments, 16 for young people and the rest for families in housing emergency) and manages it (Housing Europe, 28 April 2022, https://www.housingeurope.eu/?p=6618); four projects across several Milan districts, with rents from €250 to €450 a month (Il Giorno, 27 September 2023, https://www.ilgiorno.it/milano/cronaca/emergenza-caro-affitti-le-iniziative-sociali-per-abbattere-i-costi-stanza-a-380-euro-8b62445b); in January 2026 offering studios at Carbonia 3 at €445 a month, transitional 18-month contracts, for students and workers aged 20 to 35 with incomes up to €1,500 a month, and beds for young women at Grigioni 2035 (Abitare Sociale newsletter, Cooperativa La Cordata, January 2026, https://cloud.municipiumapp.it/s3/5229/allegati/servizi-sociali/case/abitare-sociale-newsletter/nl_gennaio_26.pdf).
- **ènostra (Energy):** “cooperativa a mutualità prevalente” for the production and sale of energy; 18,732 members and 19,068 active supply points at 31 December 2025; 16 renewable energy communities on its platform and 15 partner communities; a 2026 target of 69 GWh of renewable energy supplied (assembly presentation, May 2026, https://www.enostra.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Presentazione_Assemblea_09052026.pdf); sales of €19,400,327 in 2025 (management report 2025, https://www.enostra.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RELAZIONE_GESTIONE_2025.pdf).
- **Piazza dei Mestieri (Education):** started in Turin in 2004 by five friends; 600 young people from the age of 14 study and work at the Turin site, with further sites in Milan and Catania; courses in cooking, bar service, bakery, pastry, graphic design, IT, hairdressing and beauty, a craft brewery and a restaurant open to the public; students include young people who have left school. Avvenire, 6 December 2025, https://www.avvenire.it/famiglia/vediamoci-in-piazza-li-si-impara-un-mestiere_101656 ; a foundation in via Durandi, Turin, visited by a Ukrainian delegation to study its model (La Stampa, 19 February 2026, on the European Training Foundation site, https://openspace.etf.europa.eu/system/files/2026-03/La%20Stampa%2019022026.pdf).
- **Santagostino (Health):** founded in 2009, “a comprehensive network of specialized clinics, the first in Italy to experiment a model of health care that combines high-quality and affordable prices”, including areas the national health service does not cover, such as dentistry, psychotherapy and speech therapy; “conceived and promoted by Oltre Venture” (https://santagostino.it/about-us); Carta dei Servizi of Società e Salute SpA (Santagostino), revision of 20 January 2026: more than 50 centres opened, mainly in Lombardy, Emilia-Romagna, Liguria and Lazio (https://www.santagostino.it/downloadFile/139339).
- **PizzAut (Livelihood):** a foundation founded by Nico Acampora; pizzerias in Cassina de’ Pecchi (opened 2021) and Monza; 41 hired on permanent contracts; five apartments bought and a sixth being renovated for independent living; a third restaurant planned at the IULM university in Milan. Avvenire, 12 September 2026, https://www.avvenire.it/attualita/la-straordinaria-storia-di-nico-acampora-e-di-pizzaut-non-smettiamo-di-far-lievitare-i-sogni_112918 ; a grant of US$300,000 from the Hilton Global Foundation for housing autonomy and jobs for autistic people (Avvenire, 1 April 2026, https://www.avvenire.it/attualita/lassegno-a-sorpresa-per-i-ragazzi-di-pizzaut-300mila-dollari-da-hilton_106619).
- **Giotto social cooperative (Livelihood):** founded in Padua in 1986; has given work to more than 1,500 inmates of the Due Palazzi prison; runs a call centre booking medical visits for the local health authority, where 30 now work, and assembly work for local firms; about 70 people employed in all. Avvenire, 18 August 2025, https://www.avvenire.it/idee-e-commenti/il-lavoro-in-carcere-serve-una-riforma-realista-e-radicale_94143
