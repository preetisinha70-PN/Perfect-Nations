## The seven standards

- **Food, 98.3%:** Not food insecure; 1.7% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=IT
- **Water, 96.8%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=IT
- **Shelter, 78.2%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=IT
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=IT
- **Education, 94.5:** 94.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=IT
- **Health, 82:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~ITA
- **Livelihoods, 79.9%:** Above the national poverty line; 20.1% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=IT

Furthest from reach: Shelter (21.8%). Of Italians lack safely managed sanitation.
