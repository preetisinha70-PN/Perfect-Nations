## The seven standards

- **Food, 73.8%:** Not food insecure; 26.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=PY
- **Water, 64.4%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=PY
- **Shelter, 58.5%:** Safely managed sanitation; 15.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=PY
- **Energy, 76%:** 76% cook with clean fuels and technology; 99.8% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=PY
- **Education, 22.3:** 22.3% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=PY
- **Health, 79:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~PRY
- **Livelihoods, 79.5%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 20.5% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=PY

Furthest from reach: Education (77.7%). Of children in Paraguay cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school, on 2019 data.
