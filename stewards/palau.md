## The seven standards

- **Food, 71.9%:** Not food insecure; 28.1% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=PW
- **Water, 90.8%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=PW
- **Shelter, 99.4%:** City dwellers outside slums; 0.6% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=PW
- **Energy, 96%:** 96% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=PW
- **Education, 91.7:** 91.7% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=PW
- **Health, 75:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~PLW
- **Livelihoods, 75.1%:** Above the national poverty line; 24.9% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2006, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=PW
