## The seven standards

Not re-read at source on 17 November 2026.

- **Food, 65.6%:** Not food insecure; 34.4% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=DM

- **Education, 90.4:** 90.4% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=DM

- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for Dominica is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=DM

## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Dominica Essential Oils and Spices Cooperative (Livelihood):** after the cooperative reported a slowdown in exports, the Dominica Export Import Agency bought 3,094.27 pounds of bay oil from 78 farmers, financed by a $5 million facility of the World Bank’s Emergency Agricultural Livelihoods and Climate Resilience Project. Office of the Prime Minister, 28 February 2024, https://pressroomopm.gov.dm/dexia-supporting-livelihoods-of-dominicas-bay-oil-farmers/ ; annual general meeting on 9 November 2025, recognising members producing bay oil since 1973 and 2005 (Dom767, 11 November 2025, https://www.dom767.com/news/dominica-bay-oil-veterans-honored-for-decades-of-service-and-quality-production); a cooperative of bay leaf, spice and bay oil producers, supported by Compete Caribbean, 2021–2024 (https://www.competecaribbean.org/projects/dominica/).
- **National Co-operative Credit Union (Livelihood):** formed in 2010 by the merger of the La Salette, St. Paul, Vieille Case, Roseau and St. David’s credit unions; over 54,000 members; branches include Castle Bruce and La Plaine; describes itself as the largest credit union in Dominica and the wider Eastern Caribbean; 15th anniversary awards on 8 November 2025. Caribbean Focus, 14 November 2025, https://www.caribbeanfocus.com/?p=35195 ; nominated by Dominica’s National Designated Authority for accreditation to the Green Climate Fund (cover date 6 April 2020), https://www.greenclimate.fund/document/accredited-entity-nomination-dominica-national-cooperative-credit-union
