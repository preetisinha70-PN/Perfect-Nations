## The seven standards

- **Food, 59%:** Not food insecure; 41% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=PE
- **Water, 49.4%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=PE
- **Shelter, 54.9%:** City dwellers outside slums; 45.1% live in slums. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=PE
- **Energy, 93%:** 93% cook with clean fuels and technology; 96.5% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=PE
- **Education, 55.6:** 55.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=PE
- **Health, 68:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~PER
- **Livelihoods, 63.8%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 36.2% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=PE

Furthest from reach: Water (50.6%). Of Peruvians lack safely managed drinking water.
