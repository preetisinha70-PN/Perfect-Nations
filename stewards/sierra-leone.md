## The seven standards

- **Food, 11.9%:** Not food insecure; 88.1% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=SL
- **Water, 10.9%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=SL
- **Shelter, 16.8%:** Safely managed sanitation; 49.3% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=SL
- **Energy, 2%:** 2% cook with clean fuels and technology; 39.2% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=SL
- **Education, 57.1:** 57.1% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=SL
- **Health, 48:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~SLE
- **Livelihoods, 43.2%:** Above the national poverty line; 56.8% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2018, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=SL

Furthest from reach: Energy (98%). Of Sierra Leoneans still cook without clean fuels.
