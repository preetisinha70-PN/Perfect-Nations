### The register and the record
- **Eurobond due 2032, $700 million:** Petra (Jordan News Agency), “Jordan issues Eurobonds in international financial markets at competitive interest rates of 5.75%”, 4 November 2025: seven years, fixed 5.75%, 1.75 points below the 7.5% five-year issue of 2023, bids above three times the amount; and “Gov’t completes January Eurobond redemption after early buyback, cuts funding cost”, 29 January 2026, https://www.petra.gov.jo/en/news/govt-completes-january-eurobond-redemption-after-early-buyback-cuts-funding-cost (opened 8 November 2026): $388 million of the $1 billion 6.125% notes of 2015 repaid early in November 2025, the remaining $612 million on 29 January 2026.
- **World Bank, 8 April 2025:** press release “Jordan and World Bank Deepen Partnership for Private Sector-Led Growth, Jobs and Innovation”, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/04/08/jordan-and-world-bank-deepen-partnership-for-private-sector-led-growth-jobs-and-innovation (opened 8 November 2026): four operations, US$1.1 billion in all ($400 million, $50 million, $400 million and $250 million; the sum is arithmetic).
- **Green Climate Fund FP288:** GCF project page, https://www.greenclimate.fund/portfolio/projects/fp288 (opened 8 November 2026): approved at B.43 on 30 October 2025; US$220 million loan and US$75 million grant; accredited entity IFC; total project value listed at US$6.3 billion.
- **DFC:** DFC investment story, https://www.dfc.gov/investment-story/investing-stability-middle-east-clean-water-jordan (profiled 2026, opened 8 November 2026): loan of up to $1 billion and political risk insurance of up to $800 million; a separate $300 million grant approved by the U.S. Department of State; water for at least 4 million people. The DFC Board notice for 16 September 2026 carries the public information summary for the National Carrier Project Company (estimated total project costs $6,566,118,670). MEED (24 September 2026) reports the approval.
- **IFC:** project 47924, https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/47924/aqaba-amman-water-desalination-conveyance-aawdc (opened 8 November 2026): approved 19 February 2026, pending signing; $375 million A loan and $32 million of risk management, indicative; total project cost about $6 billion; 30-year concession.
- **EIB:** project 20190712, https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20190712 (opened 8 November 2026): EUR 427 million signed (EUR 200 million on 8 December 2022, EUR 50 million on 28 November 2023, EUR 100 million on 3 December 2023, EUR 47 million on 14 October 2024, EUR 30 million on 24 July 2025; the EUR 50 million and EUR 47 million are EU investment grants); proposed EIB finance about EUR 874 million; total cost about EUR 6,114 million.
- **Arab Fund:** Arab Fund for Economic and Social Development, 18 February 2026, https://www.arabfund.org/?p=20100 (opened 8 November 2026): KWD 58 million (about US$189 million) for the electricity supply of the National Water Carrier.

- **The National Water Carrier:** the sources give different totals: about US$6.3 billion (GCF), about US$6 billion (IFC), about US$5.8 billion including financing (Jordan News Agency and MEED) and about US$6.57 billion (DFC Board notice). No total is shown on the page.
