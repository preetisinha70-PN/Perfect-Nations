## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026, unless marked.

- **Crumbs (Food):** an app that links restaurants, bakeries, shops and other food businesses with consumers, who buy surplus food in rescue packages at steep discounts, sometimes of 50% or more; businesses pay a small commission only on packages sold; €600,000 raised in May 2026 in a round led by AYMO Ventures; founders Antonio Matušan and Robert Bukvić. Total Croatia News, May 2026, https://total-croatia-news.com/news/croatian-startup-crumbs-investment/
- **ZEZ Sunce energy cooperative (Energy):** “Sun in Križevci”, Croatia’s first community-owned solar plant; €140,000 raised from 127 citizens in ten days; profits to a fund against energy poverty and for local social initiatives; SozialMarie prize, 2025. SozialMarie brochure 2025, https://www.sozialmarie.org/assets/media/sozialmarie_boschure_2025.pdf ; project page, https://sozialmarie.org/sl/projects/9832 ; a 200 kW plant on the roof of the City Market in Križevci, 25-year roof lease, 127 citizens (Eurofound, case HR-2025-8, updated 10 December 2025, https://static.eurofound.europa.eu/covid19db/cases/HR-2025-8_3707.html).
- **KLIK Energy Cooperative (Energy):** “KLIK – Cooperative-Led Energy and Climate OSS”, Koprivnica-Križevci County; a cooperative-run Energy-Climate Office that gives energy-poor households free, continuous and independent help to reach subsidies; SozialMarie prize winner, 2026 (ceremony 11 May 2026). SozialMarie brochure 2026, https://sozialmarie.org/assets/media/sozialmarie_boschure_2026.pdf
- **Humana Nova (Livelihood):** social cooperative that runs the textile value chain from collection to sales; 59 employees, of whom 34 have a disability and 20 belong to other vulnerable groups; SozialMarie prize winner, 2026. SozialMarie brochure 2026, https://sozialmarie.org/assets/media/sozialmarie_boschure_2026.pdf ; prize of the German-Croatian economy association, Zagreb, 8 November 2024 (Interreg Danube, 14 November 2024, https://interreg-danube.eu/projects/tex-dan/news/humana-nova-wins-prize-of-german-croatian-industry-and-economy-chamber); Čakovec, social enterprise profile of 18 February 2025 (European Social Fund Plus, https://european-social-fund-plus.ec.europa.eu/en/social-innovation-match/partner/social-cooperative-humana-nova).
- **ACT Grupa (Livelihood):** supports social entrepreneurs in Croatia, “fostering impact entrepreneurship since 2003”; country coordinator for Croatia of the SEED project for a national competence centre for social innovation, funded under the ESF+ Social Innovation+ Initiative. SEED EU project, page of 1 February 2025, https://www.seedeuproject.eu/partners/act-group/ ; co-publisher with Euclid Network of the Croatian Social Enterprise Monitor 2023–2024 (CRAFT policy brief, March 2026, https://craftaction.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CRAFT-Policy-Brief_Croatia.pdf).

- The tourism share dates from 2020, a pandemic year.
- The recovery plan’s grants: the nine grant instalments in the Council annex of 20 August 2026 add up to €5,602,544,628, while the Commission page states €5.8 billion; to reconcile at the next review.
- The coupon, yield and orders of the bond due 2031 rest on a news report of the Ministry of Finance announcement.
