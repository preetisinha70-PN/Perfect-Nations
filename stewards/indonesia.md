## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **285.7m citizens, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (285,721,236), read 30 October 2026. The same figure is on The People card.
- **50.7% of land under forest:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, forest area (% of land area), read 30 October 2026, as on The Forest card.
- **$2.1B World Bank package, June 2025:** World Bank, “World Bank Approves Investments to Boost Indonesia’s Economic Growth and Access to Clean Energy”, 16 June 2025, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/06/16/world-bank-approves-investments-to-boost-indonesia-s-economic-growth-and-access-to-clean-energy (opened 8 November 2026): two operations, US$2.128 billion in all: the Productive and Sustainable Investment Development Policy Loan (US$1.5 billion) and ISLE-2 (US$600 million IBRD loan, US$12 million of Livable Planet Fund grants and US$16 million of partner grants: US$10 million from the Green Climate Fund and US$6 million from the UK through ESMAP).
- **The lead, “more than $2.9 billion since 2025”:** arithmetic from the register: US$2.128 billion (June 2025), US$500 million (I-ENET, 25 September 2025) and US$350 million (Local Service Delivery Improvement Project, 18 December 2025) make US$2.978 billion, or US$2.962 billion without the partner grants. “Nine in ten cook with clean fuel”: World Bank, clean cooking 90% (2024), as on the Energy standard.

### The register and the record
- **Inaugural sovereign panda bonds, CNY 7 billion:** Directorate General of Budget Financing and Risk Management (DJPPR), Ministry of Finance, “Government Issues Inaugural CNY7 Billion Sovereign Panda Bonds”, https://djppr.kemenkeu.go.id/en/governmentissuesinauguralcny7billionsovereignpandabonds, and its settlement notice. The pages render only their titles to these reviews. The issue date (July 2026) is from ANTARA, “Indonesia to issue US$1 billion debut panda bond on July 23”. Tenors and coupons are to be read on a Ministry page.
- **Global Sukuk of June 2024:** Ministry of Finance press release, “Republic of Indonesia successfully priced a US$ 2.35 billion Global Sukuk” (opened 8 November 2026); the three tranches sum to US$2.35 billion (arithmetic).
- **Global Sukuk of June 2021:** Ministry of Finance press release of 3 June 2021, on the Bank Indonesia investor relations site (opened 8 November 2026); the three tranches sum to US$3 billion (arithmetic).
- **First sovereign green sukuk, February 2018, US$1.25 billion:** UNDP Indonesia, press release of 3 April 2018 (opened 8 November 2026): “the world’s first sovereign green sukuk (Islamic bond)”.
