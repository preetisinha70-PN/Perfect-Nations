## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **285.7m citizens, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (285,721,236), read 30 October 2026. The same figure is on The People card.
- **50.7% of land under forest:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, forest area (% of land area), read 30 October 2026, as on The Forest card.
- **$2.1B World Bank package, June 2025:** World Bank, “World Bank Approves Investments to Boost Indonesia’s Economic Growth and Access to Clean Energy”, 16 June 2025, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/06/16/world-bank-approves-investments-to-boost-indonesia-s-economic-growth-and-access-to-clean-energy (opened 12 November 2026): two operations, a US$2.128 billion blended finance package: the Productive and Sustainable Investment Development Policy Loan (US$1.5 billion) and ISLE-2 (US$600 million IBRD loan, US$12 million of Livable Planet Fund grants and US$16 million of partner grants).
- **The lead, “more than $4.4 billion since 2025”:** arithmetic from the register: US$2.128 billion (June 2025), US$500 million (I-ENET, September 2025), US$350 million (Local Service Delivery Improvement Project, 18 December 2025) and US$1.5 billion (First Growth and Jobs Development Policy Loan, approved 6 August 2026) make US$4.478 billion; US$4.462 billion without the partner grants. “Nine in ten cook with clean fuel”: World Bank, clean cooking 90% (2024), as on the Energy standard.

- **The Forest, 50.7% of land under forest:** as above.
- **The Water, 7,240 m³ of renewable fresh water per person:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, renewable internal freshwater resources per capita, read 30 October 2026.
- **The Power, 19% of electricity from renewables, 2023:** IRENA, Renewable Energy Statistical Profile, Indonesia, last updated 22 September 2025, https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Asia/Indonesia_Asia_RE_SP.pdf (opened 12 November 2026): renewable generation 64,721 GWh of 348,977 GWh in 2023 (18.55% by arithmetic; IRENA shows 19%). Hydro 24,590 GWh, bioenergy 22,475 GWh, geothermal 16,936 GWh, solar 713 GWh, wind 8 GWh. Renewables were 15% of total energy supply in 2022. The card read “15.5%, of energy from renewables, 2021” until this review.
