## The seven standards

- **Food, 71.8%:** Not food insecure; 28.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=MU
- **Water, 99.9%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=MU
- **Shelter, 51.3%:** City dwellers outside slums; 48.7% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=MU
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=MU
- **Education, 59.5:** 59.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2006, dated. World Bank, 2006, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=MU
- **Health, 75:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MUS
- **Livelihoods, 80.9%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 19.1% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2017, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=MU
