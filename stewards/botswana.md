## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **80% of exports from diamonds:** Associated Press, 25 February 2025, reporting the IMF (diamonds about 80% of exports and a quarter of GDP), https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/ap-top-news/2025/02/26/botswana-the-country-with-the-biggest-diamonds-gets-an-improved-10-year-deal-with-miner-de-beers. The IMF document behind it is still to be cited; the latest IMF edition is the 2025 Article IV consultation (8 December 2025), https://www.imf.org/en/publications/cr/issues/2025/12/08/botswana-2025-article-iv-consultation-press-release-staff-report-and-statement-by-the-572347, whose summary page does not state the share.
- **30% to 40%, State share of Debswana sales, 2025 deal:** Government of the Republic of Botswana and De Beers Group, “Government of the Republic of Botswana and De Beers Group Confirm Diamond Partnership for the Next Generation”, joint release distributed by Media OutReach Newswire, 27 February 2025, https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/mediaoutreach/2025/02/27/government-of-the-republic-of-botswana-and-de-beers-group-confirm-diamond-partnership-for-the-next-generation/363918 (opened 13 November 2026): the Okavango Diamond Company sells 30% of Debswana’s production for five years, then 40%; a 10-year sales agreement, extendable by five years at 50:50; licences extended from August 2029 to July 2054; BWP 1 billion (about $75 million) upfront to the Diamonds for Development Fund.
- **63.3% with safely managed drinking water:** the water standard on the page (WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme, 2024, World Bank indicator SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS).
- **Population of record:** 2,562,122 (World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025).

### The record in figures
- **33% public debt to GDP, 2025/26:** the Vice President and Minister of Finance in Parliament, Daily News (Government of Botswana), 13 August 2026, https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92706 (opened 13 November 2026): 19.7% in 2022/23; domestic debt P27.2 billion to P56.06 billion and external debt P16.4 billion to P27.88 billion from 2022/23 to 2025/26; the statutory ceiling raised from 40% to 60% of GDP in March 2026. The article labels the 33% “2025/2027”; its external debt figure is for 2025/26. Daily News of 31 March 2026 (https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90822) gave 34.16% as of February 2026, projected at about 39.42% with the new facilities.
