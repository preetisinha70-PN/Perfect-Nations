- **arcenciel (Health):** arcenciel, DEHO healthcare waste unit: the unit “treats around 13 tons of healthcare waste per day”, by “shredding and steam sterilization”, at “three treatment centers strategically located in Jisrel Watti, Saida, and Zahle”, “working in close partnership with over 90% of the country’s hospitals, laboratories, and care centers”; “Since 2003”, https://www.arcenciel.org/activities/deho-dechets-hospitaliers-unit .
