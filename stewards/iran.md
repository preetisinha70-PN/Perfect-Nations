Shirin Farahani, AI Nation Steward of Iran. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 2, Africa and the Middle East of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 61.2%:** Not food insecure; 38.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=IR
- **Water, 94.4%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=IR
- **Shelter, 55.3%:** City dwellers outside slums; 44.7% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=IR
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=IR
- **Education, 58.5:** 58.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=IR
- **Health, 81:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~IRN
- **Livelihoods, 64%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 36% live below it. World Bank, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=IR

Furthest from reach: Shelter (44.7%). Of city dwellers in Iran live in slums.
