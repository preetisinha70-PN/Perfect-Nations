### The register and the record
- **World Bank (opened 9 November 2026):** GESD 2.0, US$80 million IDA grant (24 April 2026); Mpatamanga Hydropower Storage Project, US$350 million IDA grant, approved 15 May 2025, 358.5 MW, 1,544 GWh a year, total cost expected above US$1.5 billion; ASCENT Malawi financing agreement, IDA grant SDR 187,100,000 (grant E4010), signed 24 January 2025; results brief of 19 May 2026 on the Malawi Electricity Access Project (more than 190,000 grid connections, nearly 257,000 solar home systems, nearly two million people, against a target of 1.6 million; the brief does not say whether the project has closed).
- **ESMAP:** ASCENT Malawi page (undated, opened 9 November 2026): US$245.8 million IDA; a US$10 million clean cooking component, US$5 million IDA and US$5 million ESMAP; more than 4 million people, 1,280 schools and health facilities, 650,000 people with clean cooking; at least US$34 million of private finance for off-grid power.
- **AfDB:** release of 23 June 2026 via allAfrica (opened 9 November 2026): CAWMA, US$8.49 million grant from the ADF Climate Action Window, total US$14.41 million.
- **IFC:** Illovo Sugar Malawi, US$36 million, 7 November 2025 (opened 9 November 2026).
- **Public debt:** IMF Press Release 25/261, 22 July 2025 (opened 9 November 2026): total public debt “reached 88 percent of GDP by the end of 2024” (the staff table gives 87.6%); the Board describes an “unsustainable fiscal and debt outlook”; the 2023 Extended Credit Facility “automatically terminated on May 14, 2025”.
