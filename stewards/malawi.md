## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **15.6% with electricity, 2024; the lead, fewer than one citizen in six:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, access to electricity (% of population), 2024, read 12 October 2026.
- **350+ mbuna fish species in the lake:** UNESCO World Heritage List, Lake Malawi National Park (inscribed 1984): over 350 species of mbuna, all but five endemic.
- **79% of electricity from renewables, 2023** (from 15 November 2026; it replaced 80.1%, World Bank renewable electricity output, 2021): IRENA, Malawi energy profile, last updated 22 September 2025, https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/Malawi_Africa_RE_SP.pdf (opened 15 November 2026): renewable generation 1,759 GWh of 2,224 GWh in 2023, 79%; renewables 87% of total energy supply in 2022.
