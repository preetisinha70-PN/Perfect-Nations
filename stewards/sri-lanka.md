## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **$2.4B, drawn under the IMF programme since 2023; the lead, has drawn $2.4 billion under its IMF programme since 2023:** IMF Press Release 26/172, 27 May 2026 (https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/05/27/pr26172-sri-lanka-imf-completes-combined-5th-and-6th-reviews-under-eff, opened 10 November 2026): completion of the combined fifth and sixth reviews gives access to SDR 508 million (about US$695 million), “bringing the total disbursements under the arrangement to SDR 1.778 billion (about US$2.4 billion)”. The separate Rapid Financing Instrument of December 2025 (about US$206 million) is not in this total. The label now says “under the IMF programme”. On Board completion of the seventh review the total would rise to SDR 2.032 billion, about US$2.7 billion (IMF Press Release 26/318). No Board completion of the seventh review was found on 16 November 2026, so US$2.4 billion stands.
- **92%, complete lower-secondary school; the lead, keeps nine in ten of its children in school:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, lower secondary completion rate (SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS); not read at source in this review (the World Bank data pages do not open). The UNESCO Institute for Statistics SDG 4 profile for Sri Lanka (https://download.uis.unesco.org/SDG4/SDG4-Profile-Sri%20Lanka.pdf) gives a survey-based lower-secondary completion rate of 97.6% (indicator 4.1.2; the year of the column is uncertain, 2021 or 2022), a different measure.
- **21.8m citizens, 2025; the lead, an island of 21.8 million:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (21,756,000, the `population` of the nation file); not re-read.
- **The lead, restructured its debts:** Ministry of Finance, international sovereign bond exchange settled on 20 December 2024 (the govt line of the register).
- **The capital heading, rebuilt its credit after default:** Ministry of Finance press release of 22 September 2026, “Fitch Ratings Upgrades Sri Lanka to ‘B-’ with a Stable Outlook” (title read on the Ministry’s 2026 press release listing, https://treasury.gov.lk/web/press-releases/section/2026, opened 16 November 2026; the release itself not opened).
