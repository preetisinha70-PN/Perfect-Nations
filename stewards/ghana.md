## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **6.0% growth, second quarter of 2026:** Bank of Ghana, Summary of Economic and Financial Data, September 2026, https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Summary-of-Economic-and-Financial-Data-September-2026.pdf (opened 8 November 2026): overall real GDP growth, including oil, 6.0% in the second quarter of 2026; non-oil growth 5.4%. - **$3B IMF programme, completed 2026:** IMF press release, “IMF Executive Board Completes the Sixth Review of Ghana’s Arrangement Under the Extended Credit Facility ...”, 27 July 2026, https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/07/27/pr25260-ghana-imf-exec-board-completes-6th-rev-arr-ecf-concludes-2026-aiv-consult-rev-req-36mo-pci (opened 8 November 2026): final disbursement of SDR 265.9 million (about US$371 million), bringing disbursements to about US$3 billion; the Board reviewed Ghana’s request for a 36-month non-financing Policy Coordination Instrument. - **$500M rural roads, World Bank 2026:** World Bank press release, 28 May 2026 (the register line, Ghana Market Access and Connectivity Project, P513708).
- **The lead, 35.1 million:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (35,064,272), read 30 October 2026.

### The register and the record
- **Total public debt, GH¢733.9 billion, 45.9% of GDP, July 2026 (43.9% in July 2025):** Bank of Ghana, Summary of Economic and Financial Data, September 2026 (as above; data from the Ministry of Finance, the Bank of Ghana and the Ghana Statistical Service, subject to revision).
