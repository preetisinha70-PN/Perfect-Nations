### The register and the record (each line links its own source)
- **Standard Gauge Railway, Lot 6 (Tabora–Kigoma):** African Development Bank release of 4 November 2026 (via allAfrica): a US$1.2 billion commercial facility from nine banks, co-arranged by Deutsche Bank and Société Générale; an AfDB partial credit guarantee of up to US$800 million, “the Bank’s largest to date”; US$720 million guaranteed and US$480 million on clean sovereign risk; US$405.5 million of the exposure placed with insurers; 411 km; part of a US$2.3 billion package for the section.
- **IMF Press Release 26/244, 10 July 2026:** final sixth and seventh reviews of the Extended Credit Facility and third and fourth reviews of the Resilience and Sustainability Facility; immediate disbursement of about US$443.9 million (about US$154.1 million ECF and US$289.7 million RSF); total ECF access about US$1,063 million; total RSF access about US$636.5 million, with three energy-sector reform measures not completed; gross nominal debt 49.1% of GDP in 2025/26 (estimate), 50.3% in 2024/25. The release prints the SDR total as “SDR 3249 million”; its parts sum to SDR 326.49 million (arithmetic), so the SDR total is not used. The ECF was approved in July 2022 for SDR 795.58 million (about US$1,046 million at approval); the RSF in June 2024 for SDR 596.7 million (150 percent of quota).
