## The seven standards

- **Food, 49.7%:** Not food insecure; 50.3% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=NE
- **Water, 53.4%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=NE
- **Shelter, 9.1%:** Safely managed sanitation; 70.4% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=NE
- **Energy, 6%:** 6% cook with clean fuels and technology; 21.3% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=NE
- **Education, 9.6:** 9.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=NE
- **Health, 39:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~NER
- **Livelihoods, 39.5%:** Above US$3.00 a day; 60.5% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.DDAY?locations=NE

Furthest from reach: Energy (94%). Of Nigeriens still cook without clean fuels.
