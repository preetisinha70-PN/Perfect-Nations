## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Fuller Center for Housing Armenia (Shelter):** long-term, interest-free housing loans to low-income families, repaid into a revolving fund; 309 families assisted over 14 years through the partnership with the ICRC. Spring 2024 newsletter, https://fullercenterarmenia.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Spring-Newsletter_2024.pdf ; model: https://fullercenterarmenia.org/?p=578
- **SkyAgro (Food):** drone crop spraying for small farms; around 700 farmers; 30% to 40% less chemicals and water. EVN Report, “AI, Drones, Blockchain and Agriculture”, 17 December 2025, https://evnreport.com/?p=43604
- **Teach For Armenia (Education):** young leaders from Armenia and the diaspora teach for two years in schools where their skills are needed; World Bank grant of up to US$2.6 million, letter agreement of 3 July 2024, for the Mental Health and Psychosocial Support in Armenian Schools Project. https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099070924091014388/pdf/P50470111ab4be0291ad391aa91fbf9d38a.pdf ; profile: https://careercenter.am/en/company/teach-for-armenia
- **Homeland Development Initiative Foundation, HDIF (Livelihood):** founded in 2013 in Yerevan; handicrafts made by women producer partners in Talin, Gyumri, Koghb, Berd, Vardenis, Vayk and Goghovit, sold to corporate and institutional clients; Arm.wool project. GlobalGiving, https://www.globalgiving.org/donate/25676/homeland-development-initiative-foundation/ (updates of September 2026) and https://www.globalgiving.org/projects/women-economic-empowerment/reports/?pageNo=1 ; WFTO Asia, https://wfto-asia.com/?p=2729
