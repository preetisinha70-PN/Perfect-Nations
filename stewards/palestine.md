- **Energy, 61.7%:** The Gaza grid has been down since October 2023 (OCHA), and 38.3% of Palestinians live in Gaza (PCBS, end of 2025). Before the war, 100% had electricity. OCHA and PCBS, 2026, https://www.ochaopt.org/content/humanitarian-situation-report-6-march-2026
- **Education, 91.2:** 91.2% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=PS
- **Health, 65:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~PSE

## The capital instruments register

Humanitarian

- **Flash Appeal for the Occupied Palestinian Territory 2026 ($4.06B):** OCHA, published 16 December 2025, https://www.ochaopt.org/content/flash-appeal-occupied-palestinian-territory-2026. At a glance, https://www.ochaopt.org/sites/default/files/OPT_Flash_Appeal_2026_At_a_Glance_EN.pdf: Gaza Strip “$3.72 billion”, West Bank “$351.2 million”; people in need “3.62 million”, targeted “2.97 million”; “a total of 201 UN agencies, international and national NGOs, and the International Red Cross/Red Crescent Movement”.
- **UNRWA occupied Palestinian territory Flash Appeal 2026 ($1.26B):** UNRWA, via ReliefWeb, https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/unrwa-occupied-palestinian-territory-flash-appeal-2026: the appeal seeks to mobilise “US$ 1.26 billion”.

Multilateral

- **Palestinian Fund for Reconstruction and Development, PFRD ($241M):** World Bank, West Bank and Gaza country page, https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/country/westbankandgaza: “As of September 2026, the PFRD has mobilized $241 million, with 92% of paid-in funds committed.” Donors: Canada, France, Iceland, Japan, Norway, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom. The PFRD page, https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/purse_multi_donor_trust_fund/overview: the Bank “restructured the pre-existing Palestinian Umbrella for Resilience Support to the Economy (PURSE) Multi Donor Trust Fund, establishing the PFRD”.
- **Replenishment of the World Bank program for the West Bank and Gaza ($300M):** World Bank country page: “On June 30, 2026, the Board of Executive Directors approved a $300 million replenishment for the program”. “The resources will support: (i) an integrated multi-sectoral early recovery program in Gaza, subject to Board endorsement of the resumption of activities”; (ii) “strategic investments in essential services and job creation in the West Bank”; (iii) “fiscal support to sustain reform momentum and economic resilience”.
- **Palestinian Emergency Financing Facility ($60M):** Emergency financing for basic services. https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P504704. The World Bank country page lists “Palestinian Emergency Financing Facility I, II, III ($167M)”.
- **Gaza Health Emergency Response Project ($10M):** Emergency health services in Gaza. https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P503036
- **Palestinian Partnership for Infrastructure Development MDTF, PID MDTF ($299M):** World Bank country page: “Since its launch in 2012, it has channeled $299 million” and co-financed 18 investment operations, eight of them active. Allocations listed include Integrated Solid Waste Management ($5 million, PID MDTF) and ASPIRE 3, energy ($5 million, PID MDTF).

Regional

- **Team Gaza Initiative (€883.6M):** European Commission, 13 July 2026, https://north-africa-middle-east-gulf.ec.europa.eu/news/eu-steps-international-support-palestinians-major-initiative-gazas-early-recovery-and-stronger-2026-07-13_en: “bringing together €883.6 million of financial contribution to support early recovery actions in various sectors”; participants: the governments of Spain, Denmark, the United Kingdom, Germany, Norway, Finland, Italy, the Netherlands, France, Japan, Switzerland, Sweden and Belgium, “together with the European Commission, the European Investment Bank and the World Bank”.
- **PEGASE support to the Palestinian Authority, 2026 and 2027 (€310M):** same page: “the €310 million that the European Commission committed through PEGASE for 2026 and 2027”; Member States “signed €41.7 million of new contribution agreements”; €3.8 billion channelled through PEGASE since its creation in 2008.
- **Solar Power Field Project ($22M):** Islamic Development Bank, 19 June 2026, https://www.isdb.org/news/isdb-signs-development-financing-agreements-with-member-countries-during-the-2026-isdb-group-annual-meetings-in-baku: “signed a US$22 million agreement for the Solar Power Field Project”. Union of OIC News Agencies (UNA), 19 June 2026, https://una-oic.org/en/economy/2026/06/19/Palestine-and-the-Islamic-Development-Bank-signed/: “two solar power plants with a total capacity of 20 megawatts”, “energy storage systems with a capacity of 55 megawatt-hours”, “The total cost of the project is $37 million”, including “a soft loan of $12 million” from the Islamic Development Bank.
- **EU Palestine facility for local banks ($395M):** European Investment Bank, 8 June 2026, https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2026-198-eu-implements-palestine-facility-with-usd395-million-for-local-banks-and-eur2-1-million-in-technical-assistance: “sub-MSME loan agreements between the Palestine Monetary Authority (PMA) and Bank of Palestine for $150 million”; Quds Bank $100 million, Palestine Investment Bank $70 million, The National Bank $50 million, Cairo Amman Bank $25 million; “The package also includes the deployment of the remaining €2.1 million in technical assistance.”
- **Multiannual and Comprehensive Programme to support Palestine’s Recovery and Resilience (€1.6B):** European Commission, 13 July 2026: “the Commission adopted a €1.6 billion Multiannual and Comprehensive Programme”, in April 2025, for the next three years.
- **Urgent Program to Support the Palestinian People, twenty-third phase (KD 12.06M):** Arab Fund for Economic and Social Development, Annual Report 2025, https://www.arabfund.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026_06_28_08_45_28_Arab-Fund_2025-EN-FINAL.pdf: “Twenty Third Phase of the Urgent Program to Support the Palestinian People”, KD 12,063,000, approved at the April 2025 Board of Governors meeting; 124 proposed projects; shares: rural development 38.7%, associations and civil institutions 21.0%, education 16.6%, health 15.6%, economic empowerment 6.4%, technical services 1.7%.

Bilateral

- **French support for rural communities in Area C (€5M):** Arab News, 17 November 2026, https://www.arabnews.pk/middle-east/france-commits-55m-to-boost-infrastructure-in-palestinian-rural-communities-3007310: “France has pledged €5 million ($5.5 million)”; signed by the French consul general in Jerusalem, the director of AFD in Jerusalem, the Minister of Finance and the Minister of Local Government; water, sanitation, electricity and health services, farming and crafts near Tubas and Salfit; about 22,000 people by early 2027.

## The enterprises

- **Canaan Palestine (Food):** Canaan Palestine press release, 3 October 2025, https://www.finanzen.net/nachricht/aktien/20-years-strong-canaan-palestine-honors-family-farmers-in-annual-olive-harvest-tradition-14941001: “over 2,400 family farms across the West Bank”; premiums above the average market rate; sold in over 600 US retailers; mission “to sustain the livelihoods of Palestine’s indigenous farming communities”. Self-reported.
- **Flowless (Water):** Philanthropy Age, 11 November 2024, https://www.philanthropyage.org/articles/palestinian-start-ups-shine-in-global-competition/: “a Ramallah-based social enterprise using technology to improve water management”; finalist of the second Continuum Spark Awards.
