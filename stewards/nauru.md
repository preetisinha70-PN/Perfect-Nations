## The seven standards

- **Food, 70.1%:** Not food insecure; 29.9% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=NR
- **Water, 18.7%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=NR
- **Shelter, 33.2%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.6% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=NR
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=NR
- **Education, 97.9:** 97.9% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2025, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=NR
- **Health, 62:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~NRU
- **Livelihoods, 31.5%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 68.5% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2012, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=NR

Furthest from reach: Water (81.3%). Of Nauruans lack safely managed drinking water.

## The record and Capital already committed

- **A$140M:** Committed by Australia over five years under the Nauru-Australia Treaty: A$100 million of budget support and A$40 million for policing and security. Parliament of Australia, 2025, https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Joint/Treaties/Nauru-AusTreaty/Media_Releases
- **12.2%:** General government gross debt, share of GDP, 2025. IMF, WEO Oct 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/GGXWDG_NGDP@WEO/NRU
- **2.1%:** Real GDP growth projected for 2026. IMF, WEO Oct 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/NGDP_RPCH@WEO/NRU
- **Nauru-Australia Treaty (A$140M):** Budget support, policing and security, and banking services through the Commonwealth Bank of Australia. https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Joint/Treaties/Nauru-AusTreaty/Media_Releases
