## The seven standards

Not re-read at source on 19 November 2026.

- **Education, 97.9:** 97.9% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2025, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=NR
- **Health, 62:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~NRU

## The register (10 lines)

Sources opened on 19 November 2026.

- **Government of Nauru, Intergenerational Trust Fund (A$412M, held):** “As of May 2025, the NTF had asset holdings of AU$412 million”; Nauru contributed AU$22.6 million in FY2025 (about 8.5% of GDP); Taiwan Province of China is exiting the fund, with a present value of “about AU$40 million”. IMF staff report, 2025 Article IV, https://meetings.imf.org/-/media/Files/Publications/CR/2025/English/1nruea2025001-source-pdf.ashx . Established on 6 November 2015; Australia and New Zealand have publicly available memoranda with Nauru and the memorandum with Taiwan is not released; “the Fund will begin Distributions when the Committee determines that the Fund has reached a Current Value”. International Forum of Sovereign Wealth Funds, Santiago Principles self-assessment 2025, https://www.ifswf.org/assessment/ntf-2025 . The IMF Executive Board concluded the 2025 Article IV consultation on 17 September 2025 and recommended “sustained contributions to the Intergenerational Trust Fund” (press release 25/306, https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2025/09/19/pr-25306-republic-of-nauru-imf-executive-board-concludes-2025-article-iv-consultation).
- **Government of Nauru, Development Fund Projection 2025–26 (A$157.7M, budgeted):** speech of the President and Minister for Finance to Parliament, 29 August 2025: “Nauru’s development partners have pledged 157.7 million dollars”; “This includes direct budget support of 50 million dollars, and in-kind assistance valued at 107.2 million dollars”; “Australia’s allocation for the financial year is 44.9 million dollars”, with “20 million dollars in Budget Support under the NauruAustralia treaty”; Japan 31.2 million (East Micronesia undersea cable, port equipment, a police patrol boat, heavy machinery); ADB 18.9 million, of which “2.6 million dollars towards the Nauru Urban Sustainable Development Project” and “The Solar Project will be allocated 1.3 million dollars”; United Nations 11.9 million, with “6.5 million dollars for the HIV/TB Programme”; “New Zealand will provide support totalling 4.8 million dollars.” https://naurufinance.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Draft-Dev-Fund-Speech-FY25-26_FINAL.pdf . Nauru’s currency is the Australian dollar.
- **Green Climate Fund, Sustainable and Climate Resilient Connectivity for Nauru, FP052 ($26.91M, under implementation):** “FP052: Sustainable and Climate Resilient Connectivity for Nauru”; GCF financing “USD 26,910,000”; total project value “$ 65.2 M”; accredited entity the Asian Development Bank; approved “02 Oct 2017”; status “Under implementation”; Nauru “depends significantly on its port for supplies of food, energy and most other essentials”. https://www.greenclimate.fund/project/fp052 . ADB: “Key infrastructure like the new wharf, fuel pipeline, and southern container yard is operational”, https://www.adb.org/where-we-work/naoero/overview .
- **Asian Development Bank, grant to strengthen the public finances ($8.56M, approved):** ADB’s Naoero page lists the news release “ADB Increases Naoero Support as Middle East Conflict Drives Up Costs”, 17 November 2026: “ADB has approved an $8.56 million grant to help Naoero strengthen its public finances,” “including $3.56 million in additional support to help the Pacific island nation cope with rising fuel,” “shipping, and import costs caused by the Middle East conflict.” https://www.adb.org/where-we-work/naoero/overview . The release itself (https://www.adb.org/news/adb-increases-naoero-support-middle-east-conflict-drives-costs) did not open; the programme name and terms are to be read there.
- **Asian Development Bank, Sustainable and Resilient Urban Development Project ($37.5M, effective):** approval of “a $37.5 million grant”, “YAREN, NAURU (27 September 2024)”; Asian Development Fund grant; the Government of Nauru contributes $2.3 million; Nauru’s first reticulated water supply of tanks, pumps and pipes, to serve 55% of the population, more access to desalinated water, improved sanitation, an organic waste composting facility and better materials recovery and recycling. https://www.adb.org/news/adb-helps-nauru-transformative-investment-resilient-urban-services . ADB fact sheet on Naoero, 2025 edition (commitments to 31 December 2025): “ADB has committed a $37.5 million grant from the Asian Development Fund (ADF) in 2024”, https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/27748/nao-2025.pdf . Semestral environmental and social monitoring report for July to December 2025 (project 54377-002, 0970-NAU): grant effectiveness on 4 December 2025; “No civil works contracts have been awarded during this reporting period”; the water supply works package CW-01 expected to be awarded in the next period. https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/project-documents/54377/54377-002-esmr-1166101-en.pdf
- **Asian Development Bank, Solar Power Development Project ($22M, active):** “An ADB-supported $22 million renewable energy project is in the commissioning phase,” “aiming to energize a 6-megawatt solar farm spanning 4 hectares on Naoero’s elevated plateau” (https://www.adb.org/where-we-work/naoero/overview). Environmental and social monitoring report, March 2026, for July to December 2025, “Nauru: Solar Power Development Project”, Grant 0664, “financed by the Government of Nauru (the government) and the Asian Development Bank (ADB)”; “solar arrays were also energised and integrated to the power grid on 1 December 2025”; the plant is “operating at a reduced generation capacity” until the control system and the battery are online. https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/project-documents/49450/49450-009-esmr-en_43.pdf .
