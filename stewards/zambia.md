## The hero

- **$3B, Eurobonds restructured, 2024; the lead, restructured $3 billion of bonds in 2024:** The Republic of Zambia, represented by the Ministry of Finance, Final Results Announcement, 4 June 2024 (https://dev.emta.org/media/1ozdf0kx/republic_of_zambia_results_announcement.pdf, opened 11 November 2026): the notes restructured were “U.S.$750,000,000 5.375 per cent. Notes due 2022”, “U.S.$1,000,000,000 8.500 per cent. Notes due 2024” and “U.S.$1,250,000,000 8.970 per cent. Notes due 2027”; $3 billion is their sum (arithmetic). - **$1.47B, the new Extended Credit Facility, agreed at staff level:** IMF Press Release 26/331, 9 October 2026 (https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/10/09/pr26331-zambia-new-ecf, re-opened 22 November 2026): a “new 36-month arrangement under the Extended Credit Facility (ECF)” that “would have access of 1,472 million USD (SDR 1,076 million)”, “subject to approval by the IMF Executive Board” and “contingent on the implementation of agreed prior actions”; inflation “declined to 6.1 percent in September”; “Gross international reserves reached US$6.1 billion in September”; “Real GDP is projected to grow by 5.6 percent in 2026.” No Executive Board approval was found on 22 November 2026.
- **$3B, Eurobonds restructured in 2024:** see the hero.
- **$1.7B and 101.8%, the completed Extended Credit Facility and public debt:** IMF Press Release 26/024, 27 January 2026 (https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/01/27/pr-26024-zambia-imf-executive-board-completes-sixth-review-under-the-extended-credit-facility, re-opened 22 November 2026): disbursement of “SDR 138.9 million (about US$190 million)”; total disbursements “SDR 1,271.66 million (about US$1.7 billion)”; “Five bilateral agreements with official creditors have been signed.”; “Progress with commercial creditors is advancing.”; “Debt is assessed as sustainable but remains at high risk of overall and external debt distress.”; public debt 133.4% of GDP in 2023, 101.8% in 2024 (estimate) and 87.6% in 2025 (projection).
