## The seven standards

- **Food, 90.5%:** Not food insecure; 9.5% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=RS
- **Water, 75%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=RS
- **Shelter, 24.1%:** Safely managed sanitation; 1.4% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=RS
- **Energy, 89%:** 89% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=RS
- **Education, 91.2:** 91.2% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=RS
- **Health, 73:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~SRB
- **Livelihoods, 80.3%:** Above the national poverty line; 19.7% live below it. World Bank, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=RS

Furthest from reach: Shelter (75.9%). Of people in Serbia lack safely managed sanitation.
