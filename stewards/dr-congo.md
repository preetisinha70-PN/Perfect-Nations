## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **74% of the world’s cobalt, 2025:** U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026, Cobalt, https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf (opened 7 November 2026): Congo (Kinshasa) mine production 230,000 t (estimated) of 310,000 t world production in 2025; reserves 6,000,000 t of 12,000,000 t. The 74% is arithmetic (230,000 / 310,000 = 74.2%).
- **42,000 MW hydropower potential at Inga:** World Bank, Questions and answers on World Bank support for the Democratic Republic of Congo’s Grand Inga development program, updated 3 June 2025, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/factsheet/2024/10/09/questions-and-answers-on-world-bank-support-for-the-democratic-republic-of-congos-afe-drc-grand-inga-development-program (opened 14 November 2026): “hydropower potential (around 42,000 MW)”; Inga 3 “could generate between 3,000 and 11,000 MW depending on the design approach”. The World Bank release of 3 June 2025 gives Inga 3 as “potentially generating between 2 to 11 GW of power”.
- **$1.25B debut Eurobond, April 2026:** the record in figures and the register (Rawbank, Kinshasa, 9 April 2026, https://rawbank.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Rawbank-announces-the-successful-issuance-of-the-DRCs-first-ever-eurobond-for-a-total-amount-of-USD-125-billion.pdf, opened 14 November 2026: USD 1.25 billion; 5-year (2032) and 10-year (2037) tranches “with respective yields of 8,75 % et 9,50 %”; “strong interest from international investors”, “exceeding USD 5 billion across the combined 5-year and 10-year maturities”; Rawbank acted as Joint Global Coordinator alongside Citigroup and as Joint Bookrunner).
- **The lead (112.8 million citizens; electricity for fewer than one in four):** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (112,832,473), read 30 October 2026; electricity access 22.5% (World Bank, 2024), as on the Energy standard. Population of record: 112,832,473.

### The record in figures
- **18% public debt, 2025:** IMF Country Report No. 26/246, Staff Report for the 2026 Article IV Consultation, Third Review under the Extended Credit Facility Arrangement and Second Review under the Resilience and Sustainability Facility Arrangement (published 8 September 2026; staff report completed 12 June 2026), paragraph 9: “Public debt remained contained in 2025.” “The appreciation of the CDF helped reduce the debt-to-GDP ratio to about 18 percent of GDP”; “The DRC remains at moderate risk of external and overall debt distress, with some space to absorb shocks (2026 Debt Sustainability Analysis (DSA)).” https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1codea2026002.pdf (opened 14 November 2026). The earlier staff report (IMF Country Report No. 26/2, January 2026) gave 20.9 percent at end-2024 and about 18.1 percent expected at end-2025, https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1codea2026001-source-pdf.pdf (opened 14 November 2026).
- **$8.8B international reserves, end-March 2026:** IMF Press Release 26/139, 6 May 2026: “International reserves: reached US$ 8.8 billion at end-March 2026”, “still slightly below the conventional adequacy benchmark of three months of imports” (opened 14 November 2026).
- **ECF, third review:** IMF press release of 26 June 2026 (pr26227): disbursement of SDR 190.4 million (about US$258.2 million), “bringing the aggregate ECF disbursement to date to 761.3 million SDR (about US$ 1,032.4 million)”; RSF disbursement of SDR 66.6 million (about US$90.3 million); about US$348.5 million in all (opened 14 November 2026).
