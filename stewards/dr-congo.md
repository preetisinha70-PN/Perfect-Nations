## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **74% of the world’s cobalt, 2025:** U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026, Cobalt, https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf (opened 7 November 2026): Congo (Kinshasa) mine production 230,000 t (estimated) of 310,000 t world production in 2025; reserves 6,000,000 t of 12,000,000 t. The 74% is arithmetic (230,000 / 310,000 = 74.2%).
- **42,000 MW hydropower potential at Inga:** World Bank, Questions and answers on World Bank support for the Grand Inga development program, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/factsheet/2024/10/09/questions-and-answers-on-world-bank-support-for-the-democratic-republic-of-congos-afe-drc-grand-inga-development-program: around 42,000 MW; Inga 3 at 3,000 to 11,000 MW depending on its design.
- **$1.25B debut Eurobond, April 2026:** the record in figures and the register (Rawbank, 9 April 2026, https://rawbank.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Rawbank-announces-the-successful-issuance-of-the-DRCs-first-ever-eurobond-for-a-total-amount-of-USD-125-billion.pdf, opened 7 November 2026: USD 1.25 billion, 2032 at 8.75% and 2037 at 9.50%, investor interest above USD 5 billion).
- **The lead (112.8 million citizens; electricity for fewer than one in four):** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (112,832,473), read 30 October 2026; electricity access 22.5% (World Bank, 2024), as on the Energy standard.
