## The seven standards

- **Food, 82.4%:** Not food insecure; 17.6% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=DZ
- **Water, 69%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=DZ
- **Shelter, 62.5%:** Safely managed sanitation; 13.3% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=DZ
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=DZ
- **Education, 32.1:** 32.1% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2007, dated. World Bank, 2007, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=DZ
- **Health, 70:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~DZA
- **Livelihoods, 58.2%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 41.8% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2011, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=DZ

Furthest from reach: Education (67.9%). Of children in Algeria cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school, on 2007 data.
