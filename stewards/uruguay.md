## The seven standards

- **Food, 83.8%:** Not food insecure; 16.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=UY
- **Water, 99.6%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=UY
- **Shelter, 98.7%:** City dwellers outside slums; 1.3% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=UY
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=UY
- **Education, 56.4:** 56.4% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=UY
- **Health, 85:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~URY
- **Livelihoods, 82.7%:** Above the national poverty line; 17.3% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=UY
