## The enterprises

Two verified (opened 19 November 2026).

- **Kindling Kiribati (Livelihood):** a non-profit “established in 2018” that supports women-led small businesses through a ten-lesson business training programme and microloans (UNCDF, 25 August 2024, https://www.uncdf.org/article/8776/empowering-women-entrepreneurs-kindling-kiribatis-incubate-better-programme). Loans “averaging around $350 AUD”; groups “meet weekly for loan repayment”; “Full refund of interest if all payments are made on time”; “Attend 10 entrepreneurship classes to qualify for a loan” (https://kindlingkiribati.org/programs/?tab=Microfinance). 2025: 195 loans, “$53,913.60” AUD; “maintain a strong loan repayment rate of 95.55%”; “160 female graduates across South Tarawa and Abemama”; registered with the Ministry of Women, Youth, Sport and Social Affairs (Annual report 2025, https://kindlingkiribati.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2025-Annual-Report.pdf). “funded over 350 small business loans” since summer 2019 (https://kindlingkiribati.org/). The report names grants, partnerships and crowdsourcing as its funding.
- **Teitoiningaina (Livelihood):** “Teitoiningaina, a Catholic Women’s Organization in Kiribati”, provides “training in handicraft skills”; “The organization operates a facility where women from around Kiribati, particularly Catholic women,” “can supply their handicraft products, ensuring a steady source of income and preserving traditional crafts.” Government of Kiribati, Ministry of Women, Youth, Sports and Social Affairs, Beijing Platform for Action +30 Kiribati progress report (2020-2024), https://www.unwomen.org/sites/default/files/2024-09/b30_report_kiribati_en.pdf
