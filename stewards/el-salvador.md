Sources opened on 18 November 2026, unless marked.

## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **FUSAL, Fundación Salvadoreña para la Salud y el Desarrollo Humano (Education):** early-childhood work since 2004, education-quality and reading programmes; “91% de la niñez atendida mejora sus competencias de lectura, comprensión y fluidez”; more than 600 volunteers; 223 communities in the 14 departments; more than 100,000 people a year. El Diario de Hoy, 2 October 2026, https://www.eldiariodehoy.com/gente-y-empresa/fusal-educacion-primera-infancia/95252/2026/
- **Glasswing International (Education):** schools as community centres; after-school clubs run by volunteer mentors; Girls Clubs; a FabLab; SanaMente for teachers, police and health workers, at Hospital Saldaña; working with a school in Las Palmas, San Salvador, since 2010. Glasswing International, “Highlights from Our El Salvador Learning Journey”, 3 June 2025, https://glasswing.org/?p=3653 ; Glasswing El Salvador “implements a range of education, health, employability & entrepreneurship, and public space programs”, https://glasswing.org/glasswing-el-salvador/ ; schools sponsored in El Salvador visited by donors in 2025 (Snapshot 2025, https://glasswing.org/es/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Snapshot-2025_ES.pdf).
- **Kodigo (Education):** a technology academy founded in 2017; Lesson Lab and KodiTutor, an AI tutor available 24 hours; more than 1,780 people in its AI programmes by October 2026, in 70 cohorts. Estrategia & Negocios, 31 October 2026, https://www.revistaeyn.com/empresasymanagement/kodigo-lanza-herramientas-de-ia-y-bootcamps-para-capacitar-talento-en-el-salvador-BM32496351 ; programming bootcamp with IDB Lab for 1,500 people in vulnerable situations: “Participants incur no cost until they obtain, after their training, a job in the technology sector” (Dinero, undated, https://dinero.com.sv/en/technology/item/2042-bid-lab-and-kodigo-graduate-the-first-class-of-the-programming-bootcamp-in-el-salvador.html).
- **FUNDASAL, Fundación Salvadoreña de Desarrollo y Vivienda Mínima (Shelter):** a non-profit founded in 1968; mutual aid of about 24 hours a week per family, which it says cuts costs by 55%; World Habitat Awards 2025 for “Fortalecimiento de capacidades para comunidades rurales productivas y resilientes en El Salvador”, in 12 rural communities and two rural housing cooperatives; 640 earth houses built or improved for 2,708 people; living conditions and access to food improved for 50,484 people. IDEASS, https://www.ideassonline.org/public/pdf/FUNDASALWinnerHabitat2025Award-ESP.pdf
- **Hábitat para la Humanidad El Salvador (Shelter):** in El Salvador since 1992; housing programme with credit, credit-subsidy and subsidy; “Individuos servidos en el AF2025: 106 322”, of whom 17,100 through professional services, 14,645 through incremental building and 2,170 through new homes. Habitat for Humanity, El Salvador fact sheet FY2025, https://www.habitat.org/sites/default/files/documents/ESP_El%20Salvador%20AF25.pdf
- **SAC Optima (Livelihood):** lending since 2009; a savings and credit society since 2024, under the supervision of the Superintendency of the Financial System; 16 branches, about 20,000 clients, mostly MSMEs and informal sector workers; BIO loan of US$5 million (2021) and technical assistance (October 2025). BIO, https://www.bio-invest.be/en/investments/optima-servicios-financieros-s-a-de-c-v-2
- **Apoyo Integral (Livelihood):** a regulated non-bank financial institution since 2009 serving the upper micro segment and small enterprises; “55% of credit clients have no credit history in the regulated financial system”; 55% of the portfolio in rural areas; 26 branches; BIO loan of US$10 million (October 2020). BIO, https://www.bio-invest.be/en/investments/sociedad-de-ahorro-y-cr%C3%A9dito-apoyo-integral ; COFIDES technical assistance of up to €332,255 for digital services for rural micro-enterprises and credit products for women entrepreneurs (Corresponsables, 1 October 2024, https://www.corresponsables.com/actualidad/cofides-apoyara-banco-apoyo-integral-el-salvador-promover-digitalizacion).
