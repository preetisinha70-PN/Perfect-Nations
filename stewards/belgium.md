## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **microStart (Livelihood):** microcredits with support for entrepreneurs facing financial and social inequality; 538 microcredits in 2025, about €4,963,262 lent (average €9,225), offices in Brussels, Ghent, Antwerp, Liège and Hainaut; an SC-ES cooperative, created in 2011 by BNP Paribas Fortis, Adie and the European Investment Fund. microStart, Rapport annuel 2025, https://microstart.be/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RapAn2025_FR.pdf
- **DUO for a JOB (Livelihood):** intergenerational mentoring of young job seekers with a migrant background; “9032 duos” created in Belgium since 2013; 1,500 active mentors; offices in Antwerp, Brussels, Hainaut, Liège and East Flanders. DUO for a JOB, https://duoforajob.be/en/get-involved/become-a-partner ; Brussels branch, “4932 duos created and supported by our team”, https://www.duoforajob.be/en/branches/brussels
- **Epi St Gilles (Food):** social grocery in Saint-Gilles; its statutes provide for “la vente à prix réduits à un public précarisé des produits d’alimentation d’entretien et d’hygiène de qualité”; listed among food aid organisations “bénéficiant de l’agrément” as a social enterprise in the Brussels-Capital Region. Fédération des Services Sociaux, Solidarité alimentaire : Clés pour réussir mon projet, May 2025, https://www.fdss.be/wp-content/uploads/2025_CAA_PUB_repis_fiche_recon_ent-soc-bxl_V04.pdf
- **Community Land Trust Brussels (Shelter):** the trust owns the land and residents own their homes, with a limited resale price; “Reaching 200 affordable housings inhabited on a CLTB land” (2025). European Urban Initiative, Brussels case study, November 2025, https://www.urban-initiative.eu/sites/default/files/2025-11/Brussels_Case%20study.pdf ; the Brussels Region gives homes on average 40% below the private market and seven projects with 85 homes being built (be.brussels, 24 April 2023, https://be.brussels/en/housing/community-land-trust-brussels-cltb-innovative-housing-model).
- **Ecopower (Energy):** citizen energy cooperative; 73,026 members, 60,000 electricity customers, 29 wind turbines of 68 MW and 22 MWp of solar; the POWER UP project with the city of Eeklo against energy poverty, completed in 2025. Ecopower, Jaarverslag 2026 (financial year 2025), https://cdn.nimbu.io/s/4tn7vz5/channelentries/0zfyrgb/files/1775829166940/20260410_jaarverslag_2026_boekjaar_2025.pdf
- **BeCode (Education):** inclusive coding bootcamps for job seekers since 2017, free of charge, seven months, in Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, Charleroi and Liège. EU Digital Skills and Jobs Platform, updated 22 May 2026, https://digital-skills-jobs.europa.eu/en/opportunities/training/becode-bootcamps-junior-web-developer-ai-devsecops ; BeCode’s own site gives four campuses and “80% d’insertion”, https://becode.org
