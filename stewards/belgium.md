## The seven standards

- **Food, 91.9%:** Not food insecure; 8.1% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=BE
- **Water, 99.6%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=BE
- **Shelter, 94.9%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=BE
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=BE
- **Education, 91.5:** 91.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=BE
- **Health, 86:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~BEL
- **Livelihoods, 87.7%:** Above the national poverty line; 12.3% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=BE

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (12.3%). Of Belgians live below the national poverty line.
