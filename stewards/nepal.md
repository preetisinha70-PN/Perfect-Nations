## The seven standards

- **Food, 63.4%:** Not food insecure; 36.6% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=NP
- **Water, 16.5%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=NP
- **Shelter, 53.4%:** Safely managed sanitation; 40.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=NP
- **Energy, 37.2:** About 372 kWh of electricity a person a year, against the Living Line of 1,000; 45% cook with clean fuels (2024). Ember, 2024, https://ember-energy.org/data/yearly-electricity-data/

- **Health, 58:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~NPL

## The enterprises

- **Gham Power (Food):** WE4F innovator profile, https://we4f.org/innovators/gham-power-nepal-private-limited (profile of 2021, last updated January 2025): solar water pumps, a digital platform (Off Grid Bazaar), farm advice and financing for smallholders in the Southern Terai; “The microfinance partners provide collateral-free, easy financing for the systems”; pilots showed “up to 80% increase in income”. Global Voices, 4 November 2025, credits Gham Power for a photograph of farmers irrigating with solar water pumps (https://globalvoices.org/2025/11/04/here-comes-the-sun-exploring-solar-potential-in-nepal/).
- **SmartPaani (Water):** Dopper, Nepal mission page, https://dopper.com/mission/nepal (current page, with entries from 2026): “Kathmandu-based social enterprise SmartPaani”, a partner since 2018; “104 schools across Nepal are fitted with a fully functional, sustainable, and resilient safe water supply.” ICIMOD adaptation solutions, 1 December 2022 (https://www.icimod.org/adaptation-solutions/a-fully-circular-range-of-water-conservation-and-treatment-solutions/): 4,000+ systems installed, 310+ schools, 30 districts.
- **Diyalo Technologies (Water):** Ratopati, 22 March 2026, https://english.ratopati.com/story/54981: billing, accounting and website services to 852 drinking water consumer committees; the article reports complaints from committees about its fees, and the company says “We charge based on the services provided”. Reach: GSMA Mobile for Development, https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/blog/erp-and-iot-solutions-for-water-providers-diyalo-in-nepal/: ERP software for water utilities, from 175 to over 800 water providers, “serving approximately 1.2 million households, or ~5 million people”; GSMA Innovation Fund grantee. Ratopati, 22 March 2026 (https://english.ratopati.com/story/54981): provides billing, accounting and website services to 852 drinking water consumer committees; some committees have complained about its fees, which the company says follow its agreements and services.
- **Build Up Nepal (Shelter):** Deseret News, 26 September 2025, https://www.deseret.com/education/2025/09/26/all-about-empowering-university-of-utah-awards-build-up-nepal-nonprofit-250k-prize/: winner of the $250,000 Wilkes Climate Launch Prize 2025; 12,000 houses built with its bricks; about 220 active enterprises; local entrepreneurs buy machines from it and build disaster-resilient homes.
- **Chhaya Nepal (Health):** The Annapurna Express, 13 November 2025, https://theannapurnaexpress.com/story/59955: telemedicine with the Karnali Province Ministry of Social Development since 8 April 2025, linking Mugu, Humla, Dolpa, Jajarkot and Kalikot with specialists at Karnali Province Hospital, Surkhet; 1,407 patients treated. Quote: “Telemedicine service has revived both life and hope in remote districts of Karnali”.
