## The enterprises

Opened 18 and 19 November 2026.

- **Big Brother Mouse (Education):** “not-for-profit, Lao-owned project, with a Lao staff”; “Now we have 12 schools with 4200 students” (https://www.bigbrothermouse.com/laos/about.html). Little Elephant school: “First opened: September 2023 as preschool”; “Primary school added: September 2025”; “Enrollment: 388 children”; “Our modest tuition fees cover basic expenses such as teacher salaries.” https://www.bigbrothermouse.com/education/school_littleelephant.html . Books in the Lao language for children, sold to travellers to give out in villages (Uncornered Market, updated 26 April 2024, https://www.uncorneredmarket.com/big-brother-mouse/).
- **Ock Pop Tok (Livelihood):** “our artisan social enterprise, based in Luang Prabang, Laos”; “Created by women, run by women for the women of Laos”; “more than 500 artisans throughout Laos” (https://www.ockpoptok.com, page modified 26 June 2026). “Founded in 2000”; shops, webshop, classes, the Living Crafts Centre with the Silk Road Café, and the Mekong Villa; “founded on the principles of fair trade” (https://www.ockpoptok.com/about/our-story/).
- **Women With Disability Association, WWDA (Livelihood):** “The Women With Disability Association (WWDA) is leading an initiative”, “founded by Chanhpheng Sivila”; “Since its inception in 2001”; an association since 2021; weaving, pottery, ceramics and paper crafts, “all of which are sold to foreigners”; “nearly 30 disabled students from Oudomxay and Bokeo Provinces are enrolled at the center”. Tourism Laos, 15 April 2025, https://www.tourismlaos.org/2025/04/15/women-with-disabilities-association-brings-lao-handicrafts-to-the-global-stage/
- **Xon Phao (Livelihood):** “a Lao social enterprise supporting people with disabilities”, Vientiane, crediting the XonPhao Shop (Nanyang Technological University, WeeVolunteer, 25 December 2025, https://www.weeworks.wkwsci.ntu.edu.sg/WeeVolunteer/stories/2024/beyond-the-threads.html). The Xon Phao Disabled People’s Center was “created in 2017”; international orders were a reliable income before the pandemic (United Nations in Lao PDR, https://laopdr.un.org/en/107047-resilient-throughout-covid-19-story-xon-phao-small-disabled-peoples-enterprise-big). Handicraft gifts based on traditional Laotian weaving; “About 30 people work there today” (Government of Japan, Kizuna, spring 2021, https://Www.Japan.Go.Jp/kizuna/_userdata/pdf/2021/spring2021/happiness_to_women_with_disabilities.pdf).
- **Vanmai Coffee Cooperative (Livelihood):** Houaphanh province; coffee introduced as “a sustainable alternative” to “illicit livelihoods such as opium cultivation”; producers “remain dependent on intermediaries”; a warehouse and secondary processing facilities, “enabling a shift from subsistence production to value-added, market-oriented agriculture”; “Duration: November 2024 – September 2027”; “Total project budget: 506,744.27USD”; donor UNODC. UNDP, https://www.undp.org/laopdr/projects/construction-secondary-processing-facilities-vanmai-coffee-cooperative
