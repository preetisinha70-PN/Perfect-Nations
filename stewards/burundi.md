## Watch items
- **Weza Power public-private partnership:** a 25-year agreement between the Government of Burundi and Weza Power (Anzana Electric Group) to mobilise US$1.4 billion for distribution in about half the country, signed 19 October 2026 as reported by Africa.com (20 October 2026) and Semafor. Not yet in the register: no Government of Burundi or Anzana release has been found.
- **FMO, Weza Power SU:** senior debt of up to US$10 million for Phase 1, in the contracting phase (FMO project page 66850, published 23 July 2026).
- **IMF Extended Credit Facility:** approved 17 July 2023, SDR 200.2 million (about US$271 million), 38 months (IMF press release 23/266). Staff discussions on the first review in January 2024 ended without agreement (IMF press release 24/19). The 2026 Article IV mission (IMF press release 26/158, May 2026) does not mention the arrangement. Not in the register until its status is confirmed.
- **Public debt:** 42% of GDP at end-2025, IMF staff estimate (IMF press release 26/158, 2026 Article IV mission, May 2026). Not yet on the page.
- **Ruzizi III regional hydropower (Burundi, DR Congo, Rwanda):** financing by the African Development Bank, the World Bank (P178685) and the EIB; the EIB was reviewing its financing in March 2025 (Engineering News). Status to be confirmed.
- **Agricultural credit facility:** BIF 300 billion (about US$100.5 million) at 5%, run through CRDB Bank Burundi, announced by the Government in August 2026 (Ecofin, Milling Middle East and Africa). No Ministry of Finance or CRDB release found yet.
- **Colline Climate Resilience Project:** the register shows US$70 million (World Bank project page); Xinhua (21 January 2025) reports an US$88.3 million budget for the climate resilience project signed in January 2025. To be checked against the World Bank project page.
