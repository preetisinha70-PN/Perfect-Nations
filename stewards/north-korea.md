## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for North Korea. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=KP
- **Water, 66.6%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=KP
- **Shelter, 77%:** City dwellers outside slums; 23% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=KP
- **Energy, 22%:** 22% cook with clean fuels and technology; 61.4% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=KP
- **Education, Not measured:** Neither learning outcomes nor lower-secondary completion is published for North Korea. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=KP
- **Health, 77:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~PRK
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for North Korea is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.DDAY?locations=KP

Furthest from reach: Energy (78%). Of people in the DPRK still cook without clean fuels.
