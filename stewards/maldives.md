## The seven standards

- **Food, 86.6%:** Not food insecure; 13.4% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=MV
- **Water, 99.8%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=MV
- **Shelter, 65.2%:** City dwellers outside slums; 34.9% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=MV
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=MV
- **Education, 94.6:** 94.6% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=MV
- **Health, 71:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MDV
- **Livelihoods, 88.8%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 11.2% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=MV

Furthest from reach: Shelter (34.8%). Of city dwellers in Maldives live in slums.
