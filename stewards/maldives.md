## The seven standards

Not re-read at source on 19 November 2026.

- **Food, 86.6%:** Not food insecure; 13.4% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=MV
- **Water, 99.8%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=MV
- **Shelter, 65.2%:** City dwellers outside slums; 34.9% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=MV
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=MV
- **Education, 94.6:** 94.6% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=MV
- **Health, 71:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MDV

## The register (8 lines)

Sources opened on 19 November 2026 unless stated.

- **Government of Maldives and the Maldives Monetary Authority, sovereign sukuk ($500M, completed):** “The Maldives Monetary Authority (MMA) completed the settlement of the USD 500 million Sukuk on 2nd April 2026”, including “the associated coupon payments”; “met through a combination of official reserves and the Sovereign Development Fund.” Maldives Financial Review, 3 April 2026, https://mfr.mv/financial-sector/mma-announces-the-settlement-of-usd-500-million-sukuk . Amount paid: “paying US$ 524.68 million” (Manohar Parrikar IDSA, above). Reserves: “Reserves increased to $1.3 billion … in March 2026” and fell to $717.9 million in April 2026 (World Bank, Maldives Development Update 2026). The MMA’s own release was not found.
- **World Bank, Maldives Emergency Support for Critical Services Project ($40M, approved):** “Maldives Emergency Support for Critical Services Project”, approved “May 04, 2026”, principal “US$ 40,000,000” (World Bank Finances One, IBRD/IDA as of 30 September 2026, https://financesone.worldbank.org/countries/maldives). Atoll Times, 12 August 2026: “USD 40 million was reallocated from existing projects to support fuel imports”; “The two facilities bring financing arranged by the ADB and World Bank for fuel supplies and related services to USD 90 million” (https://atolltimes.mv/post/business/16799). The ADB release of 11 August 2026: “ADB’s support complements $40 million from the World Bank for the same purpose.”
- **Green Climate Fund and UNEP, TRACT, SAP050 ($25M grant, approved):** GCF financing USD 25,000,000 as a grant; approved 03 Jul 2025 at B.42; status “Under implementation”, 15% disbursed; accredited entity “United Nations Environment Programme”; co-financing USD 248,570 in kind; to 03 Jul 2030; it aims to “help protect the Maldives from climate threats by providing reliable climate information”. https://www.greenclimate.fund/project/sap050 . The GCF Maldives page lists four projects (FP007 completed, SAP050, FP190, FP165) and about USD 77.4 million of GCF financing (https://www.greenclimate.fund/countries/maldives).
- **World Bank and the Global Partnership for Education, Atoll Education Development Project, additional financing ($2.7M grant, approved):** a $2.7 million grant under GPE’s Multiplier Grant; total project financing $12.775 million; the project was “approved by the World Bank’s Board in September 2022”, with “a $4.5 million grant and a $4.5 million credit from the International Development Association (IDA)”; it aims at “improving teacher effectiveness and student learning outcomes at the secondary level”, reaching “nearly all the 100 target schools”. World Bank press release, 30 October 2024, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/10/30/world-bank-approves-additional-financing-to-improve-secondary-education-in-maldives (project P181581).
- **ADB, Energy Security Emergency Assistance Project ($50M loan, signed):** “Energy Security Emergency Assistance Project (Maldives)”, $50,000,000, “Proposed Emergency Assistance Loan” from “ADB’s ordinary capital resources”, approved on concessional terms with “An interest rate of 1.0% per year” and “A 40-year term, including a 10-year grace period” (Board minutes of 11 August 2026, https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/institutional-document/1173186/m21-26-11aug26.pdf). Release of 11 August 2026: a “$50 million emergency assistance loan” that “will fund critical diesel imports to keep power and water running”, also covering “food and health-care logistics”, and “Imported fuel produces 94% of the country’s electricity and runs the desalination plants that supply drinking water”, https://www.adb.org/news/adb-backs-philippines-maldives-800-million-middle-east-conflict-strains-public-services . Signed “on Sunday” (16 August 2026) by “Minister of Finance Hassan Zareer”, “State Trading Organisation (STO) Chief Executive Officer Shimad Ibrahim” and “ADB Regional Head Dr Thiam Hee Ng” (Atoll Times, 16 August 2026, https://atolltimes.mv/post/business/16851).
- **AIIB, Strengthening Fiscal Management and Sustainability Program ($15M, approved):** project “000815”, “USD15 million”, “Sovereign”, approved “August 28, 2024”, borrower “Republic of Maldives” (https://www.aiib.org/en/projects/details/2024/approved/Maldives-Strengthening-Fiscal-Management-and-Sustainability-Program.html). Project document of 28 August 2024: implementation “Q3-2024 to Q2-2027”; “the total buy-down amount is estimated at USD2.74 million”; “In September 2023, ADB approved a PBG” of “USD18.7 million to the Maldives” (https://aiib.org/en/projects/details/2024/_download/Maldives/AIIB-PD-Maldives-Strengthening-Fiscal-Management-and-Sustainability-Program-RBP.pdf). Ministry of Finance: the grant “reduces the interest rate by 100 basis points”; the programme aims to “strengthen fiscal sustainability and promote climate-resilient investments” (https://finance.gov.mv/projects/maldives:-strengthening-fiscal-management-and-sustainability-program).
- **Reserve Bank of India, currency swap facility (INR 30bn, active; refreshed):** “INR 30 billion”, a swap drawdown under the SAARC Currency Swap Framework, with the first drawdown approved by India; the framework covers 2024 to 2027 and includes concessional interest rate terms (MV+, 25 April 2026, https://www.plus.mv/?p=163093). ## The enterprises

Opened 19 November 2026.

- **Vaadhoo Green Farm (Food):** a community-led initiative with “commercial-scale hydroponics powered entirely by renewable energy”, with “rooftop solar panels” and rainwater harvesting, capacity for “up to 600 kilograms of cucumbers and 400 kilograms of leafy greens per harvest”, an “estimated MVR 80,000 per month in income”; eighteen women trained in hydroponic farming and 25 in solar installation and maintenance; partner the Vaadhoo Association for Future Development. UNDP Maldives, 13 January 2026, https://www.undp.org/maldives/press-releases/government-and-undp-supports-community-led-green-farm-advancing-womens-empowerment-and-renewable-energy-r-vaadhoo ; also MMTV, 13 January 2026, https://en.mmtv.mv/7744 (which places it in Gaafu Dhaalu; UNDP’s title names R. Vaadhoo).
- **Housing Development Finance Corporation (Shelter):** “the Maldives’ only dedicated housing finance institution”, established in 2004; vision of “decent and affordable homes”; mission to “offer financial and social strength to all Maldivians by providing home loans”; “24,000+” housing units funded and “6,000+” homeowners created; conventional and Islamic facilities, Lite Financing and managed schemes (https://hdfc.com.mv/about-us). Shareholders: Government of Maldives 49%, Asian Development Bank 18%, International Finance Corporation 18%, HDFC Bank Ltd (India) 15% (Annual Report 2025, https://hdfc.com.mv/api/resource-downloads/file/HDFC-Annual-Report-2025-Final-WebsiteCompressed.pdf). Earned income: “HDFC generated a net profit of MVR 26.14 million during the Quarter 2 of 2026”; loan portfolio (net) “over MVR1.63 billion” (Financial Highlights for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, https://hdfc.com.mv/api/resource-downloads/file/CMDA-2026-Q2.pdf).
- **Syllabi (Education):** “Maldivian educational technology platform Syllabi”, first established in 2018; Syllabi Exam Prep for “O-Level and A-Level examinations”, with revision notes, practice questions and answer explanations; “More than 70 schools across the Maldives are already using the platform”; “Students can use the service free of charge until September 15”. Maldives Voice, 7 September 2026, https://maldivesvoice.mv/3191
