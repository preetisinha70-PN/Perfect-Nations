## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **18.3 million citizens, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population, indicator SP.POP.TOTL, read 30 October 2026 (the World Bank data pages and API do not open in these reviews, so the figure was not re-read on 14 November 2026). Population of record: 18,289,896.
- **$5B IMF programme, 2024:** the record in figures and the register. IMF Country Report 26/84 (April 2026), which reproduces the IMF press release of 22 April 2026, https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1ecuea2026001-source-pdf.pdf (opened 14 November 2026): “Ecuador’s 48-month EFF arrangement was approved by the Executive Board in May 2024 and augmented in July 2025, providing access equivalent to SDR 3.75 billion (about US$5 billion)”; “The Board’s decision enables an immediate disbursement of SDR 280.5 million (US$394 million), bringing total disbursements under the arrangement to SDR 2.7 billion (about US$3.7 billion).”
- **$1.55B World Bank finance, 2026:** arithmetic, the two World Bank approvals of 2026 in the register: US$750 million (28 July 2026) and US$800 million (24 September 2026). The US$800 million is a programme of three phases over ten years; its first phase is US$200 million. World Bank, press release of 24 September 2026, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/09/24/world-bank-group-expands-subnational-infrastructure-finance-in-ecuador (opened 14 November 2026): “provided through a Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA)” over “three phases spanning ten years”; first phase “US$200 million in World Bank financing”.

### The record in figures
- **$68.1B public debt and other obligations, 50.05% of GDP, 31 August 2026:** Ministry of Economic and Productive Development (Ministerio de Desarrollo Económico y Productivo, the issuer named on the bulletin and on the site), “Boletín de Deuda Pública y otras obligaciones del SPNF y la Seguridad Social / PIB”, August 2026 (provisional), listed at https://www.economicoproductivo.gob.ec/https-wwwdeuda-publica-nueva-metodologia/ (opened 14 November 2026); file https://www.economicoproductivo.gob.ec/wp-content/plugins/download-monitor/download.php?id=18692 (opened 14 November 2026). “INDICADOR DE LA DEUDA PÚBLICA Y OTRAS OBLIGACIONES DEL SPNF Y LA SEGURIDAD SOCIAL / PIB”, August: PIB 136,017,057.00; external debt 52,257,577.99; internal debt 14,672,290.71; other obligations 1,140,477.26; “Total Saldo Deuda Pública” 68,070,345.96; “Relación Deuda/PIB 50.05%” (thousands of US dollars); “la información se presenta de manera provisional”. The three components sum to 68,070,345.96 and the ratio recomputes to 50.045% (arithmetic). The same bulletin gives external debt of the total public sector of US$53,540 million, including US$31,366 million owed to multilateral organisations and US$16,004 million in debt securities issued in international markets.
- **The IMF measure:** IMF Country Report 26/84, as above: “The public sector debt rose to 54.3 percent of GDP at end-2025.” The IMF and Ministry measures differ in scope and date and are not compared on the page.
- **$4B international bonds, January 2026:** IMF Country Report 26/84, as above: “issued US$4 billion in new bonds maturing in 2034 and 2039 at an average interest rate of 8.98 percent”, a US$3 billion buyback, “leaving a net amount of US$1 billion for fiscal financing”.
