## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **18.3 million citizens, 2025:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population, read 30 October 2026 (the World Bank data pages do not open in these reviews, so the figure was not re-read on 7 November 2026). Population of record: 18,289,896.
- **$5B IMF programme, 2024:** the record in figures and the register. IMF Country Report 26/84 (April 2026), https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1ecuea2026001-source-pdf.pdf (opened 7 November 2026): Extended Fund Facility approved May 2024, access “SDR 3.75 billion (about US$5 billion)” after the July 2025 augmentation; fifth review completed 22 April 2026, “immediate disbursement of SDR 280.5 million (US$394 million)”, total disbursements “SDR 2.7 billion (about US$3.7 billion)”.
- **$1.55B World Bank finance, 2026:** arithmetic, the two World Bank approvals of 2026 in the register: US$750 million (28 July 2026) and US$800 million (24 September 2026). The US$800 million is a multiphase programme of three phases over ten years; its first phase is US$200 million (World Bank, Spanish release of 24 September 2026, https://www.bancomundial.org/es/news/press-release/2026/09/24/world-bank-group-expands-subnational-infrastructure-finance-in-ecuador, opened 7 November 2026).

### The record in figures
- **$4B international bonds, January 2026, and public sector debt of 54.3% of GDP at end-2025:** IMF Country Report 26/84, as above: “In January 2026, Ecuador issued US$4 billion in new bonds maturing in 2034 and 2039”, “at an average interest rate of 8.98 percent”, US$3 billion used for a buyback, “leaving a net amount of US$1 billion for fiscal financing”; “The public sector debt rose to 54.3 percent of GDP at end-2025”.

### The enterprises
- **Omarsa:** IFC press release, 21 December 2023 (opened 7 November 2026): “a leading shrimp producer, processor, and exporter in the country”, among the top two shrimp exporters, loan of up to US$20 million.
- **Interagua:** IDB Invest, 12 December 2018 (opened 7 November 2026): 30-year concession for water and sanitation in Guayaquil; coverage target of 95% by 2031.
- **Compañía de Titularización Hipotecaria (CTH):** IDB Invest, 11 June 2019 (opened 7 November 2026): “a company dedicated to promoting financing for housing and the Ecuadorian securitization market”.
- **Universidad de Las Américas (UDLA):** IDB Invest, 15 July 2019 (opened 7 November 2026): about 15,700 students; new Quito campus for up to 2,000 more.
- **Grupo Conclina:** IFC, 7 May 2024 (opened 7 November 2026): “Ecuador’s leading health services organization”, owner of the Hospital Metropolitano.
- **Caja Central Financoop:** IDB Invest, 29 September 2026 (opened 7 November 2026).
