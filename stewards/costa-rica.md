Sources opened on 17 and 18 November 2026, unless marked.

- **World Bank, First Costa Rica Fiscal Management and Green Growth Development Policy Loan ($300M):** approved by the Executive Board on 30 September 2025; to improve the efficiency of fiscal management and promote sustainable economic activities that generate new jobs, especially in rural and coastal areas; variable-rate loan, final maturity 33.5 years including a six-year grace period. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/09/30/costa-rica-y-banco-mundial-fortalecen-gestion-fiscal-y-generacion-de-empleos

## The enterprises

Opened 17 and 18 November 2026.

- **TECHO Costa Rica (Shelter):** more than 17 years in Costa Rica’s informal settlements; more than 2,000 emergency homes, with community centres, water systems, playgrounds and public access works; one of 50 winners of the Citi Foundation Global Innovation Challenge 2024, for homes with rainwater collection, solar panels and a dry toilet for more than 250 people in rural Carrillo (Guanacaste), Pococí (Limón) and Los Chiles (Alajuela). Delfino, 27 November 2024, https://delfino.cr/2024/11/techo-y-citi-foundation-se-unen-para-combatir-la-falta-de-vivienda-en-costa-rica ; at work in 2025 in Guanacaste, where 22 concrete floors and 2 homes were built in alliance with Cementos Progreso and PEDREGAL (Delfino, 28 November 2025, https://delfino.cr/2025/11/mas-de-240-personas-en-guanacaste-ahora-cuentan-con-un-piso-de-concreto-y-un-entorno-mas-saludable-en-sus-viviendas).
- **Banco de Alimentos de Costa Rica (Food):** rescues surplus food from companies and distributes it through more than 365 social and community organisations in 69 cantons; in 2025 it received more than 3.5 million kilos of products and delivered almost 2.5 million kilos; it reaches more than 44,000 people each month, among them more than 13,500 children. Delfino, 1 September 2026, https://delfino.cr/2026/09/millones-de-kilos-contra-el-desperdicio-la-carrera-del-banco-de-alimentos-por-rescatar-mas-comida
- **ASONI, Asociación por la Sonrisa de los Niños (Education):** Laboratorio de Sueños recycles plastic bottles into 3D-printer filament for teaching materials and building blocks, so that young people from vulnerable communities develop digital skills; US$6,500 and professional support in the social category of BAC’s Yo me uno awards, 1 October 2025. Delfino, 1 October 2025, https://delfino.cr/2025/10/tres-ong-recibiran-apoyo-de-bac-para-fortalecer-sus-iniciativas-sociales-ambientales-y-economicas
- **Asociación Moviendo Esperanzas, AME (Health):** “Esperanza Sobre Ruedas Verde y Digital” modernises the management of biomechanical aids for people with disabilities and older adults in all seven provinces; US$6,500 and support for digital transformation in the social category of BAC’s Yo me uno awards, 1 October 2025. Delfino, same page as above.
- **BIOSUR, Fundación para el Estudio de la Biodiversidad del Pacífico Sur (Livelihood):** “biOsa: Cosmética Natural con Impacto Social y Ambiental” empowers women stingless-bee keepers in Rancho Quemado, Río Tigre and Puerto Jiménez through the production of natural cosmetics; US$12,000 and support for its business plan in the Triple Value category of BAC’s Yo me uno awards, 1 October 2025. Delfino, same page as above.
