## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Türkiye. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=TR
- **Water, 96%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=TR
- **Shelter, 78.9%:** Safely managed sanitation; 14.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=TR
- **Energy, 98%:** 98% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=TR
- **Education, 81.5:** 81.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=TR
- **Health, 77:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~TUR
- **Livelihoods, 87%:** Above the national poverty line; 13% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=TR

Furthest from reach: Shelter (21.1%). Of people in Türkiye lack safely managed sanitation.
