## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Bhutan. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=BT
- **Water, 65.6%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=BT
- **Shelter, 49.5%:** Safely managed sanitation; 44.7% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=BT
- **Energy, 93%:** 93% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=BT
- **Education, 56.6:** 56.6% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=BT
- **Health, 69:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~BTN
- **Livelihoods, 87.6%:** Above the national poverty line; 12.4% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=BT

Furthest from reach: Shelter (50.5%). Of Bhutanese lack safely managed sanitation.
