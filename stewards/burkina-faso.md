## The hero

- **$215.9M, agricultural transformation financing, March 2026; the lead, secured $215.9 million in 2026 to transform its farms:** World Bank, “Burkina Faso: A New Project to Accelerate the Transformation of the Agricultural Sector”, Ouagadougou, 10 March 2026 (https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/03/10/burkina-faso-a-new-project-to-accelerate-the-transformation-of-the-agricultural-sector, opened 23 November 2026): “The World Bank today approved a project totaling $215.9 million (about CFAF 118.7 billion)”.
- **5.3%, growth, 2025; the lead, gold lifted growth to 5.3% in 2025:** IMF Country Report No. 26/207, Burkina Faso: 2026 Article IV Consultation, Fifth Review under the Extended Credit Facility Arrangement, July 2026 (https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1bfaea2026002.pdf, opened 23 November 2026): “Real GDP growth reached 5.3 percent”; “exports rose by 63 percent, with gold accounting for nearly 90 percent of the total”; “the current account swung from a deficit of 3.5 percent of GDP in 2024 to a 6.3 percent surplus in 2025.” The IMF release of February 2026 had estimated growth in 2025 at 5.0 percent.
- **53.7%, total outstanding public debt, share of GDP, 2025:** the same report, Memorandum of Economic and Financial Policies of the Government, Debt Policy: “For end-2026, based on planned disbursements and debt service, the total outstanding public debt is projected to be CFAF 9,179.1 billion, up 5.6 percent compared to end-2025. In relation to GDP, this outstanding amount would represent 48.3 percent in 2026, compared to 53.7 percent in 2025, a decline of 5.4 percentage points.” and “The outstanding public debt stood at CFAF 8,692.7 billion as of December 31, 2025”. The IMF staff tables of the same report show total public debt of 53.4 percent of GDP for 2025 (preliminary), against 52.1 percent projected at the fourth review; the two figures differ by 0.3 points.
- **The lead, a nation of 24.1 million:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (24,074,580); not re-read.

## The enterprises
