Xpatloop, 7 April 2026, https://xpatloop.com/channels/2026/04/munch-insight-local-app-prevents-huf-8-billion-of-food-waste-in-hungary.html

Egyesület, From Streets to Homes Association (Shelter):** runs the Hungarian version of Housing First and the Lakhatást Most! housing agency; private owners let flats to the association, which guarantees the rent and utilities and supports tenants with social workers; more than a hundred flats in its programmes, nearly 300 residents. Economx, 28 March 2026, https://economx.hu/ingatlan/2026/03/28/csendes-forradalom-budapesten-igy-segitik-otthonhoz-a-hajlektalan-es-lakasszegenysegben-elo-embereket/ ; nominated for SozialMarie 2025 (Peer Support for People Living in Housing Poverty), https://www.sozialmarie.org/sk/projects/10010
- **Igazgyöngy Alapítvány, Real Pearl Foundation (Education):** founded 1999 in Berettyóújfalu, art school 2000; works in Told and 17 other settlements and reaches over 1,200 families a year; its founder received the Officer’s Cross of the Hungarian Order of Merit in 2026. Eduline, 24 August 2026, https://eduline.hu/kozoktatas/20260824_l-ritok-nora-allami-kituntetes-elismeres-igazgyongy-alapitvany ; Ashoka Fellow since 2014; income-generation ventures with families (furniture making, medicinal plants, textile embroidery, biobriquettes, insulation repair). Ashoka, https://www.ashoka.org/en-us/fellow/nora-ritok

Founded in 2005; “90% of employees are differently-abled” at the café, whose income funds the foundation’s work (Planeterra, undated, https://planeterra.org/?p=3536).
- **Láthatatlan Kiállítás, Invisible Exhibition (Livelihood):** tours in complete darkness led by “a blind or visually impaired professional”; reopened 8 July 2026, listed from 8 July to 31 December 2026; ticketed. Budappest, 2026, https://budappest.com/budapest-events/step-into-the-dark-why-the-invisible-exhibition-is-budapests-most-unforgettable-experience/ ; “There are currently 30 people working at the Invisible Exhibition, including 25 blind or partially sighted people”, “a protected workplace” (Hithit campaign, finished 14 December 2020, https://www.hithit.com/en/project/8717/save-the-invisible-exhibition/faq).
