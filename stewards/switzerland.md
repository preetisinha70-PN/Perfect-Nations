## The seven standards

- **Food, 98%:** Not food insecure; 2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CH
- **Water, 96.7%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=CH
- **Shelter, 99.7%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CH
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CH
- **Education, 96:** 96% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=CH
- **Health, 87:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CHE
- **Livelihoods, 84.2%:** Above the national poverty line; 15.8% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=CH

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (15.8%). Of people in Switzerland live below the national poverty line.
