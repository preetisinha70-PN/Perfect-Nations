## The record in figures

- **CHF 10.8bn:** Net worth (Reinvermögen) of the state at the end of 2024, 137.7% of GDP. Office of Statistics, Öffentliche Finanzen 2024, published 13 May 2026, https://www.statistikportal.li/de/publikation/oeffentliche-finanzen-2024/2024/01/v-1/p46934/pdf (opened 19 November 2026): “Die Vermögensbilanz des Staates wies Ende 2024 ein Reinvermögen von rund CHF 10.8 Mrd. aus.” “In Prozent des BIP ausgedrückt, beträgt das Reinvermögen 137.7% des Bruttoinlandsproduktes.” The state covers “den Zentralstaat”, “die Gemeinden und die Sozialversicherungen”. In 2024 the state’s surplus was CHF 219.1 million (“der Überschuss mit CHF 219.1 Mio.”), 2.8% of GDP, the eleventh positive result in a row; revenue CHF 1,910.8 million, expenditure CHF 1,691.7 million; the Staatsquote “unverändert bei 21.6%”.
- **2.8%:** Fiscal surplus, share of GDP, 2025. IMF, “IMF Executive Board Concludes 2026 Article IV Consultation with the Principality of Liechtenstein”, 27 March 2026, https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/03/27/pr26091-liechtenstein-imf-concludes-2026-aiv-consultation-with-the-principality-of-liechtenstein (opened 18 November 2026). Under the 2025 fiscal outcomes: “The fiscal surplus remained high at 2.8 percent of GDP.” “Net financial assets of the general government, including social security fund assets, are estimated to have stayed above 140 percent of GDP.” “General government debt has remained virtually zero.” “Growth is estimated to have been flat 2025.” The IMF’s table shows a general government balance of 3.2% of GDP for 2024, on its own definition.
- **0.5%:** General government gross debt, share of GDP, 2025. IMF, WEO October 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/GGXWDG_NGDP@WEO/LIE (accessed 10 November 2026).

## The register (3 lines)

- **Landtag and Government of Liechtenstein, Housing for people in need of protection, Unterland (CHF 1.2M, approved):** Finanzbeschluss of 5 December 2024, LGBl. 2025 Nr. 64, issued 24 January 2025, https://www.gesetze.li/chrono/pdf/2025064000 (opened 19 November 2026): a Verpflichtungskredit of 1 200 000 Franken for “Wohnraum für die Unterbringung von Schutzbedürftigen im Unterland”, and supplementary credits for 2025 totalling CHF 1,405,000, of which CHF 1,050,000 for housing for people in need of protection and CHF 150,000 for first equipment. The Government’s report, Bericht und Antrag Nr. 150/2024 of 5 November 2024, https://llv.li/serviceportal2/amtsstellen/stabstelle-regierungskanzlei/bua_150_2024_verpflichtungs--und-nachtragskredit-wohnraum-schutzbeduerftige.pdf (opened 19 November 2026): “Für die Realisierung dieses Wohnraums wird ein Verpflichtungskredit von CHF 1.2 Mio. beantragt”; a vacant commercial building in the Eschen industrial area is converted to collective housing for “maximal 70 Personen”; the timetable set opening for November 2025 and occupancy for December 2025.
- **International Monetary Fund, IMF membership quota (SDR 100M, active):** “Initial quota for Liechtenstein: SDR 100 million (about US$134.7 million).” IMF, Press Release No. 24/387, 21 October 2024, https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2024/10/21/pr-24387-liechtenstein-principality-of-liechtenstein-becomes-imfs-191st-member (opened 18 November 2026); a member’s quota “determines its capital subscription”. The IMF country page shows “Quota (SDR): 100.0 million” and “The last Article IV Executive Board Consultation was on March 21, 2025” (https://www.imf.org/en/countries/lie, opened 18 November 2026); the 2026 consultation was concluded in March 2026 (press release of 27 March 2026, above).
- **Liechtensteinisches Pfandbriefinstitut, Inaugural Liechtenstein Pfandbriefe (CHF 250M, issued):** “comprising two tranches with a total volume of CHF 250 million”; “The yield to maturity is 0.593 per cent and 0.875 per cent”; Moody’s “rates the residential mortgage-backed mortgage bonds of the Liechtensteinische Pfandbriefinstitut AG at Aaa”; “From 22 April 2026, the bonds will be listed” on SIX Swiss Exchange; “The institution was founded in January 2026 by LGT and Liechtensteinische Landesbank (LLB)”. Corporate news of 1 April 2026, https://www.valuespectrum.com/corporate_news/1400999-liechtensteinisches-pfandbriefinstitut-successfully-issues-inaugural-bonds (opened 18 November 2026). Liechtensteinische Landesbank, “LGT and LLB establish Liechtensteinisches Pfandbriefinstitut”, Vaduz, 13 January 2026, https://llb.li/en/llb/media/media-communiques/media-communiques/2026/mm-13012026 (opened 18 November 2026): the bonds are “secured by first-lien mortgages on Liechtenstein real estate”; “The new Pfandbriefe act, adopted by the Liechtenstein Landtag in December 2024” sets the legal framework.
