- **Health, Not measured:** The WHO service coverage index is not published for Liechtenstein. WHO, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for Liechtenstein is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=LI
