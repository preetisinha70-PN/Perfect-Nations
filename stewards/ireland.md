## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **FoodCloud (Food):** founded in 2012; its own technology platform, Foodiverse; three hubs, in Dublin, Galway and Cork; the Growers Project links 540 food outlets with 685 charities and community groups; “450 million meals of surplus food have been redistributed through its international network”. The Irish Times, 11 June 2026, https://www.irishtimes.com/technology/2026/06/11/tapping-tech-to-ensure-charities-get-food-for-clients-while-providers-avoid-waste/ ; described as “a tech based, non-profit, social enterprise” by Enterprise Ireland, 26 September 2023, https://www.enterprise-ireland.com/en/news/foodcloud-secures-enterprise-ireland-r-d-support-to-accelerate-the-impact-of-food-redistribution-and-cut-food-waste-at-scale
- **Duhallow Community Food Services (Food):** County Cork; “provides subsidized meals, catering, and bakery products to people in the Duhallow area”; winner, Retail, Hospitality and Professional Services, first National Social Enterprise Awards. Government of Ireland, 15 October 2025, https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-rural-and-community-development-and-the-gaeltacht/press-releases/social-enterprises-recognised-at-the-first-national-social-enterprise-awards
- **Ó Cualann Cohousing Alliance (Shelter):** “the not-for-profit developer”; “It sells to homeowners at cost”; launched 32 social homes for older people at Parkview, Ballymun, in June 2024; partner in a Killinarden scheme with planning permission for 625 homes, 383 affordable, stalled for want of finance. Dublin Inquirer, 5 June 2024, https://www.dublininquirer.com/hundreds-of-affordable-homes-held-up-because-of-funding-woes-says-not-for-profit-developer/
- **MyMind (Health):** a “not-for-profit social enterprise” and registered charity, founded in 2006; fees linked to employment status, with reduced rates for unemployed people, full-time students and seniors; Rathmines, Dublin, and online; Annual Report 2025 and Strategy 2024 to 2027 listed. MyMind, https://mymind.org/about/ (copyright 2026)
- **Turn2Me (Health):** Dublin; “a high-quality, safe, anonymous, and confidential resource where people can gain support for their mental health online”; Social Enterprise of the Year, first National Social Enterprise Awards. Government of Ireland, 15 October 2025 (same page as above).
- **CareBright CLG (Health):** Limerick and throughout Munster; “provides home and community care services for the elderly and people with disabilities”; winner, Social Finance, first National Social Enterprise Awards. Government of Ireland, 15 October 2025 (same page).
- **Clann Credo (Livelihood):** social finance lender; “bridging and variable term loans”; “In 2024, Clann Credo issued 234 loan offers to community groups, charities and social enterprises across Ireland”, “valued at more than €32 million”; “a loan book position of €53.3 million at year end”. The Irish Times, 3 December 2025, https://www.irishtimes.com/special-reports/2025/12/03/lending-with-a-heart ; at work since 1996, more than €215 million lent to more than 1,500 organisations (Changing Ireland, 14 October 2024, https://changingireland.ie/clann-credo-has-loaned-e215-million-to-community-groups-since-1996/).
- **Rise at the Cove (Livelihood):** Greystones; “provides training and employment to people in the Tiglin Challenge Limited rehabilitation program”; winner, Buy Social, first National Social Enterprise Awards. Government of Ireland, 15 October 2025 (same page).
