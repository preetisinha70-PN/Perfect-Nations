## The register (4 lines)

Sources opened on 18 November 2026.

- **State of Israel, international dollar bonds of 5, 10 and 30 years ($6bn, issued):** January 2026; spreads of 90, 100 and 125 basis points; demand of about USD 36 billion, six times the amount issued. Ministry of Finance, Annual Debt Report 2025, https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/annual-debt-report-2025/en/files-eng_Annual-Debt-Reports_annual-debt-report-2025-accessible-version-en.pdf . The offering was completed on 6 January 2026 (Globes, 7 January 2026, https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001531346: “completed the global issue of a $6 billion bond yesterday”). Planned issuance is based on the financing needs derived from the expected deficit (same report).
- **State of Israel, Israel Bonds sales in 2025 ($2.4bn, issued):** about USD 2.4 billion raised in 2025, after about USD 2.5 billion in 2024; about NIS 8.3 billion; stock of Israel Bonds debt about USD 7.5 billion at the end of 2025; part of about NIS 26 billion raised in external markets in 2025, with NIS 17.4 billion in public offerings. Same report.
- **State of Israel, international dollar bonds of 5 and 10 years ($5bn, issued):** February 2025; five years, USD 2.5 billion at a yield of 5.573%, a spread of 120 basis points; ten years, USD 2.5 billion at a yield of 5.891%, a spread of 135 basis points; demand of 4.6 times the amount issued. Same report.
- **European Investment Bank, Western Galilee Desalination Plant (€150M, signed):** project 20210765; promoter State of Israel, Ministry of Finance; signed 23 December 2023; total cost EUR 470 million; design, construction and operation of a seawater reverse osmosis desalination facility of 100 million cubic metres a year, a public-private partnership. EIB project page, https://eib.org/en/projects/all/20210765

## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **Amigour (Shelter):** “With 57 sheltered living complexes throughout Israel”; “the 7,500 elderly Israelis living in Amigour apartment complexes”, “many of whom are Holocaust survivors”; “provided more than 60,000 housing solutions for new immigrants” and disadvantaged citizens; “Since 1972”. The Jewish Agency for Israel, page modified 17 August 2026, https://www.jewishagency.org/amigour/ ; described as “The Jewish Agency for Israel’s Amigour subsidiary” (JNS, 25 December 2020, https://www.jns.org/antisemitism/tel-aviv-sees-hundreds-of-brand-new-apartments-for-seniors-holocaust-survivors).
- **Center for Educational Technology, CET (Education):** “CET is a non-profit organization”, founded in 1971; “Students from 3,400 schools learn from a wide range of content”; “18,000 teachers and teachers in training participate in 600 online professional training courses a year”; the Ivritna programme for Arabic-speaking high-school students was “developed, operated by CET, and adopted by the Ministry of Education”. CET, https://cet.ac.il/about-us/?lang=en (page modified 8 November 2026). “CET, also known as Matach, is Israel’s largest educational publisher” (eSelf and CET release, 22 April 2025, https://www.prnewswire.com/il/news-releases/eself-partners-with-israels-center-for-educational-technology-to-lead-the-worlds-first-countrywide-rollout-of-ai-tutoring-302434204.html).
- **Yad Sarah (Health):** services “all for free or at nominal cost” so that chronically ill, disabled, infirm and terminally ill people can live at home; “over 125+ branches located in every corner of Israel”; “7,000+ volunteers supported by a small staff”; serves “hundreds of thousands of people in Israel annually”; equipment lending (Donate/Borrow Equipment). Friends of Yad Sarah, with documents for FY2025, https://friendsofyadsarah.org/about-us
- **Na Laga’at Center (Livelihood):** “an innovative cultural centre in Jaffa, Israel”, “with 70 per cent of its 100+ workforce having sensory disabilities”; “the Black-Out Dark Restaurant employs blind and visually impaired wait staff”; “the Kapish Event Complex features deaf or hearing-impaired waiters”; “60 per cent self-generated income from ticket sales, the restaurant, events, and workshops”; “From 2007 to 2024 over a million visitors have experienced its programmes.” Zero Project Awardee 2025, https://zeroproject.org/view/project/f57f8d89-4caa-ef11-b8e9-0022489c2733 ; Na Laga’at Impact Report 2025 (about 100,000 participants and visitors a year), https://nalagaat.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/NaLagaat_ImpactReport-ENG.pdf
- **Koret Israel Economic Development Funds, KIEDF (Livelihood):** “KIEDF facilitates access to credit and business services for Israel’s geographic and social periphery”; 2024 highlights of ₪80M; Supporting Arab Women Entrepreneurs (SAWA), ₪13.3M and “1,450 Loans” in 2024; Arab Business Loan Fund, Emergency Loan Fund and SME Bank Guarantee Funds; “Our Impact Since 1994”, ₪1.9B in credit. KIEDF, Annual Report 2024, https://reports.jewishfed.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2024-KIEDF-Annual-Report.pdf
