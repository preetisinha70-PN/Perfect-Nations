## The seven standards

- **Food, 90.8%:** Not food insecure; 9.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=IL
- **Water, 99.5%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=IL
- **Shelter, 97.2%:** Safely managed sanitation. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=IL
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=IL
- **Education, 85.6:** 85.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=IL
- **Health, 85:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~ISR
- **Livelihoods, 95.6%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 4.4% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=IL

Furthest from reach: Education (14.4%). Of children in Israel cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school.
