## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **600 MW, Karuma hydropower, 2024; the lead, commissioned the 600 megawatt Karuma dam in 2024:** State House Uganda, 26 September 2024 (https://statehouse.go.ug/president-museveni-commissions-600mw-karuma-hydropower-plant-paving-the-way-for-ugandas-energy-and-economic-growth/, opened 11 November 2026 as https://statehouse.go.ug/?p=8241): the President “has today commissioned” “The 600 MW hydropower project”, “partly funded by the Government of Uganda (15 percent) and the Exim Bank of China (85 percent)”, “constructed by Sinohydro Corporation Limited”; the chair of the Karuma and Isimba Hydropower Project Steering Committee called it “Africa’s largest underground hydropower plant”. Also Uganda Electricity Generation Company Limited (https://uegcl.com/president-museveni-commissions-karuma-hydro-power-plant/): “The 600MW Karuma Hydro Power Plant was commissioned on September 26th, 2024”.
- **100%, of electricity from renewables, 2023; the lead, whose electricity is almost entirely renewable:** IRENA, Uganda statistical profile, last updated 22 September 2025 (https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/Uganda_Africa_RE_SP.pdf, opened 11 November 2026): generation in 2023, total 5,457 GWh, renewable 5,442 GWh (IRENA shows 100%; 99.7% by arithmetic), non-renewable 15 GWh, hydro and marine 4,832 GWh (89%), bioenergy 487 GWh (9%), solar 122 GWh (2%); renewables 93% of total energy supply in 2022 (92% in 2017). It replaces “90.0%, renewable share of electricity output”, a World Bank indicator for 2021.
- **55.3%, with electricity, 2024; the lead, nearly half of citizens are not yet connected:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, access to electricity (EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS), 2024; 44.7% without is arithmetic; not re-read (the World Bank data pages do not open in these reviews).
- **The lead, a nation of 51.4 million:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (51,384,894, the `population` of the nation file); not re-read.
