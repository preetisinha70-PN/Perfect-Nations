## The seven standards

- **Food, 94.2%:** Not food insecure; 5.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=JP
- **Water, 98.7%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=JP
- **Shelter, 98%:** City dwellers outside slums; 2% live in slums. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=JP
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=JP
- **Education, 96.4:** 96.4% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=JP
- **Health, 86:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~JPN
- **Livelihoods, 96.7%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 3.3% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2020, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=JP

Furthest from reach: Food (5.8%). Of people in Japan are moderately or severely food insecure.

## The record and Capital already committed

- **¥180.7tn:** Government bond issuance planned for fiscal 2026, 8.9 trillion yen less than fiscal 2025 after its supplementary budget. Ministry of Finance, 2025, https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/jgbs/debt_management/plan/highlight251226.pdf
- **¥1,346.7tn:** Central government bonds and borrowings outstanding, end of June 2026. Ministry of Finance, 2026, https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/jgbs/publication/newsletter/jgb2026_08e.pdf
- **204.8%:** General government gross debt, share of GDP, 2025. IMF, WEO Oct 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/GGXWDG_NGDP@WEO/JPN
- **0.7%:** Real GDP growth projected for 2026. IMF, WEO Oct 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/NGDP_RPCH@WEO/JPN
- **FY2026 JGB Issuance Plan (¥180.7tn):** Total government bond issuance for the year, with less super-long debt, based on dialogue with the market. https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/jgbs/debt_management/plan/highlight251226.pdf
