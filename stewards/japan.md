## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **Second Harvest Japan (Food):** “Japan’s first food bank”, a certified non-profit (field report of 17 December 2025, https://2hj.org/english/report/9532); in 2024, “1,318,744Kg” of donated food, “2,491,155 Meals”, “1.03 million people”, “472 organizations” (258 food pantries, 31 children’s homes, 26 soup kitchens or homeless support, 157 other). Annual report 2024 in numbers, https://2hj.org/wp/wp-content/themes/2hj/pdf/2hj_AR_Numbers_2024.pdf . The Consumer Affairs Agency “has certified four food banks”, Second Harvest Japan among them (Jiji Press, 28 July 2026, https://english.adnkronos.com/2026/07/28/japan-govt-certifies-food-banks-for-1st-time/). Earned income (事業収益) was 2.5% of 2024 revenue; “70% of 2HJ’s revenue comes from donations” (annual report 2024, https://2hj.org/wp/wp-content/themes/2hj/pdf/2hj_AR_2024_fix_web.pdf).
- **Kuradashi (Food):** “Kuradashi Co., Ltd. (Kuradashi), a company actively tackling food loss and food waste in Japan”; a logistics outsourcing service “With a nationwide network of over 150 partnered warehouses”; “A portion of proceeds supports environmental conservation, disaster relief, and other social initiatives”. Zenbird, 31 January 2025, https://zenbird.media/kuradashi-expands-logistics-services-to-tackle-food-loss-in-supply-chains/ . Founded in 2014; buys products before their best-by dates and sells them at a discount; 390,000 members and 1,085 partner companies in August 2023 (Tokyo Metropolitan Government, Tokyo Updates, 28 August 2023, https://www.tokyoupdates.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en/post-1107).
- **WOTA (Water):** a private company developing “small-scale decentralized water-regeneration systems”; after the 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake, 100 WOTA BOX units and 207 WOSH handwashing stands; “These systems supported 89% of evacuation centers that experienced prolonged water outages”; 68 medical and welfare facilities; disaster response agreements progressively with all 47 prefectures. WOTA, “Two Years Since the 2024 Noto Peninsula Earthquake”, 1 January 2026, https://wota.co.jp/en/news-260101/
- **Homedoor (Shelter):** 認定NPO法人 in Osaka; “２棟42室のシェルター” (a shelter of 42 rooms in two buildings); “7000人以上の生活” supported; HUBchari and Osaka Bike Share unified as HUBchari from 1 April 2026 at about 700 locations (“大阪府下 約700拠点”); “就労支援提供者数” more than 500. Release of Homedoor and Docomo Bike Share, 14 May 2026, https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000031.000016497.html . “HUBchari’s ride revenue is then directly and entirely redistributed to workers’ salaries and shelter operations” (Copenhagenize, 12 November 2025, https://copenhagenizeindex.eu/index.php/2025/11/12/hubchari-osakas-bike-share-that-turns-everyday-trips-into-social-impact/).
- **Katariba (Education):** an NGO that “provides learning opportunities and support to children who do not attend school”; pilot agreement with The Nippon Foundation and Misawa, Kokubunji, Miyoshi and Osaki. The Nippon Foundation, 6 October 2025, https://en.nippon-foundation.or.jp/news/articles/2025/20251006-109455.html . Founded “1st November 2001” (https://www.katariba.or.jp/english/). Room K, an online support programme launched in 2021, is run with public education authorities and public funding and accessed through participating municipalities (Nippon.com, 20 June 2024, https://nippon.com/en/japan-topics/c12703).
- **Florence (Health):** “one of Japan’s largest certified NPOs in the fields of child welfare and child rearing”, founded 12 April 2004; “our main revenue is generated from our own business income”; Florence Sick Kid Sitter Service, Medical Daycare “Helen” and Florence Children’s Food Outreach Program (https://florence.or.jp/english). Sick-child care in five new areas from 1 April 2026, 34 cities and 23 wards in all; “累計実績15万件、重大事故ゼロ” (150,000 cases, no serious accident). Florence release, 1 April 2026, https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20260401/177001.html
- **Ory Laboratory, OryLab (Livelihood):** DAWN ver.β, a café “where people with severe disabilities or intractable diseases who have difficulty going out operate bunshin robots”, run “in Nihonbashi, Tokyo”; for a café in Aarhus, Denmark, from 2 April to 30 September 2025, “the project has hired individuals in Aarhus who face mobility challenges”. OryLab, https://orylab.com/the-first-overseas-store-opens-in-aarhus-denmark-en/ ; the Tokyo café opened in June 2021 and is owned by Ory Laboratory (Barista Magazine, undated, https://baristamagazine.com/dawn-avatar-a-robot-run-cafe-made-for-inclusion-in-tokyo).
