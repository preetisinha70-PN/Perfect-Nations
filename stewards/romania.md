## The enterprises

Opened 18 and 19 November 2026. Four verified.

- **Ateliere Fără Frontiere (Education):** “Romanian social enterprise Ateliere Fără Frontiere (Workshops Without Borders, AFF)”, “more than 38,000 computers” given to schools and social bodies through “Dăm Click pe România (Let’s Click Romania)”, aiming to pass 40,000 in 2026 (Romania Insider, 18 June 2026, https://www.romania-insider.com/ngo-donations-refurbished-computers-june-2026). “a Romanian non-profit and social enterprise dedicated to the socio-professional integration of vulnerable people”, “through four social businesses” (educlick, remesh, bio&co and logitetic); “bio&co is a farm outside Bucharest where we grow organic vegetables and deliver them weekly to our urban customers”; 4,000 refurbished computers donated in 2024 (EESC Info, September 2025, https://www.eesc.europa.eu/en/news-media/eesc-info/eesc-info-september-2025/articles/131679).
- **Habitat for Humanity Romania (Shelter):** “Over 30 years, we have built more than 5 100 homes with support from over 55 000 volunteers”; families meet “financial need (below 60% of the national average income), urgency and a 500-hour ‘sweat equity’ contribution”; “Habitat rents apartments from the private market and offers them free or at subsidised rates to families in need”; an energy resilience programme “improving insulation and reducing heating costs for vulnerable households”. EESC Info, December 2025, “The power of volunteering: building homes in Romania”, https://www.eesc.europa.eu/cs/news-media/eesc-info/eesc-info-december-2025/articles/133464 . New homes in Berceni, Prahova county, in 2025 (Romania Insider, 27 May 2025, https://www.romania-insider.com/ceo-build-social-housing-may-2025).

Earned income: a lender, financed in 2026 by an EIB loan signed on 12 October 2026 (https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20250698).
- **AFIN IFN (Livelihood):** “the first non-bank financial institution in Romania dedicated to financing the social economy”, financing “social enterprises, cooperatives and non-governmental organisations”; “provided more than 130 loans totalling approximately €2.5 million across Romania”. EIF, 20 May 2026, https://www.eif.org/press/all/romanian-social-enterprises-to-get-financing-boost-as-eif-teams-up-with-domestic-lender-afin-ifn
