## The seven standards

- **Food, 81.4%:** Not food insecure; 18.6% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=RO
- **Water, 82.2%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=RO
- **Shelter, 89.8%:** Safely managed sanitation; 2.5% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=RO
- **Energy, 96%:** 96% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=RO
- **Education, 78:** 78% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=RO
- **Health, 77:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~ROU
- **Livelihoods, 81.6%:** Above the national poverty line; 18.4% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=RO

Furthest from reach: Education (22%). Of young people in Romania do not complete lower secondary school.
