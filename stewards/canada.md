Sources opened on 17 November 2026.

Housing, Infrastructure and Communities Canada, “Minister Dabrusin highlights Budget 2026’s plan to end homelessness within 10 years”, 7 November 2026, https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/news/2026/11/minister-dabrusin-highlights-budget-2026s-plan-to-end-homelessness-within-10-years.html
- **Department of Finance Canada, Debt Management Strategy 2026-27 as updated in Budget 2026 (C$582bn):** treasury bills C$284 billion, bonds C$298 billion (2-year C$110 billion, 5-year C$80 billion, 10-year C$80 billion, 30-year C$24 billion, green bonds C$4 billion); up C$16 billion from the Spring Update 2026. https://budget.canada.ca/2026/report-rapport/anx2-en.html
- **Department of Finance Canada, Debt Management Strategy 2027-28 (C$611bn projected):** total sources of borrowings C$611 billion: treasury bills C$290 billion, bonds C$316 billion, foreign currencies C$5 billion; refinancing needs C$442 billion (72% of borrowings); loans to enterprise Crown corporations C$66 billion; up to C$30 billion to fund purchases of Canada Mortgage Bonds; stock of market debt projected at C$1,912 billion by the end of 2027-28. Same source, Tables A2.2 and A2.3.

## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Indwell (Shelter):** a charity that creates supportive affordable housing communities; “the fastest growing developer of new supportive affordable housing in Ontario”; tenants in 1,435 households; impact loans and a Hamilton community bond listed among ways to give. https://indwell.ca/about-us/
- **Second Harvest (Food):** “Canada’s largest food rescue organization”; 126 million pounds of food rescued and redistributed from more than 11,100 food donors; 7.2 million people supported through more than 4,000 non-profit partners (impact last year, as the page stated on 17 November 2026). https://www.secondharvest.ca/about/impact
- **Water First (Water):** a registered Canadian charity; eight new Indigenous water operators from the Anishinaabeg of Kabapikotawangag Resource Council internship; 73 interns from 47 Indigenous communities graduated; more than 100 Indigenous communities since 2009. Water First release, Kenora, 21 May 2026, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/21/3299311/0/en/New-Water-Operators-Graduate-Internship-Program.html
- **Building Up (Livelihood):** Toronto social enterprise and non-profit contractor; over 4,000 applications a year, about 100 accepted, eight weeks of intensive training; more than 900 trained alumni; energy-efficiency retrofits for social housing and low- and moderate-income households. CLEAResult, 5 November 2025, https://www.clearesult.com/insights/building-up-and-clearesult-renovating-opportunities-in-toronto
- **SolarShare (Energy):** Ontario non-profit co-operative owned by about 2,000 members; 51 solar photovoltaic systems, about 15 MW; C$53,806,042 in revenue generated; 2026 SolarBonds open from 16 January 2026, three- to seven-year terms, up to 4.0% fixed interest. https://solarbonds.ca/solarbonds2026
