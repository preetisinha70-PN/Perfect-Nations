## The seven standards

- **Food, 89.8%:** Not food insecure; 10.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CA
- **Water, 96.9%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=CA
- **Shelter, 81.3%:** Safely managed sanitation; 1.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CA
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CA
- **Education, 95.7:** 95.7% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2016, dated. World Bank, 2016, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=CA
- **Health, 92:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CAN
- **Livelihoods, 99.4%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 0.6% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=CA

Furthest from reach: Shelter (18.7%). Of Canadians lack safely managed sanitation.
