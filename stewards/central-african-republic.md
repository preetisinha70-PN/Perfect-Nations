## The seven standards

- **Food, 11.9%:** Not food insecure; 88.1% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CF
- **Water, 6.2%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=CF
- **Shelter, 13.5%:** Safely managed sanitation; 68.9% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CF
- **Energy, 1%:** 1% cook with clean fuels and technology; 18.2% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CF
- **Education, 8.9:** 8.9% of young people complete lower secondary school; 2016, dated. World Bank, 2016, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=CF
- **Health, 39:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CAF
- **Livelihoods, 28.4%:** Above US$3.00 a day; 71.6% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.DDAY?locations=CF

Furthest from reach: Energy (99%). Of Central Africans still cook without clean fuels.
