## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Eberbach-Polesye (Health):** a company in Mozyr, described by UNDP as a social commercial enterprise (“социальным коммерческим предприятием ООО «Эбербах-Полесье»”, UNDP, “На пути к инклюзии”, 4 March 2025, https://www.undp.org/ru/node/510916); opened the first production of polyurethane wheelchair tyres in Belarus in 2025; about 24,000 people in Belarus use wheelchairs; 150 people have received new tyres free of charge. UNDP Belarus, “Работа без барьеров”, 14 January 2026, https://www.undp.org/ru/node/565121
- **Raik, public association (Livelihood):** coffee-roasting workshop in Mozyr and the first fully barrier-free space in the country for learning to make coffee, with training as a barista or coffee roaster. UNDP Belarus, “Работа без барьеров”, 14 January 2026, https://www.undp.org/ru/node/565121
- **ApiMir (Livelihood):** centre for the development and promotion of beekeeping in Zhlobin district, created with the support of UNDP and the local authorities; demonstration apiary, training and consultations for beekeepers from across Belarus. UNDP Belarus, “Региональная формула достижения ЦУР: опыт Гомельской области”, 19 September 2025, https://www.undp.org/ru/node/545436
- **Eco-Innovation Fund (Livelihood):** local fund for the support of entrepreneurship and rural development in Bragin district; local farmers have learned to use environmentally clean technologies for crops, livestock and cheese-making. UNDP Belarus, 19 September 2025 (same page).
