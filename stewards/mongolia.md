## The seven standards

- **Food, 95.2%:** Not food insecure; 4.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=MN
- **Water, 43.4%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=MN
- **Shelter, 70%:** Safely managed sanitation; 17.9% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=MN
- **Energy, 59%:** 59% cook with clean fuels and technology; 99.1% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=MN
- **Education, 60.5:** 60.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2007, dated. World Bank, 2007, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=MN
- **Health, 70:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MNG
- **Livelihoods, 72.9%:** Above the national poverty line; 27.1% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=MN

Furthest from reach: Water (56.6%). Of Mongolians lack safely managed drinking water.
