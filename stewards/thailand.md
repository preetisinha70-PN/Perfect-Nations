## The seven standards

- **Food, 94.6%:** Not food insecure; 5.4% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=TH
- **Water, 100%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=TH
- **Shelter, 26.7%:** Safely managed sanitation; 2% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=TH
- **Energy, 88%:** 88% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=TH
- **Education, 76.6:** 76.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2011, dated. World Bank, 2011, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=TH
- **Health, 82:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~THA
- **Livelihoods, 88.3%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 11.7% live below it. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=TH

Furthest from reach: Shelter (73.3%). Of Thais lack safely managed sanitation.
