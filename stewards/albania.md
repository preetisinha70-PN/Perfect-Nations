## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Fondi BESA (Energy):** Albania’s largest regulated non-banking microfinance institution, about 27,500 MSME clients (mid-2025); green loans for energy-efficiency home improvements and small solar PV. responsAbility, 11 February 2026, https://www.responsability.com/en/press-releases/responsability-commits-green-loan-facility-to-fondi-besa-in-albania
- **Albaponik (Food):** hydroponic farm in Baldushk near Tirana, over 3,000 square metres, water use reduced by up to 90%. EU Delegation to Albania, 12 June 2025, https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/albania/albaponik-revolutionising-sustainable-farming-albania-eu-support_en
- **Agro & Social Fund (Food):** microfinance for farmers and rural micro-entrepreneurs, set up in 2001 by World Vision Albania; 3,720 beneficiaries. Grameen Crédit Agricole Foundation, https://www.gca-foundation.org/en/organisation/agro-social-fund/
- **Psikologu Online (Health):** online psychological consultations, established in 2021; 40–70 new monthly clients. New European Bauhaus Prizes, https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/node/366876
- **FED Invest (Livelihood):** microfinance for vulnerable groups; €10 million CEB loan approved in April 2025. Council of Europe Development Bank, 5 April 2025, https://coebank.org/en/news-and-publications/news/ceb-approves-twelve-new-loans-totalling-983-5-million-euros/
- **Crystal Team (Livelihood):** professional cleaning social enterprise in Berat employing Roma and Egyptian women; EU4Innovation Challenge Fund. EU Delegation to Albania, https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/albania/margins-opportunity-roma-women-building-futures-berat_en
