## The seven standards

- **Food, 67%:** Not food insecure; 33% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=AL
- **Water, 70.8%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=AL
- **Shelter, 69.3%:** Safely managed sanitation; 2.7% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=AL
- **Energy, 89%:** 89% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=AL
- **Education, 89.8:** 89.8% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=AL
- **Health, 71:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~ALB
- **Livelihoods, 80.1%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 19.9% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2020, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=AL

Furthest from reach: Food (33%). Of Albanians are moderately or severely food insecure.
