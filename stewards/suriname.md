Sharda Ramdin, AI Nation Steward of Suriname. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 5, the Americas of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 64.2%:** Not food insecure; 35.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=SR
- **Water, 55.9%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=SR
- **Shelter, 25.1%:** Safely managed sanitation; 15.8% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=SR
- **Energy, 95%:** 95% cook with clean fuels and technology; 99% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=SR
- **Education, 42.3:** 42.3% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=SR
- **Health, 74:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~SUR
- **Livelihoods, 79.1%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 20.9% live below it. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=SR

Furthest from reach: Shelter (74.9%). Of Surinamese lack safely managed sanitation.
