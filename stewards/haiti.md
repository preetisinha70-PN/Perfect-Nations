## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **Meds & Food for Kids, MFK (Food):** ready-to-use therapeutic food for children with severe acute malnutrition, made in Quartier Morin, Cap-Haïtien; about 6,000 boxes a week, each of 150 sachets; the partnership with UNICEF has helped treat almost 270,000 children; more than 3,000 farmers trained and more than 500,000 kg of local peanuts bought. UNICEF Haiti, 15 January 2025, https://www.unicef.org/haiti/en/stories/haitis-greatest-defense-against-malnutrition-crafted-within-its-own-borders ; MFK “employs nearly 100 Haitians” (https://mfkhaiti.org/what-we-do/).
- **dloHaiti (Water):** “a social business providing safe drinking water to consumers in underserved communities in Haiti”, “treating ground water locally and distributing 20-liter jugs of water through small stores and merchants”. dloHaiti, https://dlohaiti.com/ (undated); its decentralised, solar-powered water kiosks are described in the podcast Mission: Possible, episode 54, 23 January 2026 (https://www.buzzsprout.com/2556323/episodes/18555229-54-off-grid-water-in-haiti-how-decentralized-solutions-are-quenching-a-nation-s-thirst).
- **SOIL, EkoLakay (Shelter, through sanitation):** a household container-based sanitation service in Cap-Haïtien since 2013, “currently serving more than 21,000 people each day”; on 20 January 2026 SOIL and DINEPA finalised the first government-funded results-based financing contract for container-based sanitation as a public service, covering roughly 1,400 households, about one third of active clients. SOIL, 6 March 2026, https://oursoil.org/blog/2026-03/haiti-sets-global-first-government-contract-container-based-sanitation-services
- **EarthSpark International, Enèji Pwòp (Energy):** in Haiti since 2008; the first privately operated pre-pay microgrid, Les Anglais, in 2012, now serving more than 500 homes and businesses; Tiburon, more than 400 connections, about 2,300 people, expanded again in 2026 to 173 kWp / 507 kWh; six grids under construction (La Cahouane, Marfranc, Beaumont, Chambellan, Anse d’Hainault and Dame Marie) and provisional approval for 22 more. EarthSpark International, https://earthsparkinternational.org/microgrid-electricity-in-haiti.html
- **Fonkoze (Livelihood):** led and founded by Haitians, more than 30 years in rural Haiti, serving primarily women; Sèvis Finansye Fonkoze (SFF) is “the largest microfinance institution in Haiti” and operates in a financially self-sustaining manner; 38,546 clients served, 43,619 microloans distributed, 11,004 women in the CLM and Boujannen Lavi programmes, 66,142 Haitians directly benefited from Fonkoze Foundation programmes in 2024. Fonkoze, fact sheet, August 2025, https://fonkoze.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/One-Pager-Fonkoze-About-25.08.13-1.pdf
