## The seven standards

Not re-read at source on 23 November 2026 (the World Bank API and data pages did not open in this review, and no newer primary release of the same indicators was found).

- **Food, 62.1%:** Not food insecure; 37.9% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CI
- **Water, 36.4%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=CI
- **Shelter, 27.1%:** Safely managed sanitation. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CI
- **Energy, 31.5:** About 315 kWh of electricity a person a year, against 1,000 kWh; 48% cook with clean fuels (2024). Electricity demand per person 315 kWh, 2024 (Ember, Yearly Electricity Data, 2026 release, https://ember-energy.org/data/yearly-electricity-data/; read through the Our World in Data energy dataset, accessed 12 November 2026). 315 / 1,000 = 31.5% (arithmetic).
- **Education, 20.1:** 20.1% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=CI
- **Health, 46:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CIV
- **Livelihoods, 58.4%:** Above US$4.20 a day; 41.6% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.LMIC?locations=CI

Furthest from reach: Education (80%). Of Ivorian children could not read a simple text by the end of primary school in 2019.
