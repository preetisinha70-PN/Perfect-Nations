## The enterprises

Opened 19 November 2026.

- **Holdbart (Food):** Summa Equity, 12 December 2025: Holdbart buys surplus food that retailers cannot sell through normal channels and resells it at lower prices; “Holdbart’s model directly addresses this by purchasing and distributing surplus food”; “In 2025, the company expanded its store network to 23 locations across Norway, with ongoing work to secure future openings”; “In 2024 alone, these efforts resulted in 13,746 tons of food saved and 37,114 tons of avoided CO₂e emissions”; its COO and Head of Purchasing: “good food reaches people who need it”; with Summa Foundation it gave NOK 2 million in groceries and financial support to Matsentralen Norway and seven regional centres, https://summaequity.com/holdbart-and-summa-foundation-donate-nok-2-million-to-support-food-access-in-norway/
- **Forskerfabrikken (Education):** Ferd, annual report 2025, Social Entrepreneurs: it “works to increase children’s understanding of how science and technology shape individuals, society and nature”, through hands-on learning and nationwide programmes; “At year end 2025, FSE’s portfolio comprised ten direct investments, five fund investments and four outcome-based contracts”, https://aarsrapport2025.ferd.no/en/social-entrepreneurs/
- **KLAR (Education):** Ferd, annual report 2025: it “has developed a digital learning tool that introduces multilingual learners to the professional language” used in education, to help more students complete vocational upper secondary education and move into work; customers include county authorities, schools and adult education providers (page above).
- **Motitech (Health):** Ferd, annual report 2025: it “offers an innovative motivational tool encourages physical activity among older adults and people with dementia”, designed to prevent falls, improve health outcomes and enhance quality of life (page above).
- **Lifetools (Health):** Ferd, annual report 2025: it “has developed the digital communication tool KnowMe, which helps individuals with communication challenges” express themselves, with measurable gains in participation, self-expression and social inclusion (page above).
- **GET Academy (Livelihood):** Ferd, annual report 2025: it “offers young people outside the labour market a vocational education programme”: a 20-week intensive coding course followed by a year of mentored work placement (page above).
- **Gammel Nok (Livelihood):** Ferd, annual report 2025: it “delivers staffing solutions, practical services and home-based care to both private and public sector clients”; its workers are all aged 50 or over; it employs more than 400 senior workers, has its head office in Asker and has expanded to Hamar and Østfold (page above).
