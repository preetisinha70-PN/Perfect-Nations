The capital instruments register (15 lines, opened 26 November 2026):

Humanitarian
- **EU humanitarian aid for Myanmar, 2026, €38.6M.** European Commission (DG ECHO), 30 January 2026, https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/news-stories/news/eu-allocates-eu63-million-response-crisis-myanmar-and-its-impact-neighbouring-countries-2026-01-30_en. The EU “allocated €63 million in humanitarian aid”, of which more than €38.6 million for Myanmar and €23.4 million for Bangladesh; food assistance and emergency nutrition, shelter, water and sanitation, health care, mine risk education and education in emergencies.
- **WFP plan for Myanmar, 2026, $125M.** WFP, 11 December 2025, https://www.wfp.org/news/wfp-warns-myanmar-faces-rising-displacement-and-unacceptable-hunger-levels-2026: “requiring a budget of US$125 million”; “aims to assist 1.3 million people”; more than 12 million people “will face acute hunger in 2026”.
- **Myanmar Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan 2026, $890M.** ReliefWeb (OCHA), https://reliefweb.int/report/myanmar/myanmar-humanitarian-needs-and-response-plan-2026-enmy: “16.2 million people … require life-saving assistance”; “the Myanmar 2026 HNRP target has been set at 4.9 million people”; “The funding required to implement the plan amounts to $890 million”. UN in Myanmar press release, 10 December 2025, https://myanmar.un.org/en/node/306830: 2.6 million people prioritised at “US$521 million”. HNRP 2026 Q1 dashboard, https://myanmar.un.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/MMR%202026%20HNRP%20Q1%20dashboard.pdf: “$223M (25%)”, “Based on FTS records as of 31 March 2026”; “humanitarian partners reached 1.6 million people”. Humanitarian Update No. 52, 13 July 2026: “43 per cent of the US$890 million required has been received”.
- **UNICEF Humanitarian Action for Children, Myanmar 2026, $267.2M.** UNICEF, https://www.unicef.org/appeals/myanmar: “The UNICEF funding requirement is US$267.2 million”; “3.3 million people, including 2.6 million children”. UNICEF Humanitarian Situation Report No. 1, January to June 2026, https://www.unicef.org/myanmar/media/14426/file/UNICEF-Myanmar-Humanitarian-Situation-Report-No-1-January-to-June-2026.pdf.pdf: “By mid-2026, the funding level stood at US$95.4 million, with a gap of US$171.7 million (64 per cent)”.
- **Myanmar Earthquake: HNRP Flash Addendum 2025, $275.2M, Completed.** OCHA, https://humanitarianaction.info/plan/1399: published 11 April 2025; 1.1 million people targeted; requirement $275.2 million; $209.6 million received (76.2%) on 10 February 2026. The 2025 plan year has ended; Humanitarian Update No. 52 records “the concurrent response to earthquake-related needs following the March 2025 earthquakes” in 2026.
- **DEC Myanmar Earthquake Appeal, £28M.** Disasters Emergency Committee, six-month report, published February 2026, https://www.dec.org.uk/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-03/Myanmar%206%20month%20report.pdf: “launched a humanitarian appeal on 3 April 2025”; “has raised £28.0 million as of February 2026”, including £5 million from the UK government’s matching scheme; Phase 2 from October 2025 to March 2027; “Total 6-month reach: 533,000 people”.

- **Global Fund emergency funding for the earthquake response, $5M.** Global Fund, 14 April 2025, https://www.theglobalfund.org/en/news/2025/2025-04-14-global-fund-to-provide-usd5-million-in-emergency-relief-to-support-earthquake-response-in-myanmar/: “emergency funding allocation of US$5 million”; antiretroviral drugs, TB medications and malaria diagnostics.

## The enterprises

- **Proximity Designs (Food).** Cartier Philanthropy, published 4 August 2026, https://www.cartierphilanthropy.org/news/myanmar-farmers-tackle-a-burning-issue: “Proximity Designs – an organisation that helps smallholder farmers in Myanmar increase their productivity and incomes through innovative technologies and services”; farmers who adopted its method burned “up to 33% less during the 2024 monsoon season, and 74% less in the 2025 summer season”; “a household-level gain of USD 247 in the summer season and USD 301 in the monsoon season”. Also: Global Innovation Fund grant, 2024 (above). Earned income: MIT Solve submission, 2020, https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/31057: “earned income from the sale of our products and services” covered about 21% of operating expenses in FY20; “Since 2004, we’ve served 907,000+ farm households across rural Myanmar”.
- **MyFarmer Labs (Food).** SEARCA, 29 May 2025, https://www.searca.org/news/myanmar-startup-launches-digital-platform-smallholder-farmer-investments: the MyFarmer App, “the country's first crowd-farming platform”, “directly connects individual investors, known as digital farmers, with local smallholder farmers”; pilot in Southern Shan State, 10 farmers each receiving “USD 250”; “More than 500 farmers from over 20 villages took part” in onboarding events. Earned income not shown.
- **Nae Thit Kyan Mar (New Day Health) (Health).** Community Partners International, 15 August 2024, https://cpintl.org/type/impact-story/making-ncd-care-affordable-it-got-so-bad-i-had-to-have-my-leg-amputated: a social enterprise clinic in South Dagon Township, Yangon; “Its affordable NCD care covers prevention, diagnosis and treatment.”; “Patients and donors split the cost of treatment.”; “about 400 patients have enrolled for NCD services at our clinic”. Earlier: Community Partners International, 21 July 2023, https://cpintl.org/type/impact-story/health-care-as-a-social-enterprise-you-can-see-a-doctor-for-less-than-a-dollar: “Our revenue in the first nine months of operation covered about 20% of our operating costs”.
- **Early Dawn Microfinance (Livelihood).** FMO project disclosure 65457, effective 23 April 2025, published 6 October 2025, https://www.fmo.nl/project-detail/65457: “a leading microfinance institution in Myanmar and an existing FMO client”; “among the top five MFIs in the country, with over USD 90 million in assets”; FMO provides “the equivalent of EUR 10 million in local currency to support microloans for women with limited access to finance in (semi-)urban Myanmar”.
- **BRAC Myanmar Microfinance Company Limited (Livelihood).** BRAC USA, undated page (highlights of a 2024 survey of clients), https://www.bracusa.org/solutions/development/financial-inclusion/microfinance/brac-myanmar-microfinance-company-limited/: “Providing inclusive financial services to people living in poverty”; women’s microenterprise, agriculture and small enterprise loans; “124,846” borrowers; “265,597” savers; “99 %” of clients women. The figures carry no date.
