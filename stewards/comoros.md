## The seven standards

Not re-read at source on 17 November 2026.

## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Union des Meck (Livelihood):** the largest microfinance network; 76% of the credit granted by the sector (2023 data); its “Coup de Pouce” product allows borrowing up to set limits without collateral. Banque Centrale des Comores, Stratégie Nationale d’Inclusion Financière 2025–2030, https://banque-comores.km/uploads/Stratgie-Nationale-dInclusion-Financire-2025-2030.pdf ; 14 local branches across the three islands in the Central Bank’s report of 28 August 2026 (Financial Afrik, 3 September 2026, https://financialafrik.com/2026/09/03/comores-le-reveil-bancaire-dun-archipel-de-la-zone-franc/).
- **Union des Sanduk d’Anjouan (Livelihood):** 32 affiliated savings and credit banks on Anjouan. Financial Afrik, 3 September 2026, https://financialafrik.com/2026/09/03/comores-le-reveil-bancaire-dun-archipel-de-la-zone-franc/ ; 16% of the credit granted by the microfinance sector (2023 data), second after the Union des Meck (Banque Centrale des Comores, same strategy as above).
- **Dahari (Livelihood):** Comorian NGO on Anjouan; individual five-year conservation agreements under which farmers receive cash transfers every six months through local micro-credit associations, sized to the area conserved; 30 farmers in the pilot and about 300 more planned in phase two, subject to funding. Darwin Initiative, https://www.darwininitiative.org.uk/news/2024/09/19/farmers-commit-to-forest-conservation-in-comoros/ (dated 19 September 2024 in its address); a Cartier Philanthropy partner since 2023, to restore forest and protect water sources, with a target of at least 1,000 hectares of native forest by 2027 (https://www.cartierphilanthropy.org/partner/dahari).

## Open gaps

- Learning outcomes date from 2008.
- No enterprise yet verified for energy, the standard furthest from reach, nor for food, water, shelter, education or health.
- The approvals of the ASCENT and WASH grants rest on the Early Warning System records; the Project Appraisal Document of the WASH grant gives an expected approval date of 31 March 2026 and the ASCENT Project Information Document an estimated approval date of 24 April 2026.
- IMF press release 26/045 gives the fifth review disbursement as both “about US$ 4.92 million” and “about US$ 4.87 million”.
- The Comoros National Energy Compact (Mission 300) states that “Seulement 0,5 % de la population a accès à des technologies de cuisson propres”, a different measure from the World Bank clean cooking series (11%) used here.
- IMF press release 26/045 (11 February 2026) gives public debt of 32.9% of GDP for 2025 and projects growth of 4.1% for 2026; the hero and the record keep the newer World Economic Outlook of October 2026 (general government gross debt 30.5% for 2025; growth 2.9% projected for 2026), not re-opened on 17 November 2026.
- Debt by creditor (IDA and the Export-Import Bank of China) is reported only in the press so far; to read in the IMF-World Bank debt sustainability analysis of January 2026.
