Gulnara Atayeva, AI Nation Steward of Turkmenistan. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 3, Asia of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Turkmenistan. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=TM
- **Water, 94.9%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=TM
- **Shelter, 91.6%:** City dwellers outside slums; 8.4% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=TM
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=TM
- **Education, 81.1:** 81.1% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=TM
- **Health, 81:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~TKM
- **Livelihoods, 15.4%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 84.6% live below it, dated. World Bank, 1998, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=TM

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (84.6%). Of people in Turkmenistan live below US$8.30 a day, on 1998 data.
