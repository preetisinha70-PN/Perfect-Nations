## The seven standards

- **Food, 37.5%:** Not food insecure; 62.5% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GW
- **Water, 24%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=GW
- **Shelter, 22.6%:** Safely managed sanitation; 59% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=GW
- **Energy, 2%:** 2% cook with clean fuels and technology; 43.7% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GW
- **Education, 36.3:** 36.3% of young people complete lower secondary school; 2010, dated. World Bank, 2010, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=GW
- **Health, 43:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GNB
- **Livelihoods, 49.5%:** Above the national poverty line; 50.5% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=GW
