## The enterprises

None yet verified as operating in Guinea-Bissau now. Two candidates are documented in a 2022 case study and wait for a current source:

- **FRES Guiné-Bissau (energy):** fee-for-service solar home systems with pay-as-you-go mobile payments, founded in 2011, 3,248 customers and 13 energy shops by 2020, in Bafatá, Gabú, Quinara and Tombali. ALER, case study (© 2022), https://www.lerenovaveis.org/contents/lerpublication/aler_caso_de_estudo_fres_691.pdf
- **Bambadinca Community Energy Service, run by the Bambadinca Community Development Association (ACDB) (energy and water):** a solar-hybrid mini-grid of 312 kWp operating since July 2015, with more than 650 customers on prepayment and a social tariff; ACDB has managed the Bambadinca water supply since 2002. ALER, case study (published March 2022), https://www.lerenovaveis.org/contents/lerpublication/aler_caso_de_estudo_bambadinca_8706.pdf

No country amount is given.
