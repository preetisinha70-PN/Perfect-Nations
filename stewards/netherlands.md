## The seven standards

- **Food, 92.5%:** Not food insecure; 7.5% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=NL
- **Water, 100%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=NL
- **Shelter, 97.5%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=NL
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=NL
- **Education, 95.5:** 95.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=NL
- **Health, 85:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~NLD
- **Livelihoods, 85.5%:** Above the national poverty line; 14.5% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=NL

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (14.5%). Of people in the Netherlands live below the national poverty line.
