## The enterprises

Opened 19 November 2026.

- **Herenboeren (Food):** EU CAP Network, inspiring examples (June 2025): “22 operational farms”, “8 start-ups”, “almost 10.000 households engaged”, “20.000 civilian farmers”; local groups found a cooperative, collect 250 member households, rent land and hire farmers, https://eu-cap-network.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-06/eu-cap-network-tg-vfwcs-2nd-meeting-inspiring-examples-paul-05.pdf . Ashoka: Geert van der Veer, Fellow since 2017, is “putting the use of farm land back into the hands of local communities, using a model of collective ownership and management”; about 200 residents each invest €2,000 and hire a farmer; a foundation raises funds for people who cannot afford the investment, https://www.ashoka.org/en/fellow/geert-van-der-veer . European Social Fund Plus: “Herenboeren is a Dutch cooperative movement where households co-own and co-manage nature-driven farms”, https://european-social-fund-plus.ec.europa.eu/en/social-innovation-match/case-study/herenboeren
- **Phood Farm (Food):** Rabo Foundation, 27 June 2024: “Phood Farm is an Aquaponic Care Farm and Community Farm located in the southeast of Eindhoven”; “The products are grown hyper-locally and delivered directly to end consumers and businesses”; jobs and daytime activities for young people facing obstacles to work, such as autism, ADHD or burnout; a gift and then a social loan from the Rabo Foundation, https://www.rabobank.nl/en/about-us/rabofoundation/project/011433263/growing-opportunities-phood-farms-journey . Listed among the social enterprises on the Rabo Foundation’s page, https://www.rabobank.nl/en/about-us/rabofoundation/who-we-are/social-enterprises
- **Energie van Ons (Energy):** Solar Magazine, “15 juli 2026”: Energie VanOns and om | nieuwe energie “gaan per direct samen verder als de landelijke coöperatieve energieleverancier Energie van Ons”; “Door de krachten te bundelen, ontstaat er nu een landelijke coöperatieve beweging met meer dan 250 lokale energiecoöperaties.”; “lokaal opgewekte, 100 procent duurzame energie zonder winstoogmerk”, https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i44287/energieleveranciers-vanons-en-om-fuseren-tot-energie-van-ons . Interreg Europe good practice (30 January 2020): Energie VanOns “buys locally produced energy from local cooperatives and sells it to small end users”; “A share of the income will return directly to the energy cooperative.”, https://interregeurope.eu/good-practices/energie-vanons
- **Buurtzorg (Health):** “Buurtzorg (neighborhood care) was founded in 2006”; “In the first ten years Buurtzorg grew to over 10,000 nurses, in 850 teams in towns and villages all over the Netherlands.”; “more than 14,000 professionals today”; page footer “© 2026 Buurtzorg International”, https://www.buurtzorg.com/about-us/ . World Economic Forum, 2017: “units of up to 12 nurses, who are responsible for between 40 and 60 people within a particular area”, https://www.weforum.org/stories/2017/06/the-dutch-community-care-revolution/
- **Prins Heerlijk (Education):** Rabo Foundation, 9 July 2024: training centres where “eligible young people can learn to work in the catering, retail or healthcare sectors”; “almost 400 young people with a learning difficulty in 2023 alone”; each year 75 earn a vocational (MBO) diploma and 110 secure a paid job; supported since 2008, https://www.rabobank.nl/en/about-us/rabofoundation/project/011432563/learn-work-and-do-it-yourself-at-prins-heerlijk
- **Brownies&downieS (Livelihood):** autumn 2025 menu (“Menukaart najaar 2025”): “Colleagues at a distance from the labour market work in our cafés alongside employees with regular job prospects.”; 58 numbered locations, from Alkmaar to Zwolle, https://issuu.com/browniesanddownies/docs/en_menukaart_najaar_2025 . Since “1 september 2022” the “sociale franchiseformule Brownies&downieS” is part of Albron; then “54 vestigingen” and “ruim 1000 enthousiaste medewerkers” (nieuws.nl, https://nieuws.nl/nl12/browniesdownies-komt-in-handen-van-albron). Brownies&downieS Capelle is among the social entrepreneurs accepted by 27 municipalities under article 2.82 of the Public Procurement Act (Gemeente Den Haag, page changed 19 October 2026, https://www.denhaag.nl/nl/werk-bijstand-en-uitkering/inkopen-bij-een-sociaal-ondernemer/).
- **Oscar Circulair (Livelihood):** Rabo Foundation, 9 July 2024: “Oscar Circulair's aim is to solve two problems common to most cities: emissions and waste.”; it collects and sorts the waste of local businesses through eight entrepreneurs’ collectives in “Amsterdam and Rotterdam”; it employs “Oscars”, some in vulnerable positions on the job market, 14 of whom joined in 2023; supported since 2021, https://www.rabobank.nl/en/about-us/rabofoundation/project/011432551/towards-waste-free-cities-with-oscar-circular
