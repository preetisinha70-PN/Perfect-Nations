## The seven standards

Not re-read at source on 17 November 2026.

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Brunei. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=BN
- **Water, 100%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=BN
- **Shelter, 78.4%:** City dwellers outside slums; 21.6% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=BN
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=BN
- **Education, 100:** 100% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=BN
- **Health, 84:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~BRN
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for Brunei is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=BN

## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **SMARTER Brunei (Livelihood):** the Society for the Management of Autism Related issues in Training, Education and Resources runs four social enterprises: the SMARTER Autism Shop (2008, relaunched in March 2025), the SMARTER Bakery (2014), the SMARTER Bistro and Café (2017) and the BJAK Café, in partnership with the Ministry of Culture, Youth and Sports, to provide “real jobs, real wages and real pathways to independence for autistic individuals”. ## Watch items

- Government Sukuk Al-Ijarah: no series after the 263rd (6 August 2026) was found on 17 November 2026. The BDIF news page for the 263rd series gives BND18.55 billion issued since 2006; the central bank’s release gives BND18.60 billion.
- BDCB Islamic Bills (I-Bills), the central bank’s short-term Islamic bills: BND7.95 billion issued since the first offering on 22 October 2020; BND63.00 million outstanding on 3 September 2026 (266th issuance, 3 September 2026, https://cms.bdcb.gov.bn/storage/uploads/publications/17884845286379260.pdf).
- IMF, “Brunei Darussalam: 2026 Article IV Consultation-Press Release; Staff Report; and Statement by the Executive Director”, 25 September 2026: “The fiscal deficit is expected to remain large, highlighting structural imbalances.” https://www.imf.org/en/publications/cr/issues/2026/09/25/brunei-darussalam-2026-article-iv-consultation-press-release-staff-report-and-statement-by-579862
- Islamic Development Bank: Memorandum of Understanding for a strategic partnership and Country Engagement Framework for 2025 to 2030, signed 28 August 2024; priorities include Islamic finance, the SME ecosystem, climate change, capacity development, digitalisation and the empowerment of women and youth; no amount stated. https://www.isdb.org/news/islamic-development-bank-and-brunei-darussalam-sign-strategic-partnership
- Baiduri Bank: US$2 billion Euro Medium Term Note Programme established on 5 October 2026 and S$280 million 2.95% three-year senior unsecured notes (October 2026); the bank’s release states no use of proceeds tied to the seven standards.
- Baiduri Bank: solar financing for a 12 MWp ground-mounted plant at Sungai Liang Industrial Park (SPARK), developed by Hibiscus Petroleum, and earlier for the 30 MW Kampong Belimbing plant (The Star, 19 February 2026); no amount and no bank release read.
- Yayasan Sultan Haji Hassanal Bolkiah: Sara Diri (Self-Reliance) Programme, ASEAN Inclusive Business Award 2024 (145 participants, BND500,000 since 2015; Biz Brunei, 24 September 2024); Housing Assistance Scheme 2025–2026, five houses for families in need (Borneo Bulletin, carried by The Star, 23 May 2026).
- Projek FEED Management Services: social enterprise that mentors members of financially challenged families to start businesses; ASEAN Business Awards 2020, Inclusive Business; latest report found on a 2023 mentoring session (Borneo Bulletin).
