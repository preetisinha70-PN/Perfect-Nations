## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **171 GW of solar installed, September 2026:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, physical progress to 30 September 2026: solar 171,051.46 MW; renewables 299,246.53 MW (as listed in the page sources). No figure to 31 October 2026 had been published when this review was made.
- **99.9% of people with electricity, 2024:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, access to electricity (% of population), 2024, read 30 October 2026.
- **INR 15,000 crore of green bonds scheduled, October 2026 to January 2027:** Reserve Bank of India, issuance calendar, 25 September 2026 (the register line): five auctions of INR 3,000 crore; the total is arithmetic.
- **The lead, 1.46 billion:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (1,463,865,525), read 30 October 2026. “More than half its children could not read a simple text by the age of ten”: World Bank learning poverty, 56.1% (2017), as on the Education standard.

### The Sovereign Assets
- **The Sun, 171 GW, 30 September 2026; 299 GW of renewable capacity:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, as above.
- **The Grid, 99.9%, 2024; about 1,400 kWh a person a year:** World Bank, World Development Indicators (electricity access); Central Electricity Authority, General Review 2025, as reported by Powerline (per-person consumption 1,400 kWh, 2023-24).
- **The Land, 59.9% of land in agriculture, 2024; one person in eight undernourished:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, agricultural land; FAO prevalence of undernourishment, 12% (2023).
- **The Green Bonds, 7.65%, 1 October 2026:** Reserve Bank of India, auction result for the 7.50% GOI SGrB 2056, 1 October 2026 (cut-off yield 7.6539%), the register line.
- **The People, 1.46bn, 2025; nearly two in three outside the cities:** World Bank, World Development Indicators (population and urban share), read 30 October 2026.

The fiscal deficit is estimated at 4.3% of GDP and total expenditure at INR 53.5 lakh crore.
