## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **Hábitat para la Humanidad Honduras (Shelter):** “Entre 2022 y diciembre de 2025 ya se han ejecutado 2,972 pisos, beneficiando a 14,860 personas”; goal of 9,160 concrete floors by 2028 for about 45,000 people; each floor costs an estimated L13,000, with unskilled labour from the families. La Prensa, 2 March 2026, interview with its executive director, https://www.laprensa.hn/premium/vivienda-social-credito-habitat-humanidad-honduras-IN29535814 . Credit for homes and land repaid in instalments: Habitat for Humanity, Honduras country page (fiscal year 2023 figures), https://www.habitat.org/sites/default/files/documents/ESP_Honduras_AF23.pdf , opened 18 November 2026 (“a través de créditos con cuotas y plazos accesibles”).
- **Proyecto Mirador (Energy):** a clean cooking non-profit begun in 2004; 400,000 clean cookstoves installed in homes across Honduras and Guatemala, more than 1.6 million people; carbon credits issued under Gold Standard project IDs GS2758 and GS12798 (Honduras); AA (Tier 1) rating from Calyx Global. Business Wire, 7 April 2026, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260407434068/en/Proyecto-Mirador-Becomes-First-Latin-American-Cookstove-Project-to-Earn-AA-Rating-from-Calyx-Global
- **ASHONPLAFA, Asociación Hondureña de Planificación de Familia (Health):** a non-governmental organisation; services “through our clinical medical programs, social marketing and community services”; commitment to provide family planning to 400,000 women and 60,000 young people and adolescents, an increase of 15%. FP2030 commitment page, 17 March 2025, https://www.fp2030.org/?p=38581
- **Café Orgánico Marcala, COMSA (Livelihood):** “a leading social enterprise based in Marcala, La Paz”; agreement with Lutheran World Relief to reach 1,500 farming families in La Paz and Intibucá. Corus International, 25 August 2025, https://corusinternational.org/story-hub/strengthening-coffee-farmers-honduras-new-partnerships-molina-de-honduras-and-comsa . COMSA produces, processes and sells organic coffee and its associated producers can access its credit; Oikocredit partner since 2017 (Oikocredit, 16 July 2024, https://oikocredit.coop/l/fr/oikoproject/project/56586/pdf). Its producers’ association APROCOMSA, founded in 2023, has 1,095 members (Cooperative Coffees, https://coopcoffees.com/member/profile/943887).
- **Cooperativa ELGA, Coopelga (Livelihood):** “Cooperativa ELGA is the leading cooperative in Honduras, being the cooperative with the most assets in Honduras”, offering savings and credit services, with “a majority female membership”; an advisory agreement with IDB Invest, funded through We-Fi, to design a strategy to finance women-led SMEs. IDB Invest, 26 August 2024, https://idbinvest.org/en/news-media/idb-invest-partners-coopelga-promote-honduran-women-entrepreneurs-3
