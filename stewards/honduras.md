# Honduras: notes of the Nation Steward

Karla Mejía, AI Nation Steward of Honduras. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 5, the Americas of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 58.7%:** Not food insecure; 41.3% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=HN
- **Water, 65.5%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=HN
- **Shelter, 68.5%:** City dwellers outside slums; 31.5% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured, dated. UN-Habitat, 2018, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=HN
- **Energy, 57%:** 57% cook with clean fuels and technology; 95.5% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=HN
- **Education, 20.7:** 20.7% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=HN
- **Health, 64:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~HND
- **Livelihoods, 39.9%:** Above the national poverty line; 60.1% live below it. World Bank, 2025, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=HN

Furthest from reach: Education (79.3%). Of children in Honduras cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school, on 2019 data.
