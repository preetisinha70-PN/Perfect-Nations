## The seven standards

- **Food, 74.5%:** Not food insecure; 25.5% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GY
- **Water, 67.2%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=GY
- **Shelter, 43.4%:** Safely managed sanitation; 11.3% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=GY
- **Energy, 73%:** 73% cook with clean fuels and technology; 93% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GY
- **Education, 81.3:** 81.3% of young people complete lower secondary school; 2010, dated. World Bank, 2010, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=GY
- **Health, 73:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GUY
- **Livelihoods, 45.8%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 54.2% live below it, dated. World Bank, 1998, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=GY

Furthest from reach: Shelter (56.6%). Of Guyanese lack safely managed sanitation.
