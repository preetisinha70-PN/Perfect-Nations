## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Bahrain Food Bank (Food):** run by the Conserving Bounties Society; recovers surplus food and distributes it to families and low-income people; more than three million meals saved and distributed since 2014. GDN, 29 September 2026, https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/1407936/Food-bank-saves-three-million-meals-for-needy-families . Also listed as a social enterprise (“Conserving Bounties – Bahrain Food Bank”) by StartUp Bahrain, 9 April 2023, https://startupbahrain.com/blog/building-a-better-bahrain-the-importance-of-social-entrepreneurship
- **Wajba (Food):** platform that links food and beverage vendors with customers to sell surplus meals at lower prices; winner of the first Bahrain edition of Zain Great Idea. StartUp Bahrain, 16 October 2025, https://startupbahrain.com/blog/wajba-wins-zain-great-idea-bahrain-heads-to-silicon-valley . First place in the StartUp Bahrain Pitch series: ANBA, 22 August 2025, https://anba.com.br/en/bahrain-puts-its-startups-on-the-podium/
- **Ebdaa Bahrain Microfinance (Livelihood):** Bahrain’s first specialised microfinance institution, established 11 February 2009 at the initiative of the founder of AGFUND; more than 24,000 financing facilities worth over BD28 million, more than 10,000 of them to Bahraini women. TradeArabia, 16 February 2026, https://www.tradearabia.com/News/389302/Ebdaa-celebrates-17-years-of-supporting-entrepreneurs ; operating under the name Ebdaa Bahrain Microfinance, TradeArabia, 4 April 2026, https://www.tradearabia.com/News/413630/Ebdaa-Bahrain-Microfinance-names-acting-CEO/BANK
