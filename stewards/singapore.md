## The seven standards

- **Food, 90.5%:** Not food insecure; 9.5% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=SG
- **Water, 100%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=SG
- **Shelter, 100%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=SG
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=SG
- **Education, 96.6:** 96.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=SG
- **Health, 88:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~SGP
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for Singapore is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=SG

Furthest from reach: Food (9.5%). Of Singaporeans are moderately or severely food insecure.
