## The register (14 lines)

Sources opened on 17 November 2026.

- **World Bank, Horn of Africa Initiative: Djibouti Regional Economic Corridor, additional financing ($45M):** IDA grant, approved, announced 18 June 2026; total World Bank financing for the project US$205 million, after a US$70 million original project (December 2021) and US$90 million of additional financing (September 2024); widening of high-risk sections of National Road 1 between Arta and Doudoubala; 70 kilometres of roads to climate-resilient standards; more than 250,000 people with more resilient roads and a 30% cut in road deaths targeted by 2033. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/18/additional-financing-to-strengthen-djibouti-s-regional-economic-corridor
- **World Bank, Djibouti Economic Diversification Program ($25.75M):** IDA grant, the country’s first Program-for-Results financing, approved (dateline 27 March 2026); total envelope US$75.75 million over 2026 to 2034; the first operation in the region co-led by the World Bank and IFC. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/03/30/world-bank-group-financing-of-over-us-25-million-to-support-economic-diversification-in-djibouti
- **World Bank, Djibouti Groundwater Resilience and Water Supply Project, DJIRESA ($35M):** grant approved, announced 16 March 2026; rural water systems, operation and maintenance, a national Groundwater Information Management System; reliable basic water services for 127,000 people and climate resilience for at least 167,000. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/03/16/-35-million-grant-to-expand-safe-water-access-for-rural-communities-in-djibouti ; the Early Warning System record (P511492) gives an IDA grant of US$36.50 million and a voting date of 16 March 2026 (https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/projects/p511492-djibouti-groundwater-for-resilience-and-water-supp/).
- **World Bank and Global Partnership for Education, Expanding Opportunities for Learning Project, additional financing ($11.35M):** approved, announced 7 May 2025; IDA grant US$6.5 million and GPE grant US$4.85 million; total project financing US$41.7 million. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/05/07/new-world-bank-support-for-education-and-improved-learning-opportunities-in-djibouti
- **World Bank, Integrated Community Resilience Project ($31M):** approved 14 March 2025; includes US$9 million from IDA’s Window for Host Communities and Refugees; Dikhil, Obock and Ali Sabieh. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/03/14/new-project-to-strengthen-communities-and-support-vulnerable-groups-in-djibouti
- **World Bank, Economic and Financial Governance Project ($20M):** IDA grant approved 16 January 2025; tax collection, transparency of public spending, online government services. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/01/16/new-project-will-help-djibouti-enhance-public-finance-and-develop-digital-services
- **African Development Bank Group and Green Climate Fund, four agreements ($80M):** signed 5 February 2026; ADF grant US$22 million for seven kilometres of urban roads in Djibouti city (first phase of the Integrated Urban Infrastructure and Climate Change Adaptation Project); ADF grant US$30 million for a road axis of the Djibouti–Ethiopia–South Sudan Regional Transport Corridor; GCF grant US$14 million and GCF loan US$14 million under BREFOL. Financial Afrik, 10 February 2026, https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/02/10/african-development-bank-and-djibouti-sign-80-million-financing-agreements-to-strengthen-infrastructure-and-food-security/ (the African Development Bank’s own page, https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-and-djibouti-sign-agreements-80-million-strengthen-infrastructure-and-food-security-90724, could not be opened on 17 November 2026).
- **Islamic Development Bank, National Oncology Center ($26.1M):** financing agreement of US$26.10 million signed in Jeddah, 3 February 2026; total project cost US$28.35 million; outpatient cancer services for 6,000 people and inpatient services for 1,200 people each year. https://www.isdb.org/news/isdb-and-djibouti-sign-agreement-to-build-first-national-oncology-center
- **AFD and European Union, Djibouti City Sanitation Networks Extension and Rehabilitation Project, PERRAD (€40M):** second wastewater treatment plant inaugurated at Douda and work launched on the sewerage networks, 16 June 2025; total budget for the works in neighbourhoods 5, 6, 7, 7-bis and Cité Stade EUR 40 million; about twenty kilometres of networks; nearly 30,000 people to access the collective sanitation service; the first phase was co-financed with €20.2 million by AFD and the European Union from 2019. https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/djibouti/republic-djibouti-afd-and-european-union-are-partners-strengthening-sanitation-djibouti-city_en
- **AFD, health system reform support, Parss (€7M):** grant; one of three financing agreements signed with the Ministry of the Economy and Finance, announced 17 March 2023; quality of care in six public polyclinics. https://www.afd.fr/fr/actualites/djibouti-renforcer-secteurs-sante-energie-finance
- **AFD, energy efficiency in administrative buildings, Peeba (€7M):** grant to the Republic of Djibouti; 16 March 2023 to 16 March 2028; status “En cours”; about ten administrative buildings and a revolving fund. https://www.afd.fr/fr/projets/soutenir-lefficacite-energetique-dans-les-batiments-administratifs
- **AFD, performance of the ONEAD water service, Prospero (€10M):** AFD grant of €10 million with €5 million of EU funds delegated to AFD, announced 7 October 2022. https://www.afd.fr/fr/actualites/assainissement-djibouti-ue-et-afd-sengagent
- **FMO, Red Sea Power, Ghoubet wind farm ($16.67M):** senior secured loan to refinance a 60 MW wind power plant; effective date 21 December 2022; status “Approved investment”. https://www.fmo.nl/project-detail/61244

## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Agricultural cooperative of Antar, Damerjog (Food):** solar irrigation pumps for 6 lead farmers and 40 members; regular supplier to catering companies; of the 40 beneficiaries supported, 70% are women; sales up 50% and household income up 80%, with support financed by the China Merchants Foundation. UNDP Djibouti, Annual Report 2025 (April 2026), page 9, https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/undp_djibouti_annual_report2025.pdf
- **Agricultural cooperative of Dafenaytou (Food):** more than 70 members, the majority of them women, who hold half the board seats; family vegetable gardens and forage plots with simple irrigation and resistant seeds, under the IGAD project “The land is life”, funded by the African Development Bank through the Africa Climate Change Fund. Ecofin Agency (author listed as AfDB), 20 May 2025, https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/2005-46881-the-land-is-life-a-regional-project-supported-by-the-afdb-boosts-rural-womens-climate-resilience-in-djibouti
- **Medyc (Health):** e-health company launched in 2019; online consultations, home care, real-time glucose monitoring for diabetes; 1,200 people registered on its platform; medicines and test strips delivered to 300 to 500 patients. Gavi, VaccinesWork, 29 August 2024, https://www.gavi.org/fr/vaccineswork/doucement-mais-surement-sante-devient-numerique-djibouti ; teleconsultation platform whose largest client is the National Social Security Fund (World Bank, 20 December 2023, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2023/12/20/how-young-entrepreneurs-are-shaping-a-new-business-landscape-in-djibouti); We Are Tech Africa, 13 August 2024, gives 2020 as the year of creation (https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/a-djibouti-moubarik-mahamoud-facilite-l-acces-aux-soins-de-sante).
- **Caisse Populaire d’Épargne et de Crédit, CPEC (Livelihood):** microfinance network strengthened by the World Bank Group in its programme for women’s and youth entrepreneurship (P165558), under which about 4,000 beneficiaries accessed financing from 2018 to March 2024, 55% of them women. World Bank, 19 June 2026, https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/06/19/a-winning-bet-on-women-s-and-youth-entrepreneurship-in-djibouti ; created at the beginning of 2008, savings and small loans for people without access to banks (Djiboutian Agency for Social Development, 14 September 2014, https://www.adds.dj/en/adds-strengthens-the-popular-savings-and-credit/); “une coopérative de finance solidaire” (ADDS, 23 September 2012, https://adds.dj/addscpec-signature-dune-convention-de-financement-de-70-millions-de-fd).
- **Women’s agricultural cooperative of Guirori, Tadjourah (Livelihood):** fodder grown on communal land managed by the cooperative, benefits shared equally; an IGAD project equips 30 beneficiaries, mostly women, with equipment, drought-resistant seeds and training. IGAD, 3 December 2025, https://igad.int/?p=23123
