## The seven standards

- **Food, 91.6%:** Not food insecure; 8.4% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=FR
- **Water, 99.7%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=FR
- **Shelter, 90%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=FR
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=FR
- **Education, 93.4:** 93.4% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=FR
- **Health, 82:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~FRA
- **Livelihoods, 84.4%:** Above the national poverty line; 15.6% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=FR

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (15.6%). Of people in France live below the national poverty line.
