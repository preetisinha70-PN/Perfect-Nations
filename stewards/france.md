## The register (12 lines)

Sources opened on 18 November 2026.

- **Caisse des Dépôts, sustainable bond due 25 February 2031 (€1bn, issued):** launched and priced on 21 April 2026 after the announcement of 20 April; spread of 9 basis points above the OAT curve; final order book of €3.2 billion from more than 83 investors; the tenth ESG-labelled bond of the Caisse des Dépôts since 2017; issued under its Green, Social or Sustainable Financing Framework updated in July 2025 (Moody’s second-party opinion SQS2); current portfolio of eligible assets 46% social housing, 34% green real estate, 12% renewable energy, 7% sustainable transport and mobility and 1% health and social care, the social and solidarity economy and energy efficiency. Caisse des Dépôts, press release, https://www.caissedesdepots.fr/sites/cdc.fr/files/2026-04/28042026_PR_Caisse_des_D%C3%A9p%C3%B4ts_Sustainable%20Bond_1Md%E2%82%AC.pdf
- **French Republic, green OAT 3.80% 25 June 2037 (€10bn, issued):** launched by syndication on 14 April 2026; settlement 21 April 2026; yield 3.899%, price 99.106; total demand of more than €113 billion; lead managers Barclays, BNP Paribas, BofA, Crédit Agricole CIB, Deutsche Bank and Morgan Stanley; green OATs target State budget spending dedicated to mitigating climate change, with adaptation, biodiversity and pollution prevention. Agence France Trésor, https://www.aft.gouv.fr/en/node/13717 ; the 2026 programme announced a syndicated green OAT “with a shorter maturity than the existing nominal green OATs” (https://www.aft.gouv.fr/en/node/13632); green OATs outstanding €105.7 billion at 15 August 2026 (monthly bulletin no. 435).
- **French Republic, State financing programme 2026 (€310bn, plan):** medium and long-term issues net of buybacks of €310.0 billion; bonds linked to French and European inflation about 10% of net issuance; indicative programme of 30 December 2025. At 31 July 2026, 71.2% complete: €244.7 billion issued gross, €23.8 billion bought back, €220.8 billion net. Agence France Trésor, https://www.aft.gouv.fr/en/node/13632 ; progress from monthly bulletin no. 435, August 2026, https://www.aft.gouv.fr/files/medias-aft/7_Publications/7.2_BM/435_Monthly%20bulletin%20August%202026.pdf
- **European Union, recovery and resilience plan (€40.3bn grants, active):** entirely financed by RRF grants, no loans; Council approval 13 July 2021; updated 14 July 2023 with REPowerEU measures; 49.5% for climate objectives and 21.6% for the digital transition; pre-financing €5.1 billion, then payments of €10.3 billion, €7.5 billion (decision of 30 May 2024) and €3.26 billion, as listed on the Commission country page. European Commission, https://commission.europa.eu/business-economy-euro/economic-recovery/recovery-and-resilience-facility/frances-recovery-and-resilience-plan_fr . Final payment: the Commission assessed France’s fifth and final payment request of €6.1 billion positively on 29 September 2026, with all 176 milestones and targets fulfilled (press release IP/26/2007, read in the copy of Lovguiden, https://www.lovguiden.dk/en/det-offentlige/europa-kommissionen-press-corner/2026-09-29-kommissionen-godkender-frankrigs-sidste-udbetaling-paa-61-mia-euro); The Brussels Times reports on 30 October 2026 that the payment was made and that France has received 100% of the funding (https://www.brusselstimes.com/2384926/france-secures-final-e6-1-billion-in-eu-recovery-funds-after-reforms). The measures named include transport infrastructure, hydrogen, digital public services, cultural heritage, hospitals and health facilities, and the energy renovation of public and private buildings (Lovguiden copy).
- **European Investment Bank, Bordeaux University Hospital (€269M, signed):** loan agreement signed at the end of 2025, announced on 8 July 2026 during an EIB visit to Bordeaux; the first EIB loan to the hospital; first phase of a multi-year programme for 2024–2029; renovation, refurbishment, extensions and new buildings under 18 projects of the property master plan, with medical and non-medical equipment, IT and building energy efficiency. https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2026-241-france-la-bei-soutient-la-modernisation-du-centre-hospitalier-universitaire-de-bordeaux-avec-un-pret-historique
- **European Investment Bank, CDC Habitat second tranche (€250M, signed):** signed 30 June 2026 (project page), announced 3 July 2026; second tranche of a €500 million envelope approved in 2025, after a first tranche of €250 million in 2025; about 3,470 energy-efficient intermediate rental homes under income conditions, mainly in urban areas with strong rental pressure, for middle-income households, young workers and essential workers. https://www.eib.org/fr/press/all/2026-235-la-bei-accorde-une-deuxieme-tranche-de-250-millions-d-euros-au-groupe-cdc-habitat-pour-accelerer-le-developpement-de-logements-intermediaires-en-france
- **Council of Europe Development Bank, Project 40, Fondation Apprentis d’Auteuil (€40M, approved):** approved 19 June 2026; redevelopment of the foundation’s historic site, increasing the capacity, quality and sustainability of social, educational and housing facilities for vulnerable children, teenagers and young adults. https://coebank.org/en/project-financing/projects-approved-administrative-council/project-40-support-for-vulnerable-youth-and-families/ . Devdiscourse reports on 12 October 2026 that two loans of €40 million each from the EIB and the CEB, backed by an InvestEU guarantee, were signed, for care for young people in child protection and unaccompanied minors, a residence for young workers, social housing for families and a hospitality school, with more than 1,200 people expected to be supported each year (https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/business/3990626-paris-youth-and-family-support-project-secures-80-million-from-two-european-banks).
- **Council of Europe Development Bank, Crédit Coopératif (€150M, approved):** one of fourteen loans totalling €1.2 billion approved in June 2026; to “improve access to affordable healthcare, social services, education and professional development”. CEB news of 19 June 2026, https://coebank.org/en/news-and-publications/news/ceb-approves-fourteen-loans-totalling-1-2-billion-euros/
- **Council of Europe Development Bank, Caisse des Dépôts, specific housing and child protection (€200M, signed):** loan package signed on 11 March 2026 by the CEB Governor and the Chief Executive of the Caisse des Dépôts; for the Banque des Territoires’ financing of facilities for children in care and of housing and reception facilities for young people in care, people in severe hardship, women who are victims of violence, dependent elderly people, persons with disabilities, and students and young workers of limited means; cumulative CEB support for Banque des Territoires initiatives €650 million; three earlier loans of 2016–2021 co-financed more than 15,000 places in care facilities. https://coebank.org/en/news-and-publications/news/the-council-of-europe-development-bank-and-caisse-des-depots-strengthen-their-cooperation-in-support-of-child-protection-and-specific-housing-in-france/

- **European Union, European Social Fund Plus 2021–2027 (€6.7bn, approved):** the ESF+ share of €18.4 billion of cohesion policy funding for France in 2021–2027 (with €3.5 billion under the European Regional Development Fund and a Just Transition Fund of around €1 billion); job market access, skills, training and career guidance, and the fight against poverty and social exclusion, including “food and material assistance, including to children”. European Commission, press release IP/22/3368, Brussels, 2 June 2022, https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_22_3368/IP_22_3368_EN.pdf

## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **Groupe Ares (Livelihood):** “Depuis plus de 30 ans, le Groupe ARES lutte contre l’exclusion sociale et professionnelle”; “plus de 2000 personnes accompagnées chaque année”; rates of exit into employment or training of 65%, 66% and 67% in recent years. Groupe Ares, “Évaluation d’impact social 2023 des établissements IAE”, hosted by Avise, https://www.avise.org/sites/default/files/media/document/MIS%20Ares%202023%20-%20Rapport%20%28taille%20minimale%29.pdf ; a job offer on makesense dated June or July 2026 (no longer open) states that the group “a pour vocation de favoriser l’insertion de personnes en grande exclusion” (https://jobs.makesense.org/fr/jobs/groupe-ares-conseiller-en-insertion-professionnel-hf-tmumQLiEPHoYBt2ZM1WB).
- **Envie (Livelihood):** network of 53 enterprises and associations employing 3,780 people, 3,000 of them in work integration for thirteen months on average; it repairs, refurbishes and resells household appliances, and its school trains in logistics, repair workshops and sales. City of Paris, updated 9 December 2024, https://www.paris.fr/pages/insertion-formation-reparation-le-tierce-gagnant-de-l-ecole-envie-29537 ; the Banque des Territoires (Localtis, 18 April 2025) describes the network’s collection, recovery and refurbishment of electrical and electronic equipment by employees in work integration (https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/economie-circulaire-1000-emplois-menaces-dans-le-reseau-envie-suite-au-dernier-appel-doffres).
- **Phenix (Food):** certified B Corp since January 2019 (B Impact score 112.0); headquarters Île-de-France; a network of 12,000 merchants and 1,500 partner associations; “Phenix sauve plus 70 tonnes de produits chaque jour”; “150 millions de repas sauvés du gaspillage depuis sa création en 2014”. B Lab, https://www.bcorporation.net//en-us/find-a-b-corp/company/phenix
- **Foncière d’Habitat et Humanisme (Shelter):** created in 1986; “Elle dispose d’un agrément d’Entreprise Solidaire d’Utilité Sociale (ESUS)”; acquires and renovates homes for people in difficulty; “Le parc de logements mis en location s’élève à 6 235 logements à fin 2025”; 11,246 shareholders; €21.8 million raised in 2025; rents €23.2 million in 2025. Foncière d’Habitat et Humanisme, annual report 2025, https://www.habitat-humanisme.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ra-fonciere-hh-2025-0426-bd.pdf
- **Siel Bleu (Health):** founded in Strasbourg in 1997; adapted physical activity for older people, people with chronic illness, with disabilities or losing their autonomy; nearly 115,000 participants each week; 10,000 places of intervention in France; 816 employees in the field; the Banque des Territoires invested €1 million in associative bonds. Banque des Territoires, published 26 January 2026, https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/realisation/association-siel-bleu
- **Simplon (Education):** “Entreprise Solidaire d’Utilité Sociale (ESUS)”; a training organisation of the social and solidarity economy since 2013; mission “rendre accessibles les compétences et métiers du numérique à toutes et tous”; more than 40,000 people trained; 75.2% positive outcomes after training; 120 campuses in France and 24 countries. Simplon, makesense profile, https://jobs.makesense.org/fr/projects/simplonco-1534/transparency ; the Banque des Territoires (28 March 2022) describes it as the first network of free courses labelled “Grande École du Numérique”, ESUS-approved since 2013, with €2.25 million invested by the Banque des Territoires (https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/simplon-le-numerique-pour-tous).
