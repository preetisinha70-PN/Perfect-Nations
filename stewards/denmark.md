## The register (11 lines)

Sources opened on 17 November 2026, unless marked.

- **Kingdom of Denmark, government bond issuance programme 2026 (DKK 65bn, plan):** target for domestic government bond issuance at market value, kept for the second half of 2026; DKK 34.7 billion issued by auctions and tap sales to the end of June; green bonds expected to amount to a maximum of DKK 10 billion in proceeds in 2026, allocated to eligible green expenditures in 2025. Danmarks Nationalbank, on behalf of the Minister of Finance, 25 June 2026. https://www.nationalbanken.dk/media/40formk0/central-government-borrowing-strategy-2nd-half-2026.pdf ; the strategy for 2026 of 17 December 2025 set the same DKK 65 billion target (https://via.ritzau.dk/files/90446/14728767/321101/en).
- **Kingdom of Denmark, green government bond DGB 2.25% 2035G under the European Green Bond Standard (DKK 7bn):** issued 23 September 2025; due 15 November 2035; coupon 2.25%; greenium of 1.5 basis points; proceeds refinance expenditure on sustainable land use, renewable energy and clean transportation. Danmarks Nationalbank, market announcement, https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/news-and-knowledge/press/market-announcements/2025/the-government-has-issued-a-10-year-green-bond-under-the-eugb-framework ; the first green government bond under the European Green Bond Standard (Danmarks Nationalbank, “Central government borrowing and debt 2025”, 5 February 2026, https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/news-and-knowledge/publications-and-speeches/report/2026/central-government-borrowing-and-debt-2025).
- **Landsbyggefonden, social housing renovation framework 2020–2026 (DKK 30bn, plan):** DKK 30 billion in 2019 prices for 2020–2026; DKK 2.2 billion a year in 2019 prices for each of 2022–2026; about DKK 4.4 billion in 2019 prices remaining, about DKK 2.4 billion in 2025 prices in each of 2025 and 2026; a new framework to be negotiated for the years after 2026. Minister for Social Affairs and Housing, answer to question 95 of the Housing Committee, corrected 11 September 2025. https://www.ft.dk/samling/20241/almdel/bou/spm/95/svar/2156719/3066718.pdf
- **European Union, recovery and resilience plan (€1.63bn grants, active):** grants of EUR 1,625,890,885 and no loans; plan approved by Council implementing decision of 13 July 2021; amendment proposed by the Commission on 10 April 2026 with the financial contribution unchanged; Commission implementing decision of 24 July 2026 authorising the fifth instalment; 69% of the plan for climate objectives and 27% for the digital transition; plan value €1,812 million including national resources. European Commission, https://reforms-investments.ec.europa.eu/recovery-and-resilience-facility-1/country-pages/denmarks-recovery-and-resilience-plan_en ; Council document 8126/26, https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-8126-2026-INIT/en/pdf . Full receipt: the Commission announced on 31 July 2026 a final payment of €359 million, after which Denmark had received 100% of its €1.63 billion allocation, the first member state to do so (European Commission press release IP/26/1713, read in the copy of Lovguiden, https://lovguiden.dk/en/det-offentlige/europa-kommissionen-press-corner/2026-07-31-eu-udbetaler-186-mia-euro-til-belgien-bulgarien-danmark-og-slovenien , and in The Brussels Times, 31 July 2026, https://www.brusselstimes.com/2253973/denmark-becomes-first-eu-nation-to-complete-recovery-fund-plan-securing-e1-63b).
- **European Social Fund Plus, social inclusion call 2026 (DKK 79.4M, plan):** up to DKK 79.4 million from the European Social Fund Plus in the 2026 investment plan, for small and medium-sized firms that lack workers to hire unemployed people from the margins of the labour market; 300 firms, 400 people into jobs and 300 still employed after at least three months expected; call expected March 2026, applications due August 2026, recommendations to the board expected on 5 November 2026. Danish Board of Business Development, investment memo of 26 January 2026. https://udviklingidanmark.erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/sites/default/files/2026-05/Bilag%201.%20Investeringsopl%C3%A6g%20Social%20inklusion.pdf
- **European Union, European Social Fund Plus 2021–2027 (€120M):** the ESF+ share of Denmark’s €808 million cohesion policy strategy for 2021–2027; skills, an inclusive labour market and support for people on the margins of the labour market, including social integration and combatting homelessness. European Commission, 30 May 2022. https://european-social-fund-plus.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-adopts-partnership-denmark-2021-2027
- **Nordic Investment Bank, Sindal Biogas A/S (DKK 182M):** 13-year loan, about €24.5 million; listed among NIB’s signed loans with the date 4 August 2026; announced 5 August 2026; to double biomethane output from 22 to 44 million Nm³ a year, with possible CO₂ capture and liquefaction. https://www.nib.int/news/nib-finances-biogas-expansion-in-denmark ; loan page https://www.nib.int/loan/sindal-biogas-a-s-83755
- **European Investment Bank, Danske Bank pan-EU wind power package (€250M):** guarantees for advance payments and performance bonds linked to supply contracts of wind equipment manufacturers in the EU, including grid interconnector infrastructure; signed 10 July 2026; total cost about €4,000 million. EIB project 20240611, https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20240611
- **European Investment Bank, Ringkjøbing Landbobank loan for SMEs II (€80M):** signed 9 July 2026 (release date 5 June 2026); on-lending to SMEs and mid-caps, mainly in Denmark, with a climate action and environmental sustainability window; at least 15% of the resulting investments expected for climate action. EIB project 20260127, https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20260127
- **Nordic Investment Bank, Ringkjøbing Landbobank A/S (DKK 700M):** 10-year loan, announced 26 June 2026; on-lending to environmental projects, SMEs and small mid-caps in Denmark; at least 50% of the loan funds for eligible environmental projects. https://www.nib.int/news/nib-supports-environmental-projects-smes-and-smcs-through-ringkjobing-landbobank-in-denmark/
- **Nordic Investment Bank, Solar Park Kvosted ApS (€21.5M):** 11-year loan, announced 11 February 2026; a 50 MW/200 MWh battery energy storage system at the existing 100 MWp solar park in Viborg Municipality, Central Jutland; supported by the InvestEU programme. https://www.nib.int/news/nib-finances-battery-storage-addition-to-solar-park-in-denmark

## The enterprises

Opened 17 November 2026.

- **Specialisterne (Livelihood):** “Founded in Denmark in 2004 by Thorkil Sonne, Specialisterne has grown to operate in twenty-six countries around the world”; “10,000+ neurodivergent individuals have been hired”; named to the Forbes Accessibility 200 in 2026; office in Denmark. Specialisterne, https://specialisterne.com/ (page last modified 3 June 2026); its method of identifying candidates, assessing their skills in assessment centres and training them for jobs is described by the European Social Fund Plus, Social Innovation Match, https://european-social-fund-plus.ec.europa.eu/en/social-innovation-match/case-study/specialisterne-foundation
- **Væksthuset (Livelihood):** “Væksthuset er en socialøkonomisk virksomhed og en erhvervsdrivende fond” (a social enterprise and a commercial foundation); works with people to bring them into jobs or education; framework agreements with the City of Copenhagen for 2024–2025 for adults and young people, with hybrid internships and paid hours. City of Copenhagen, “Rammeaftaler for samarbejdet mellem Københavns Kommune og Væksthuset, 2024-2025” (experience summary 2025, published in May 2026), https://bif-indkoeb.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-05/V%C3%A6ksthuset%20-%20Udsatte%20Unge%20og%20voksne%20samt%20specialiseret%20indsats%20-%20Erfaringsopsamling%202025.pdf
- **Hus Forbi (Livelihood):** street newspaper of homeless sellers, who earn 25 kroner on each paper sold; seven in ten sales through MobilePay; an agreement of May 2025 gives sellers a tax deduction of DKK 42,766 so that they do not pay tax on their sales. Altinget, 5 May 2025, https://www.altinget.dk/social/artikel/skrappe-hvidvaskregler-var-ved-at-slaa-hus-forbi-ihjel-men-med-nybrud-i-skattelovgivningen-har-avisen-faaet-rygstoed-til-at-fortsaette
- **Gladteknik (Livelihood):** IT company that creates work and community for people with disabilities; received the disability prize of the Minister for Social Affairs and Housing on 3 December 2025. Altinget, 3 December 2025, https://www.altinget.dk/social/artikel/socialministerens-handicappris-gaar-til-glad-teknik
- **FødevareBanken (Food):** since 2008 it passes surplus food from the food industry on to social organisations; 2025: 6.5 million meals and 487 social recipient organisations; MadPlus, launched with Salling Group in 2025, collects surplus food from 520 Netto, føtex and Bilka stores (1,137 tonnes in 2025). FødevareBanken, press release, 21 May 2026, https://via.ritzau.dk/pressemeddelelse/14940828/fodevarebanken-og-salling-group-gor-succesfuld-model-til-ny-national-standard-mod-madspild
