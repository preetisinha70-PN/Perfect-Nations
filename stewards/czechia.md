Včelka brochure, https://media.vcelka.cz/Vcelka-Brozura-Tipy-A-Triky.pdf (undated); “Společnost Včelka aplikaci stále rozvíjí” (the company Včelka keeps developing the app), Charles University master’s thesis record, September 2025, https://dspace.cuni.cz/handle/20.500.11956/203714

Interview with its chairman in Statistika&My, June 2026, as published by Kurzy.cz, 24 June 2026, https://www.kurzy.cz/tema/10080561.html
