## The seven standards

- **Food, 90%:** Not food insecure; 10% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CZ
- **Water, 98.1%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=CZ
- **Shelter, 91.4%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CZ
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CZ
- **Education, 85.8:** 85.8% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=CZ
- **Health, 83:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CZE
- **Livelihoods, 89.8%:** Above the national poverty line; 10.2% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=CZ

Furthest from reach: Education (14.2%). Of children in Czechia cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school.
