Opened 17 November 2026.

- **FINCA Azerbaijan (Food):** microfinance under Central Bank licence since 1999; Hybrid Agricultural Insurance Loan covering half of farmers’ insurance premiums, recognised at COP30 by the Microfinance Centre and the Azerbaijan Micro-Finance Association. https://mfc.org.pl/?p=35813 ; rural residents 70% of clients and nearly three-quarters of a million people served, https://finca.az/en/impact/ ; news of 12 November 2026, https://finca.az/en/news/
- **Viator (Livelihood):** microcredit institution since 1999, begun as a project of the Norwegian Humanitarian Enterprise; Grameen Crédit Agricole Foundation partner since 2011. https://gca-foundation.org/en/organisation/viator-2
- **Kashalata Café (Livelihood):** the first inclusive café in Baku, initiated by the public association Birgə və Sağlam; 20 young people employed, some with autism spectrum disorder and other neurodiverse characteristics. Trend, 11 August 2026, https://www.trend.az/business/4214312.html
