## The seven standards

- **Food, 86%:** Not food insecure; 14% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=AZ
- **Water, 57.8%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=AZ
- **Shelter, 68%:** Safely managed sanitation; 26.9% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=AZ
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=AZ
- **Education, 62.5:** 62.5% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=AZ
- **Health, 67:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~AZE
- **Livelihoods, 64.1%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 35.9% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2005, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=AZ

Furthest from reach: Water (42.2%). Of Azerbaijanis lack safely managed drinking water.
