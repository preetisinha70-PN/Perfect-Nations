## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **$20B, Mozambique LNG, restarted; the lead, a $20 billion gas project:** the Journal of Petroleum Technology reported on 6 November 2025 that TotalEnergies plans to restart Mozambique LNG “with a budget pegged at $20.5 billion” (https://jpt.spe.org/totalenergies-lifts-force-majeure-on-mozambique-lng, opened 9 November 2026). This is a secondary source. TotalEnergies’ own releases give no project cost: the release of 29 January 2026 (https://totalenergies.com/system/files/documents/totalenergies_pr-mozambique-LNG-announces-full-restart-all-activities-onshore-and-offshore-in-mozambique_2026_en.pdf, opened 9 November 2026) records the lifting of force majeure on 7 November 2025, 40% progress and first LNG expected in 2029; the clarification of 2 December 2025 (as linked in the register) gives project financing of USD 15.4 billion concluded in 2020.
- **82.7%, renewable share of electricity output:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, renewable electricity output (% of total electricity output), 2021, accessed 12 October 2026.
- **36.9%, with electricity, 2024:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, access to electricity (% of population), 2024, accessed 12 October 2026, confirmed on the World Bank country data page on 9 November 2026. A World Bank results story of 2 September 2025 gives a different measure: access “from 31 percent in 2018 to 60 percent by the end of 2024”.
- **The lead, nearly two in three below the national poverty line:** World Bank, Mozambique overview, https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/mozambique/overview (last modified 25 September 2026, opened 9 November 2026): “the national poverty rate rising from 48.4 % in 2014/15 to 62.9 % in 2022”.
- **The lead, a nation of 35.6 million:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (35,631,653, the `population` of the nation file), accessed 12 October 2026.
