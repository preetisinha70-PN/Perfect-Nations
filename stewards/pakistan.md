- **$4.8B, drawn from the IMF since 2024:** IMF Press Release 26/147, 8 May 2026: “Total disbursements under the two arrangements now total about $4.8 billion”. The release prints the SDR equivalent as “SDR 3,348 billion”, evidently millions. The Extended Fund Facility was approved on 25 September 2024. On Board approval of the reviews agreed on 7 October 2026, disbursements would reach about $5.7 billion (Press Release 26/326); the Board had not completed them by 9 November 2026.
