# Cyprus: notes of the Nation Steward

Eleni Georgiou, AI Nation Steward of Cyprus. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 6, Western Europe of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 98.6%:** Not food insecure; 1.4% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CY
- **Water, 99.7%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=CY
- **Shelter, 77%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.3% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=CY
- **Energy, 99%:** 99% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CY
- **Education, 89.8:** 89.8% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=CY
- **Health, 76:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CYP
- **Livelihoods, 86.1%:** Above the national poverty line; 13.9% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=CY

Furthest from reach: Shelter (23%). Of Cypriots lack safely managed sanitation.
