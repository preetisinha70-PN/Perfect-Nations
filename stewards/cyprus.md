## The enterprises

- **Food Connect, Friends of the Earth Cyprus (Food):** a free app that links hotels, restaurants, bakeries, supermarkets and food producers with surplus food to charities and the public, who reserve and collect it at no cost; launched in July 2026; operates in Cyprus, Malta and Portugal under the EU Life programme. Cyprus Mail, 23 July 2026, https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/07/23/food-connect-app-launched-to-tackle-cyprus-food-surplus (opened 17 November 2026).
- **Tylliria Energy Community (Energy):** a not-for-profit energy community of the local councils of Kato Pyrgos, Pano Pyrgos, Pachyammos, Mosfili and Pigenia; established September 2025 and registered November 2025; solar panels and storage across about 200 buildings with a €2.2 million public-private grant; over 100 applications in Phase A; technical support from the Cyprus Energy Agency. FEDARENE, 2026, https://fedarene.org/best-practice/the-tylliria-energy-community-a-milestone-for-communities-led-energy-in-cyprus/ (opened 17 November 2026).
- **Agia Skepi Therapeutic Community (Health):** Cyprus’s first and only residential therapeutic community for people with long-term substance dependence, established in 1999 as an initiative of Machairas Monastery; its certified organic products are sold in supermarkets and organic stores across Cyprus; production gives members work experience and vocational skills, and it employs programme graduates. Cyprus Business Now, 15 August 2026, https://www.cbn.com.cy/article/132628 (opened 18 November 2026).
- **True Heart Café, Voice for Autism (Livelihood):** opened in May 2026 at 10B Yiannis Kranidiotis Street, Nicosia, as the country’s first registered social enterprise; eighteen individuals with support needs have completed training and work in the café; profits are reinvested in training. Cyprus Mail, 15 May 2026, https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/05/15/cyprus-opens-its-first-social-enterprise-with-the-launch-of-true-heart-cafe (opened 17 November 2026).
