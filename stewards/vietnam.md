## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **Up to $5B, portfolio flows from the index upgrade, World Bank estimate:** World Bank feature “Viet Nam: Equity Upgrade Takes Off”, 9 October 2026 (https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/10/09/viet-nam-equity-upgrade-takes-off, opened 11 November 2026): “World Bank estimates suggest the upgrade could generate up to US$5 billion in portfolio flows in the first few years.” A projection, labelled as such. The figure is the World Bank’s own; it is not a FTSE Russell figure.
- **The lead, a stock market that joined the FTSE emerging-market indices in September 2026:** FTSE Russell, results of the September 2025 country classification review, 7 October 2025 (https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/ftse-russell/2025/ftse-russell-country-classification-september-2025): Vietnam “will be reclassified from Frontier to Secondary Emerging market status”, “with an effective date of Monday 21 September 2026”, subject to an interim review in March 2026. FTSE Russell, “Why Vietnam is joining the FTSE GEIS”, 6 May 2026 (https://www.lseg.com/en/insights/ftse-russell/why-vietnam-is-joining-the-ftse-geis): reclassification confirmed on 7 April 2026; inclusion “will occur in four tranches, beginning in September 2026 and concluding in September 2027”. The World Bank feature above: 27 Vietnamese blue-chip stocks “joined the FTSE Global All Cap index on September 21, 2026”.
- **101.6m citizens, 2025; the lead, a nation of 101.6 million:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (101,598,527, the `population` of the nation file); not re-read.
- **91%, cook with clean fuel; the lead, nine in ten cook with clean fuel:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, access to clean fuels and technologies for cooking (EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS), 2024; not re-read.

### The Sovereign Assets
- **The Power, 45% of electricity from renewables, 2023:** IRENA, Viet Nam statistical profile, “Last updated on: 22 September, 2025” (https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Asia/Viet-Nam_Asia_RE_SP.pdf, opened 11 November 2026): electricity generation in 2023, renewable 120,302 GWh of 267,984 GWh (IRENA shows 45%; 44.9%, arithmetic); hydro and marine 81,727 GWh (30%), solar 25,696 GWh (10%), wind 11,593 GWh (4%), bioenergy 1,286 GWh. Renewables were 19% of total energy supply in 2022 (22% in 2017). It replaces “41.2%, of energy from renewables, 2021”, which IRENA does not support as a share of energy.
