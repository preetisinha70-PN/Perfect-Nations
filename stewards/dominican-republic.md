## The enterprises

Opened 18 November 2026.

- **DREAM Project (Education):** a north-coast non-profit founded in 1995 that “serves over 7,000 children and youth each year” (18 August 2023); one of six winners of the UNESCO International Literacy Prizes (30 October 2023); its My Very Own Library reading programme “will be implemented in rural public schools, including Pueblo Viejo, Sánchez Ramírez” (31 March 2025); Cabarete, Puerto Plata. DREAM Project, https://www.dominicandream.org/news/tag/Development+Social+Impact ; STEAM classrooms for more than 580 children in Callejón de la Loma and La Ciénaga with JustUsedTech, https://www.dominicandream.org/news/dream-project-steam-classrooms-justusedtech
- **Instituto 512 (Education):** with Mentu, launched Lucero, “una plataforma integral de herramientas basadas en inteligencia artificial” of six tools (four for teachers, two for management teams), piloted in 25 schools of the Modelo FARO in the 2025–2026 school year, for an estimated 189 teachers, 61 members of management teams and more than 4,500 students. El Nuevo Diario, 14 October 2025, https://elnuevodiario.com.do/instituto-512-y-mentu-impulsan-lucero-para-fortalecer-la-ensenanza-y-la-gestion-educativa/ ; also CDN, 14 October 2025, https://cdn.com.do/nacionales/lucero-para-fortalecer-la-ensenanza-y-la-gestion-educativa/ . Its Diplomado en Liderazgo y Gestión Educativa, begun in 2014, certified 259 school leaders in 2021, with more than 1,000 expected in all (Acento, 25 October 2021, https://acento.com.do/actualidad/instituto-512-certifica-a-259-lideres-del-sistema-educativo-dominicano-en-liderazgo-y-gestion-8998794.html); it reported work in more than 225 schools in the 18 education regions (El Dinero, 29 March 2022, https://eldinero.com.do/192328/fundacion-rica-inicia-educacion-e-instituto-512-en-alianza-por-la-mejora-continua-de-la-educacion/).
- **Perfecto Labs (Education):** runs the Perfectico AI Incubator, “a student-led tech incubator” for youth aged 10 to 18 in underserved communities, headquartered in Yamasá; non-profit; semi-finalist in the 2025 Global Learning Challenge; profile updated 17 July 2025. MIT Solve, https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/95823
- **Hábitat para la Humanidad República Dominicana (Shelter):** “la sustitución de 2476 pisos de tierra o en mal estado por pisos de concreto”, 1,407 of them in fiscal year 2024; its housing microfinance programme has mobilised US$25.6 million and reached more than 8,000 families; agreement with IDB Lab to “implementar la modalidad de pago por resultados (PPR) en la República Dominicana” in sanitation. Habitat for Humanity, “Alianzas e innovación: República Dominicana impulsa mejoras en vivienda y saneamiento” (2024), https://www.habitat.org/lac-es/node/58721
- **Banco Adopem (Livelihood):** savings and credit bank since 2004, grown from ADOPEM, the Asociación Dominicana para el Desarrollo de la Mujer, an NGO founded in 1982 for microcredit to women; 89% of its women clients are considered economically vulnerable; 67% of active loans in the small-business portfolio are held by women. IDB Invest, DEBrief No. 35, August 2025, https://www.idbinvest.org/en/download/25278 ; 147,266 loans for RD$9,332 million disbursed in 2024 (Acento, 21 May 2025, https://acento.com.do/economia/banco-adopem-otorgo-147266-prestamos-en-2024-9500626.html); a five-year gender bond for the peso equivalent of US$15 million subscribed by IDB Invest, March 2024 (Acento, 9 March 2024, https://acento.com.do/economia/bid-invest-y-banco-adopem-lanzan-el-primer-bono-de-genero-en-republica-dominicana-9313591.html).
