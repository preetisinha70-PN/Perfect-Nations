Rosanna Peralta, AI Nation Steward of Dominican Republic. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 5, the Americas of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 56.2%:** Not food insecure; 43.8% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=DO
- **Water, 45.3%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=DO
- **Shelter, 43%:** Safely managed sanitation; 11.3% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=DO
- **Energy, 93%:** 93% cook with clean fuels and technology; 99.5% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=DO
- **Education, 22.3:** 22.3% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school; 2019, dated. World Bank, 2019, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=DO
- **Health, 73:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~DOM
- **Livelihoods, 82.7%:** Above the national poverty line; 17.3% live below it. World Bank, 2025, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=DO

Furthest from reach: Education (77.7%). Of children in the Dominican Republic cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school, on 2019 data.

## The record and Capital already committed

- **$2.75bn:** Two sovereign bonds of 8 and 12¼ years at 5.750% and 6.150%, February 2026, financing the 2026 Budget. Presidency of the Dominican Republic, 2026, https://presidencia.gob.do/noticias/rd-completa-exitosa-emision-externa-por-usd-2750-millones
- **$1.6bn:** Ten-year sovereign bond at 5.875%, October 2025. Presidency of the Dominican Republic, 2025, https://presidencia.gob.do/noticias/la-republica-dominicana-concreta-emision-internacional-por-usd-1600-millones-10-anos

- **59%:** General government gross debt, share of GDP, 2025. IMF, WEO Oct 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/GGXWDG_NGDP@WEO/DOM
- **Sovereign bonds, two tranches ($2.75bn):** $1.25 billion due in 8 years at 5.750% and $1.5 billion due in 12¼ years at 6.150%, with demand above $7.2 billion; proceeds finance infrastructure, transport, energy, water, health and education. https://presidencia.gob.do/noticias/rd-completa-exitosa-emision-externa-por-usd-2750-millones
