- **REDI Recycling (Livelihood):** Civil Today, “May 31, 2025”: collects “selected non-hazardous waste” from about 400 residential and commercial buildings and carries it by electric tricycle to recycling depots; its director: “it became a social enterprise”, https://civil.today/zekir-the-woman-who-collected-plastic-bottles-ignited-the-spark-for-redi-recycling/ . Also listed as “REDI Recycling”, North Macedonia, among New European Bauhaus Prizes applications, https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/application/17286
- **Reto Nadezh (Health):** ARNO brochure (above): “this social enterprise, which is also a center for addiction rehabilitation”, in Batinci, Studenichani Municipality; “Customers come to the store to find repaired cabinets, closets, armchairs, desks, sofas, or chairs”; “The funds from the sale go to the mission of Reto Nadezh”; “Work therapy of 30-40 residents annually”; “In early 2025, the center had four mentors”.
- **In Vivo (Green Vivo) (Livelihood):** ARNO brochure (above): Sopishte Municipality, Skopje; “Since then, In Vivo has been developing its own brand of biohumus, Green Vivo”; “Financial self-sustainability through sale of biohumus”; “We have a special focus on homeless people and former persons deprived of their liberty”; capacity of 15 to 16 tons of bio-humus a year.
