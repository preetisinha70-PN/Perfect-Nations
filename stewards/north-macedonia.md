## The seven standards

- **Food, 84.8%:** Not food insecure; 15.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=MK
- **Water, 80.8%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=MK
- **Shelter, 12.1%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.3% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=MK
- **Energy, 87%:** 87% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=MK
- **Education, 67:** 67% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=MK
- **Health, 69:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~MKD
- **Livelihoods, 78.1%:** Above the national poverty line; 21.9% live below it. World Bank, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=MK

Furthest from reach: Shelter (87.9%). Of people in North Macedonia lack safely managed sanitation.
