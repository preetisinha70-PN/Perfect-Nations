- **Government of Grenada, restructured international bonds due 2030, natural disaster clause (US$12M deferred):** two near-term payments, due November 2024 and May 2025, deferred under a disaster clause and added to the lump-sum payments due at maturity in 2030; “the first time such a clause has been invoked”; US$12 million, 0.9% of 2024 GDP (Box 1). IMF, Grenada: 2025 Article IV Consultation, staff report, January 2026, https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1grdea2026001-source-pdf.pdf ; the Ministry of Finance names “two instruments for which payments were deferred under arrangements triggered by Hurricane Beryl”, to be “honoured in line with the revised terms” (Debt Report 2025).
- **Green Climate Fund, FP325 CABRIC in the Caribbean (US$42.04M grant, approved):** approved by the GCF Board on 28 October 2026 (B.46); accredited entity the Caribbean Community Climate Change Centre; the Bahamas, Belize, Grenada, Jamaica and Saint Lucia; US$2,009,100 in-kind co-financing; about 983,000 people expected to gain resilience; small, medium and large grant windows for locally led adaptation, last-mile early warning and resilient shelters and clinics. The share for Grenada is not stated. https://www.greenclimate.fund/portfolio/projects/fp325
- **World Bank (IDA), Caribbean Resilient Renewable Energy Infrastructure Investment Facility (US$20M for Grenada, approved):** approved 17 April 2025, P180831; a US$110 million regional facility for Grenada, Saint Lucia, and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines; partial credit guarantees to mobilise up to US$120 million of commercial credit; battery storage and grid modernisation (World Bank press release, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/04/17/caribbean-countries-strengthen-energy-resilience-via-new-facility). - **World Bank (IDA) and Clean Technology Fund, Caribbean Efficient and Green Energy Buildings Project (US$48.5M, approved):** approved 28 February 2025, P179519; IDA concessional financing of US$40 million and a Clean Technology Fund loan of US$8.5 million to Grenada; part of a US$131.87 million project with Guyana and Saint Lucia; public buildings and renewable energy systems. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/03/04/caribbean-countries-move-toward-energy-sustainability-with-new-regional-project ; terms in the Debt Report 2025 (Table 5): 40-year maturity, 10 years’ grace, IDA at 1.2% fixed.
