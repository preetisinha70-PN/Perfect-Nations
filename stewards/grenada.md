## The seven standards

- **Food, 82.7%:** Not food insecure; 17.3% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GD
- **Water, 89.9%:** Safely managed drinking water, dated. WHO/UNICEF, 2017, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=GD
- **Shelter, Not measured:** Neither safely managed sanitation nor urban slums is measured for Grenada. WHO/UNICEF, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=GD
- **Energy, 96%:** 96% cook with clean fuels and technology; 95.6% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GD
- **Education, 89.5:** 89.5% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=GD
- **Health, 78:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GRD
- **Livelihoods, 75%:** Above the national poverty line; 25% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2018, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=GD

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (25%). Of Grenadians live below the national poverty line, on 2018 data.
