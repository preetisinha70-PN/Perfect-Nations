## The register (10 lines)

Each line links its own source. Opened on 23 November 2026.

- **Promoting Sustainable Land Management to Achieve Land Degradation Neutrality, GEF, $8.4M (Concept approved):** GEF project 12080 (https://www.thegef.org/projects-operations/projects/12080): agency United Nations Development Programme; status “Concept Approved” on 3 June 2026 (received by the GEF on 16 September 2025); GEF project grant USD 8,401,400; co-financing USD 30,600,000; GEF Trust Fund; executing agency Ministry of Land, Water and Environment. UNDP Eritrea, “Eritrea advances climate resilience and sustainable land management through new investment”, 8 July 2026 (https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-advances-climate-resilience-and-sustainable-land-management-through-new-investment): the GEF “has recently approved” USD 8.4 million; five years; 6,294 hectares of degraded land restored; about 11,851 hectares under improved management; more than 25,000 people expected to benefit, nearly half of them women.
- **ASCENT-GREEN: Resilient Energy Access for Inclusive Development (FP291), Green Climate Fund and World Bank (Approved):** GCF project page (https://www.greenclimate.fund/project/fp291): 21 countries, Eritrea among them; GCF financing USD 250,000,000 (a USD 50,000,000 loan and grants of USD 160,000,000 and USD 40,000,000); co-financing USD 445,000,000; total USD 695 million; approved at B.44 on 28 March 2026; funded activity agreement executed 11 May 2026. No share for Eritrea is published. The GCF country page for Eritrea (https://www.greenclimate.fund/countries/eritrea) lists FP291 with the date 17 April 2026.
- **Community Based Marine and Coastal Ecosystem Restoration, Least Developed Countries Fund, $8.93M (Concept approved):** GEF project 11886 (https://www.thegef.org/projects-operations/projects/11886): agency International Fund for Agricultural Development; status “Concept Approved” on 19 December 2025; GEF grant USD 8,932,420; co-financing USD 22,271,000; Least Developed Countries Fund; executing agencies Ministry of Marine Resources and Ministry of Land, Water and Environment. Households and mangroves as reported by Devdiscourse, 23 December 2025 (https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/other/3739300-ifad-gef-back-445m-climate-resilience-projects-in-malawi-eritrea-kiribati): “Approximately 21,320 households are expected to benefit”; about 3,500 hectares of degraded mangroves; a total investment of about US$32 million. IFAD’s own release of 22 December 2025 did not open.
- **GPE System Transformation and Girls’ Education Accelerator grant, $5.91M; GPE System Transformation grant, $9.456M; GPE System Capacity grant, $2.2M (Active):** GPE, Education in Eritrea (https://globalpartnership.org/country/eritrea): “System transformation and Girls’ Education Accelerator”, 2025 to 2028, allocation US$5,910,000, utilization US$428,536; “System transformation”, 2024 to 2027, US$9,456,000, utilization US$2,531,202; “System capacity”, 2024 to 2027, US$2,200,000, utilization US$163,848; grant agent UNICEF. GPE, Decision on top-up triggers and system transformation grant to Eritrea (https://globalpartnership.org/content/decision-top-triggers-and-system-transformation-grant-eritrea-may-2024): on 10 June 2024 the Board approved a system transformation grant “in support of the Inclusive and Transformative Reform Agenda for Quality Learning for All Girls and Boys in Eritrea program” “in the amount of US$11,820,000”.
- **Water, Sanitation and Hygiene Adaptation Project, $9.82M (Approved):** see the record. Six regions (Debub, Maekel, Anseba, Gash Barka, Northern Red Sea and Southern Red Sea); 21 new complete water systems and 38 non-functioning systems rehabilitated; ten health centres and twelve schools; drinking water within about 500 metres. The African Development Bank lists it as P-ER-E00-011 (the mapafrica.afdb.org page did not open).
- **Eritrea Energy Integrated Project, $58M (Approved):** see the hero. $37.31 million from the African Development Fund and $20.73 million from the Transition Support Facility; a 34-megawatt solar-powered mini-grid system; 542 kilometres of distribution lines; around 306,000 people.
- **Desert to Power Eritrea 12 MW Mini-Grid Project, $19.5M (Signed):** see the record. More than 235,000 people, 20% of them women and youth; over 160 schools and 90 health centres in Gash Barka; 25 local companies trained and equipped. The February 2026 release does not say whether the 34 MW project builds on this grant.
- **Sustainable Smallholder Irrigated Agriculture in Arid and Semi-Arid Lands, Italy (Signed):** UNDP Eritrea, Newsletter, August to December 2025 (https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/undp-eritrea_newsletter-issue-august-december-2025.pdf): signed on 8 October 2025 by UNDP Eritrea, the Embassy of Italy and the Italian Agency for Development Cooperation (AICS), for Maekel, Anseba and Gash Barka. No amount is published.
