## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Saudi Arabia. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=SA
- **Water, 99%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=SA
- **Shelter, 83.9%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.6% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=SA
- **Energy, 100%:** 100% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=SA
- **Education, 67.6:** 67.6% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=SA
- **Health, 83:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~SAU
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for Saudi Arabia is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=SA

Furthest from reach: Education (32.4%). Of children in Saudi Arabia cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school.
