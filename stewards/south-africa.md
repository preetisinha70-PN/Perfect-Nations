## Sources of the figures that carry no link of their own

### The hero
- **$13.7B, Just Energy Transition Partnership pledges, 2025:** UK Government, 12-month Just Energy Transition Partnership leaders’ update, 9 December 2025 (https://www.gov.uk/government/news/12-month-just-energy-transition-partnership-leaders-update-2025, opened 10 November 2026): International Partners Group pledges of US$10bn, US$12.4bn with other bilateral pledges and US$13.7bn with multilateral development bank pledges; the United States withdrew on 28 February 2025. The hero had shown the initial mobilisation of US$8.5 billion (joint statement of 7 November 2022), which the register and the record still mention.
- **74, health service coverage index, 2023:** WHO service coverage index, the Health standard (WHO, 2023, via Our World in Data); not re-read.
- **8%, of electricity from renewables, 2023; the lead, more than nine-tenths of electricity from non-renewable sources:** IRENA, South Africa statistical profile, last updated 22 September 2025 (https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/South-Africa_Africa_RE_SP.pdf, opened 10 November 2026 through IRENA’s mirror): 16,700 GWh of 200,815 GWh generated in 2023 were renewable (8.3%, arithmetic; the profile rounds to 8%); renewables were 4% of total energy supply in 2022. The hero and The Sun and Wind card had shown 6.4%, renewable share of electricity output, 2021 (World Bank, World Development Indicators).
- **The lead, a first-of-its-kind wildlife conservation bond:** World Bank release of 23 March 2022 (“first-of-its-kind, outcome-based, financial instrument”, issued by IBRD) and World Bank feature of the same date (“South Africa pioneers innovative wildlife conservation bond”). The lead had said South Africa “issued” the bond; the issuer is IBRD.
- **The lead, a nation of 64.7 million:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, population 2025 (64,747,319, the `population` of the nation file); not re-read.
