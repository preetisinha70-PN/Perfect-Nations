## The record in figures

- **$300M:** External commercial debt bought back with a Standard Chartered loan backed by a $200 million IDB guarantee, 22 November 2024. IDB, https://www.iadb.org/en/news/bahamas-launches-debt-ocean-conservation-swap-support-idb (opened 17 November 2026): “a new US$300 million loan arranged by Standard Chartered Bank”; “US$200 million credit guarantee from the IDB”; “US$70 million co-guarantee from Builders Vision and US$30 million credit insurance from AXA XL”.
- **$124M:** Estimated savings for marine conservation over the next 15 years. IDB, same page: “will generate an estimated US$124 million in savings for marine conservation”; “An endowment, expected to grow to US$20 million by 2039”. The Nature Conservancy, 21 November 2024, https://nature.org/en-us/newsroom/tnc-announces-new-nature-bonds-project-bahamas/ (opened 17 November 2026): “an expected USD 124 million of new cash funding”, managed by the Bahamas Protected Areas Fund.
- **71.7%:** General government gross debt, share of GDP, 2025. IMF, WEO October 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/GGXWDG_NGDP@WEO/BHS (accessed 10 November 2026).
- **2.5%:** Real GDP growth projected for 2026. IMF, WEO October 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/NGDP_RPCH@WEO/BHS (accessed 10 November 2026).

Other published figures for public debt and growth, opened 17 November 2026:

- IMF, Press Release No. 26/031, “IMF Executive Board Concludes 2025 Article IV Consultation with The Bahamas”, 5 February 2026, https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/02/04/pr-26031-the-bahamas-imf-executive-board-concludes-2025-article-iv-consultation: “Growth is expected to be around 2.8 percent in 2025”; “Central government debt remains elevated, at around 74 percent of GDP”; the fiscal deficit narrowed to 0.5 percent of GDP in FY2024/25.
