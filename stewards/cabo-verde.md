## The seven standards

- **Food, 67.9%:** Not food insecure; 32.1% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=CV
- **Water, 92.3%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=CV
- **Shelter, 53.6%:** City dwellers outside slums; 46.4% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=CV
- **Energy, 89%:** 89% cook with clean fuels and technology; 98.4% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=CV
- **Education, 83:** 83% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=CV
- **Health, 71:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~CPV
- **Livelihoods, 35%:** Above US$8.30 a day; 65% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2015, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=CV

Furthest from reach: Livelihood (65%). Of Cabo Verdeans live below US$8.30 a day, on 2015 data.

## The enterprises

- **Cabeólica (Energy):** four wind farms, about 20% of the country’s electricity; 13.5 MW of wind and 26 MWh of storage added, inaugurated December 2025. EIB, release of 1 December 2025 (above).
- **Frescomar (Food):** fish cannery in Mindelo; 1,650 workers and 80% of national exports for the group, according to its commercial director. DNoticias (Lusa), https://www.dnoticias.pt/mundo/maior-exportador-de-cabo-verde-preve-aumentar-producao-em-20-em-2020-CX5527051 (circa 2019; dated).
- **Águas de Ponta Preta (Water):** pilot with Hérium on the remineralisation of desalinated water on Sal. Inforpress, June 2026, https://inforpress.cv/en/article-10287
- **Health 360 (Health):** integrated health management system for municipalities, clinics and hospitals, Praia. StartupBlink, January 2025, https://startupblink.com/top-startups/praia-cv (a directory listing; no user figures).
- **Morabi (Livelihood):** women’s association giving microcredit; created the Morabi savings and credit cooperative in December 2018, with a state guarantee covering 80% of a loan it took. Notícias ao Minuto (Lusa), https://www.noticiasaominuto.com/mundo/1911011/garantidos-projetos-de-associao-de-autopromoo-da-mulher-cabo-verdiana
