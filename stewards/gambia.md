## The seven standards

Not re-read at source on 16 November 2026.

- **Food, 52.8%:** Not food insecure; 47.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GM
- **Water, 48.2%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=GM
- **Shelter, 28.1%:** Safely managed sanitation; 37.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=GM
- **Energy, 3%:** 3% cook with clean fuels and technology; 69% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GM
- **Education, 61.3:** 61.3% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2025, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=GM
- **Health, 53:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GMB
- **Livelihoods, 46.6%:** Above the national poverty line; 53.4% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2020, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=GM

Furthest from reach: Energy (97%). Of Gambians still cook without clean fuels.

- **82.1%:** Public debt at the end of 2025, US$2,115.8 million (external 51.2%, domestic 30.9% of GDP); fiscal deficit of 5% of GDP in 2025. IMF Country Report No. 26/175, July 2026, https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1gmbea2026001.pdf (opened 16 November 2026)
- **$22.5M:** SDR 16.58 million released on 6 July 2026 (fifth ECF review, second RSF review). Same source.
- **$100M:** IMF Extended Credit Facility approved 12 January 2024. IMF, 2024, https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2024/01/12/pr2402-the-gambia-imf-executive-board-approves-usd100m-new-ecf-concludes-2023-article-iv

World Bank release on The Gambia Economic Update and Public Finance Review, 9 June 2026, https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/09/charting-a-path-to-stronger-growth-fiscal-resilience-and-women-s-economic-participation-in-the-gambia (opened 16 November 2026)

Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs, “National Assembly Approves 2026 Budget”, https://mofea.gov.gm/?p=6713 ; Gambiana gives D37.85 billion for 2025. ## The enterprises

- **Salam Financial Services (Livelihood):** the second largest microfinance institution in The Gambia, 15 branches. IFC disclosure, https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/48929/mfp-bop-supersonicz

- **GamFruits (Food, new):** Mastercard Foundation: a climate-smart community fruit farm in Gunjur founded by two Mastercard Foundation Scholars; Resolution Social Venture Challenge, 2018. https://mastercardfdn.org/?p=7698 . Older source; current activity not confirmed.
- **Suntech (Energy, new):** VC4A venture profile: pay-as-you-go solar, rent-to-own, a small deposit and weekly payments over about 18 months by scratch card or mobile money. https://vc4a.com/ventures/suntech/ . Undated profile.
- **Kids in Technology (Education, new):** The Point: 100 children graduated from a six-week programme in digital innovation, coding and robotics; founded by Kawsu Jabbi. https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/young-gambian-innovators-shine-at-kids-in-technology-graduation
- **InnovaRx Global Health (Health, new):** The Point, 6 October 2025: memorandum with Florence Healthcare (UK) for a joint clinic model, https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/innovarx-global-health-florence-healthcare-sign-partnership-to-transform-healthcare-in-gambia ; Design Observer profile of the founder, Dr Ismail Badjie (pharmacy, telemedicine, point-of-care diagnostics, last-mile delivery), https://designobserver.com/dr-ismail-d-badjie/
