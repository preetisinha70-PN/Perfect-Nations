## The seven standards

- **Food, 52.8%:** Not food insecure; 47.2% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GM
- **Water, 48.2%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=GM
- **Shelter, 28.1%:** Safely managed sanitation; 37.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=GM
- **Energy, 3%:** 3% cook with clean fuels and technology; 69% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GM
- **Education, 61.3:** 61.3% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2025, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=GM
- **Health, 53:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GMB
- **Livelihoods, 46.6%:** Above the national poverty line; 53.4% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2020, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=GM

Furthest from reach: Energy (97%). Of Gambians still cook without clean fuels.
