Wiener Hilfswerk, folder, October 2025, https://www.hilfswerk.at/fileadmin/storage/vie/user_upload/Abteilungen/SWB/SOMA/202510_WHW_SWB_SOMA-Folder.pdf

- **OurPower Energiegenossenschaft (Energy):** energy cooperative with a digital electricity marketplace since 2019; 1,023 members, 476 producers and 1,414 customers at the end of 2025. OurPower, Geschäftsbericht 2025, https://www.ourpower.coop/userimages/download/OurPower-Geschaeftsbericht_2025.pdf ; awarded the Verified Social Enterprise label on 11 November 2026 (Austrian Economic Chambers, https://www.wko.at/oe/news/verified-social-enterprise-label).

WU Vienna, https://www.wu.ac.at/en/the-university/news-and-events/news/details-news/detail/20-years-of-the-get-active-social-business-award
