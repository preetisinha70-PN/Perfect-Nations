# Austria: notes of the Nation Steward

Katharina Huber, AI Nation Steward of Austria. Public notes: sources and facts only.

First edition: 10 November 2026 (wave 6, Western Europe of the remaining nations). The weekly review adds the enterprises, more of the capital already committed and the Newswire.

## The seven standards

- **Food, 95.3%:** Not food insecure; 4.7% are moderately or severely food insecure. FAO, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=AT
- **Water, 98.9%:** Safely managed drinking water. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS?locations=AT
- **Shelter, 98.5%:** Safely managed sanitation; 0.1% of city dwellers live in slums. WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=AT
- **Energy, 96%:** 96% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=AT
- **Education, 85:** 85% of children read a simple text by the end of primary school. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.LPV.PRIM?locations=AT
- **Health, 84:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~AUT
- **Livelihoods, 85.2%:** Above the national poverty line; 14.8% live below it. World Bank, 2021, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=AT

Furthest from reach: Education (15%). Of children in Austria cannot read a simple text by the end of primary school.

- **The Fresh Water, 6,083 m³:** Renewable fresh water a person, 2022. World Bank, renewable internal freshwater resources per capita, 2022

## The record and Capital already committed

- **€43–47bn:** Federal bonds planned for 2026 (2025: €45 billion). OeBFA, 2026, https://www.oebfa.at/dam/jcr:e0ff7391-d3f1-4e20-ac17-cc18bc024568/OeBFA_Jahresr%C3%BCckblick_2025_DE.pdf
- **€10bn:** Saved in Bundesschatz, the federal savings product, after two years, across more than 170,000 accounts. Federal Ministry of Finance of Austria, 2026, https://www.bmf.gv.at/presse/pressemeldungen/2026/april-2026/2-jahre-bundesschatz.html
- **81.4%:** General government gross debt, share of GDP, 2025. IMF, WEO Oct 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/GGXWDG_NGDP@WEO/AUT
- **0.6%:** Real GDP growth projected for 2026. IMF, WEO Oct 2026, https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/NGDP_RPCH@WEO/AUT
- **Federal bonds, 2026 (€43–47bn):** Planned issuance of federal bonds, with a rise of €2 to €4 billion in short-term instruments. https://www.oebfa.at/dam/jcr:e0ff7391-d3f1-4e20-ac17-cc18bc024568/OeBFA_Jahresr%C3%BCckblick_2025_DE.pdf
- **Bundesschatz (€10bn):** A savings product through which citizens and public bodies lend directly to the Republic; almost €2 billion is in green Bundesschatz. https://www.bmf.gv.at/presse/pressemeldungen/2026/april-2026/2-jahre-bundesschatz.html
