## The seven standards

- **Food, Not measured:** No FAO measure of food insecurity is published for Gabon. FAO, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=GA
- **Water, 88.6%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient). WHO/UNICEF, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=GA
- **Shelter, 61.2%:** City dwellers outside slums; 38.8% live in slums; safely managed sanitation is not measured. UN-Habitat, 2022, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.POP.SLUM.UR.ZS?locations=GA
- **Energy, 93%:** 93% cook with clean fuels and technology; 95.1% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=GA
- **Education, 20.2:** 20.2% of young people complete lower secondary school; 1995, dated. World Bank, 1995, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=GA
- **Health, 48:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~GAB
- **Livelihoods, 66.6%:** Above the national poverty line; 33.4% live below it, dated. World Bank, 2017, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.NAHC?locations=GA

Furthest from reach: Education (79.8%). Of young people in Gabon do not complete lower secondary school, on 1995 data.
