## The seven standards

- **Food, 78.9%:** Not food insecure; 21.1% are moderately or severely food insecure, dated. FAO, 2020, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SN.ITK.MSFI.ZS?locations=KN
- **Water, 98.6%:** Basic drinking water; safely managed water is not measured (lenient), dated. WHO/UNICEF, 2017, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.H2O.BASW.ZS?locations=KN
- **Shelter, Not measured:** Neither safely managed sanitation nor urban slums is measured for Saint Kitts and Nevis. WHO/UNICEF, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.SMSS.ZS?locations=KN
- **Energy, 96%:** 96% cook with clean fuels and technology; 100% have electricity. World Bank, 2024, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.CFT.ACCS.ZS?locations=KN
- **Education, 100:** 100% of young people complete lower secondary school. World Bank, 2023, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS?locations=KN
- **Health, 80:** WHO service coverage index. WHO, 2023, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/universal-health-coverage-index?country=~KNA
- **Livelihoods, Not measured:** No poverty survey for Saint Kitts and Nevis is published by the World Bank. World Bank, https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.UMIC?locations=KN
