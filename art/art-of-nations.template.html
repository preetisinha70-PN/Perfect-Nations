The idea

The other half of the ledger

Perfect Nations designs how every citizen can have food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood. Art of Nations keeps the account a balance sheet leaves out: what a nation is for.

  1. 01

    Culture as capital

    A nation’s songs, crafts and gardens are wealth no balance sheet records. Each portrait enters that wealth in the ledger.

  2. 02

    Identity as asset

    A people that sees itself clearly invests in itself. Each bloom grows from the nation’s own landscape and its own colours.

  3. 03

    Beauty as public value

    Beauty belongs to every citizen, as clean water does. Every work bought in the gallery carries Perfect Nations forward.
