The structure

The Blue Food Swap, ready for market.

Innovative finance carries it: guaranteed debt replaces costly debt, and the savings grow food at home. AI makes it provable, from the first dollar to the last boat.

  1. TodayCostly debt

    Bonds or loans with high coupons, chosen with the Ministry of Finance.

  2. The conversionGuaranteed debt

    A development bank guarantee lowers the cost, as in 2024.

  3. The savingThe difference in cost

    Every year, for the life of the new debt.

  4. Bound by lawThe Blue Food Fund

    Ring-fenced for farms, fisheries and sea farming.

  5. The resultMore food at home

    Fewer Barbadians going without; more grown and caught on the island.

AI checks every stepIt tracks each dollar from the fund to the farm or the boat, forecasts each harvest and catch, and reports the share of Barbadians who are food insecure every quarter.

The indicative term sheet

The terms the mandate takes to market.

Illustrative: no terms have been agreed with any party. Sizes and prices are set in the mandate, never assumed.

Borrower
The Government of Barbados
Adviser
Perfect Nations, sovereign adviser to the Government
Instrument
A debt conversion: guaranteed debt replaces costly debt, following the 2024 debt-for-climate-resilience conversion, which was structured as a sustainability-linked loan (IDB).
Credit enhancement
A development bank guarantee. To be sought in phase two.
Size, tenor, price
Set from the debt chosen and the food gap of up to $192 million a year. No figure is assumed here.
Use of savings
The Blue Food Fund, established in law, for local farms, fisheries and sea farming only.
Linked terms
Terms can be linked to the food indicator, as the 2024 conversion was linked to its own targets. To be agreed with the guarantor and lenders.
Resilience
Climate resilient debt clauses, as in Barbados’s 2035 global bond, under which payments may be deferred after a defined disaster (Parliament of Barbados).
Indicators
The share of Barbadians moderately or severely food insecure, 31% in 2022 (FAO), and food grown and caught at home. Targets set with Government.
Verification
AI monitoring with an independent verifier; every disbursement and every quarterly result published.

Where AI works

Five jobs AI does for the mandate.

Innovative finance funds the swap. AI makes it possible to value, monitor and prove it at the scale of a nation, at a cost a small island can carry.

  • Value

    The ledger

    AI reads satellite images, fisheries landings and the national registers, so independent valuers start from evidence, not estimates.

  • Monitor

    The food indicator

    Short phone surveys, market prices and estimates of harvest and catch give the food indicator every quarter, not once a decade.

  • Verify

    Every disbursement

    AI follows each dollar from the Blue Food Fund to the farm, the boat or the sea farm it finances.

  • Match

    The capital

    AI screens guarantors, lenders and investors whose mandates fit the deal, so the approach goes in the right order.

  • Report

    The public record

    A public report every quarter: the savings, the fund, the food indicator, each with its source.

Governance first

Built to win the Perfect Nations Cup.

The Cup is awarded only to financing instruments that pass two tests. The mandate is designed to pass both.

  • Test one

    Led by good governance

    With no room for corruption: the fund’s accounts published in full, and nothing disbursed without a verified result.

  • Test two

    Tracked by AI to every beneficiary

    With the impact measured and published: every farm, boat and sea farm the savings reach, every quarter.

  • Disclosure

    One rule on fees
