Day 2 · 02 Perfect Capita

Each standard costed in Barbadian prices.

Perfect Capita today: $6,204 a person a year, BBD 12,407. The Lab turns a Perfect Nations estimate into a figure Barbados owns, by settling six open questions with the ministries.

StandardA person a yearThe question the Lab settles

Day 3 · 03 Capital architecture and 04 Launch

One structure, drawn with your team: the Blue Food Swap.

Innovative finance carries it: guaranteed debt replaces costly debt, and the savings grow food at home. AI makes it provable, from the first dollar to the last boat.

  1. TodayCostly debt

    Bonds or loans with high coupons, chosen with the Ministry of Finance.

  2. The conversionGuaranteed debt

    A development bank guarantee lowers the cost, as in 2024.

  3. The savingThe difference in cost

    Every year, for the life of the new debt.

  4. Bound by lawThe Blue Food Fund

    Ring-fenced for farms, fisheries and sea farming.

  5. The resultMore food at home

    Fewer Barbadians going without; more grown and caught on the island.

AI checks every stepIt tracks each dollar from the fund to the farm or the boat, forecasts each harvest and catch, and reports the share of Barbadians who are food insecure every quarter.

The draft term sheet

What the Lab writes down.

Illustrative: no terms have been agreed with any party, and no size is assumed before the Lab.

Borrower
The Government of Barbados
Instrument
A debt conversion: guaranteed debt replaces costly debt, following the 2024 debt-for-climate-resilience conversion, which was structured as a sustainability-linked loan.
Credit enhancement
A development bank guarantee. To be sought.
Size and tenor
Set in the Lab from the debt chosen and the food gap of up to $192 million a year. No figure is assumed here.
Use of savings
The Blue Food Fund, for local farms, fisheries and sea farming only.
Indicators
The share of Barbadians moderately or severely food insecure, 31% in 2022 (FAO), and food grown and caught at home. Targets set with Government.
Verification
AI monitoring with an independent verifier, published every quarter.
Governance
The fund’s accounts published in full; nothing is disbursed without a verified result.
