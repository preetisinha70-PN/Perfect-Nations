    ReDesign Studio · Perfect Capita

    The Ghana wheel: held at 18.8 by shelter

    • To setPerfect CapitaThe yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards, set with Ghana in its own prices.
    • To setThe price of Perfect GhanaPerfect Capita times the population: the national bill, each year.
    • 18.8CoverageOut of 100, set by shelter, the standard Ghana provides least.
    • ~28mThe GapGhanaians without a safely managed toilet at home: 81 in every hundred.

    The Living LineThe poverty line measures income. The Living Line measures a life: food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood. Perfect Capita is the yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards. Coverage scores each standard strictly out of 100, and a nation is held at its weakest: Ghana at 18.8, by shelter, with energy at 35 and water at 42.9 next.

    Perfect Nations estimate, to be confirmed with Ghana. Figures marked dated rest on surveys more than five years old; lenient measures are marked as such. The strict measures

    • Water42.9%Safely managed drinking waterWHO/UNICEF, 2024
    • Shelter18.8%Safely managed sanitationWHO/UNICEF, 2024
    • Energy3535% cook with clean fuels and technologyWorld Bank, 2024

    Strict measures. Food: share not moderately or severely food insecure (FAO). Water: safely managed drinking water where measured; where it is not, basic water, marked as such. Shelter: the lower of safely managed sanitation and the share outside urban slums. Energy: the lower of electricity per person against 1,000 kWh and clean cooking. Education: the lower of children reading by the end of primary school and lower-secondary completion. Health: WHO service coverage index. Livelihood: share above the stricter of the national poverty line and the World Bank line for the nation’s income group. Each figure links to its source.
