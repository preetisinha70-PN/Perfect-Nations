Why Perfect Nations

Every nation already owns what it needs to guarantee a basic standard of living for its people. My work is to help it see those assets, price that standard, and build the capital to close the distance.

Preeti Sinha · President and CEO

  • Count what a nation ownsLand, water, coast, forest, energy, institutions and people: the wealth no balance sheet records.
  • Measure what mattersPerfect Capita, the new GDP: the yearly cost, per person, of lifting everyone above the Living Line in all seven basic living standards.

The logo

A race every nation can win

The Perfect Nations logo turns the contest between nations into the one race worth winning: the race to guarantee seven basic living standards to every citizen.

The cup
A championship cup, its handles raised like arms in victory. Development is not charity. It is a race, and every nation can enter.
The laurels
Honour earned in public, never inherited. The branches meet at the foot of the cup, because no nation rises alone.
The seven stars
One for each basic living standard: food, water, shelter, energy, education, health and livelihood. Together they crown the prize.
The gold
The wealth every nation already owns but has never counted: water, land, coast, forest, energy, institutions and people.

In this race, every winner makes the world richer, and no nation has to lose.
